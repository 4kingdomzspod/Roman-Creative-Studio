# Export Standard

## Primary delivery
- Resolution: 1080 × 1920 (9:16 vertical)
- Frame rate: 30fps (match source; do not conform 24/25fps footage up to 30)
- Codec: H.264
- Container: MP4
- Audio: AAC, dialogue intelligible, music/SFX not clipping

## Platform-specific limits (bitrate caps, duration limits, file-size caps)
**VERIFY CURRENT PLATFORM SETTINGS** — TikTok/Instagram encoding recommendations change over time; check each platform's current upload spec before export rather than trusting a fixed number here.

## Pre-export checklist
- [ ] Sequence resolution/fps matches this standard (SYSTEM.md §5)
- [ ] Progress tracker numbers current (STATE.md)
- [ ] Captions burned in or platform-native, spell-checked
- [ ] Audio peaks not clipping; music under dialogue
- [ ] No black frames at head/tail
- [ ] File named per ASSET-SYSTEM.md (`RCS90_EPNN_FINAL`)

Full pre-publish QC (story/pacing/branding, not just export mechanics): SYSTEM.md §10.
