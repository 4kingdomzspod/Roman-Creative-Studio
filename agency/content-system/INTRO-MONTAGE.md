# Intro Montage — RCS Signature Open
**Status: APPROVED — v1.0.** Locked after creative-blueprint review (see STATE.md changelog for the validation/approval record). Structure, timing, and shot order below are the standard — do not redesign the concept without a new version bump (SYSTEM.md §11).

**Length:** 7.5s (Standard). **Role:** opens every episode — covers SYSTEM.md §2's HOOK + visual proof + SIGNATURE MONTAGE beats as one continuous piece — then ends in a hard cut into that episode's own title card. Builds series recognition — a viewer should know it's RCS within the first second.

**Emotional arc:** Started Somewhere → Started Working → Started Building → Now You're Watching the Journey.
**Feel:** cinematic, authentic, modern, energetic, documentary-style, entrepreneurial, personal, premium. **Avoid:** corporate polish, cheesy transitions, generic YouTube-intro tropes, TikTok template look, over-editing. The footage is the star — cuts and titles support it, never compete with it.

**Momentum Bridge (0:01.75–0:02.25 nominal):** this is an audio-structure marker (riser entrance), not a separate shot or an empty gap. Shot 05 (Planning) physically holds on screen through this span rather than cutting away — frame-accurate on-screen duration is 26 frames/0.87s (frames 42–67): the first ~10-11 frames play as originally cut, then the same shot continues uncut while the riser builds underneath, until Shot 06 hard-cuts in at frame 68. No new shot placeholder was added to fill this — it uses Shot 05's own footage, held longer.
- If Shot 05's real footage is a single continuous clip (e.g., a pan across notes/whiteboard), trim it to the full 26 frames — its own motion covers the hold.
- If Shot 05's footage was chosen as a short static insert, either pick a candidate with more inherent movement, or apply a slow 103–105% push-in over the 26 frames (same keyframe technique as elsewhere in this doc) so it doesn't sit dead for 0.87s. No speed ramp — Shot 05 stays 100%.

## Music & Beat Structure
Target tempo: 120 BPM (usable range 100–140). Beat length at 120 BPM: 0.5s/beat. Not a specific licensed track — any cinematic/modern instrumental in this BPM range with a riser-to-drop shape fits.
- Beat 1 (0:00) — Hook lands on the downbeat
- Beats 2–5 (0:00.45–0:01.75) — Build section, cuts roughly on-beat but slightly ahead (0.30–0.35s) — urgent, not metronomic
- 0:01.75 — Riser enters under the Momentum Bridge (Shot 05 hold)
- ~0:03.15 — Riser peaks into Shot 09 (mood shot)
- 0:03.75 — Bass drop / impact exactly on Shot 10 (Identity) entrance
- 0:05.25 — Secondary hit on Title entrance
- ~0:06.10 — Small accent when "ROMAN CREATIVE STUDIO" line appears
- 0:07.00 — Music ducks or cuts hard into episode audio

## Typography
**Font: Plus Jakarta Sans** (SYSTEM.md §3's already-approved title font — free, OFL, already loaded sitewide). Not Neue Montreal, Helvetica Now, or any paid alternative. When sourcing local font files for the editor (not the website's Google Fonts subset), get the full family so the Medium (500) weight below is available.

Line 1 (main): `90 DAYS.` / `BUILDING FROM ZERO.` — Bold/Semibold, all caps.
Line 2 (secondary): `ROMAN CREATIVE STUDIO` — Medium, all caps, tracking +20–40 ("stamped" feel).
Size: Line 1 ~9–11% of frame height/line; Line 2 ~40% the size of Line 1.
Placement: Centered, lower-third or true center depending on footage brightness in Shot 10/11.
Entrance: Line 1 fades + scales 96%→100% over 4–5 frames starting at frame 158, timed to the title-entrance impact (completes ~frame 162–163). Line 2 is staggered, not simultaneous — its entrance is timed so it **completes at the ~0:06.10 beat accent** (frame ~183, §Music & Beat Structure), not just "~0.5s after Line 1 starts": start Line 2's fade+scale at frame ~178–179 (same 4–5 frame duration as Line 1), completing at ~183.
Exit: Quick fade (3–4 frames), no slide-out.

## Motion Design
- Opacity fades for all title entrances/exits.
- Scale micro-push (96%→100%) on Line 1 entrance only.
- Titles stay locked in place once visible — no moving text.
- **Optional, non-default:** subtle blur-to-sharp on the Shot 10 (Identity) transition — 2–3 frame gaussian blur easing to 0. Skip it if it doesn't read cleanly on the real footage; it's a nice-to-have, not required for approval.
- Avoid: 3D transforms, particle effects, glitch/RGB-split, Fusion-only nodes.

## Color
**Default: manual baseline grade, no LUT.** Same correction-first order as SYSTEM.md §4: **exposure → white balance → contrast → highlights/shadows → saturation** (→ skin-tone consistency check). Approximate targets for this montage: contrast +8 to +12, saturation -5 to -10, shadows lifted slightly and cooled 2–3° toward blue, highlights warm and protected from clipping. Consistent white balance across all 10 shots matters more than any single grade choice — they come from different original sources/lighting.

**LUT: optional experimental workflow only, never the default.** No orange/teal or any other creative LUT is part of the approved standard. A LUT may only become standard after it's tested against real footage and shown to improve consistency (SYSTEM.md §4 step 3) — that decision, if made, is logged in STATE.md, not assumed here.

## Editing Language (applies to both editors)
Primary: hard cut, match cut, movement-based cut. Secondary (sparingly, only when it improves the cut): whip, speed ramp, punch-in.
**Sanctioned exceptions (this montage only, single-use each):**
- Cross-dissolve into Shot 10 (Identity) only — nowhere else. Pinned to **3 frames (0.1s)**, the top of the originally-approved 2–3 frame range, chosen because 2 frames can read as imperceptible at 30fps. Both editors should use this same value for consistency.
- Optional 2–3 frame blur-to-sharp on the same Shot 10 transition (see §Motion Design) — not required.
Avoid everywhere else: zoom-transition presets, spins, glitches, light leaks, unearned wipes.

## Progress Tracker
**Not part of this montage.** `DAY XX/90` / `$X/$10,000` belongs to the episode template (SYSTEM.md §6 slot 4, the episode's own title card) — it does not appear anywhere in the reusable intro, so the intro never needs re-editing when the numbers change.

## Variations (fold-in, same template — not a competing system)
