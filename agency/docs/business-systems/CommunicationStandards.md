## Purpose

Establish clear, consistent communication standards for all interactions at Roman Creative Studio — with clients, prospects, contractors, and vendors. Communication quality is a differentiator. How RCS communicates IS part of the premium experience.

## Business Value

Clear communication standards prevent misaligned expectations, reduce revision cycles, build client trust, protect the business in disputes, and create a replicable experience as the team grows.

---

## Communication Philosophy

> "Communicate like a premium partner, not a freelancer."

1. **Proactive over reactive.** Clients should never have to ask "what's the status?" We tell them before they ask.
2. **Clarity over brevity.** Write clearly, even if it takes one more sentence.
3. **Professional always.** Every email, every message — even on a bad day.
4. **Document decisions.** If it was decided verbally, confirm it in writing. If it isn't written, it didn't happen.
5. **Respect their time.** Keep meetings focused. Keep emails actionable. No unnecessary communications.

## Channel Standards

### Email

**Primary channel for:** All client project communication, proposals, approvals, invoices, formal decisions.

**Standards:**
- Subject line: Always include the project name or client name. Example: `Bright Smile Dental — Design V2 Ready for Review`
- Response time: Within 1 business day (Monday–Friday, 9am–6pm EST). Aim for same-day during active projects.
- Signature: Professional email signature on every outbound email:
  ```
  Alexander
  Roman Creative Studio
  Alexander@romancreativestudio.co
  romancreativestudio.co
  ```
- Thread hygiene: Keep all project discussion in a single ongoing thread, not scattered emails
- Approvals: Always request written confirmation in email. "Please reply to confirm approval."
- Decisions: Summarize any verbal decisions in a follow-up email: "As discussed on our call today..."

**Tone:** Professional, warm, direct. Not formal/corporate. Not casual/sloppy.

**What Not To Do:**
- Don't send multiple short emails — consolidate
- Don't use all caps
- Don't send invoices without a personal note
- Don't rely on email for urgent matters (use phone)

---

### Phone / Video Call

**Primary channel for:** Discovery calls, kickoff meetings, design reviews, problem-solving conversations.

**Standards:**
- All calls scheduled in advance via Calendly (no surprise calls without prior email)
- Video calls preferred over phone-only (builds trust, enables screen sharing)
- Calls start on time. If running late, send a message within 5 minutes.
- All calls end with: stated next steps, owner, and due date
- Follow every call with a meeting notes email within 24 hours
- Video call platform: Google Meet or Zoom (client preference)

**No-Call Policy:**
- Do not accept calls about project approvals — require written email confirmation
- Do not make commitments on a call that haven't been reviewed — "Let me confirm that by email"

---

### Slack (Future — once team > 1)

**Primary channel for:** Internal team communication, quick questions, file sharing between team members.

**Standards (future):**
- Slack is internal only. Never use Slack for client communication.
- Response time: Within 2 hours during business hours.
- Use threads for replies (don't clutter channels with direct replies)
- No Slack after 7pm unless emergency
- Project channels: `#project-[clientslug]` for each active project
- Do not use Slack DMs for decisions that should be in a project record

## Emergency Communication

**Definition of Emergency:** Site is down, data breach suspected, critical security vulnerability, content emergency (defamatory content, legal notice).

**Emergency Protocol:**
1. Client emails Alexander@romancreativestudio.co with subject line starting `[URGENT]`
2. Founder acknowledges within 1 hour (business hours) or 2 hours (after hours)
3. Diagnosis begins immediately
4. Client updated every 30 minutes until resolved
5. Post-incident summary sent within 24 hours of resolution

**After-Hours Emergencies:**
RCS does not guarantee after-hours response for non-critical issues. Care Plan clients can expect a response for site-down emergencies within 2 hours any day of the week.

## Difficult Conversations

### Handling Scope Creep Requests
```
Script:
"That's a great idea — it's outside the scope we agreed on, but 
I can put together a quick quote for adding it. Want me to do that 
so you can decide?"

Never say: "That's not in the scope." (Defensive)
Always say: "That's a great idea — here's how we can add it." (Premium partnership)
```

### Handling Difficult Feedback
```
Script:
"Thanks for the feedback — I want to make sure I fully understand 
before we revise. Can you help me understand what specific aspect 
isn't working, and what direction you'd like to go instead?"
```

### Handling Missed Deadlines (by client)
```
Email Template:
"Hi [Name],

I wanted to flag that the [content / approval / assets] we needed 
by [date] hasn't arrived yet. I want to make sure we keep your 
project on track.

If we receive it by [new date], we can still hit your [milestone/launch date].
If we receive it after that, the new target date would be [adjusted date].

Is there anything I can help unblock?

Alexander"
```

### Handling Requests to Rush
```
Script:
"I completely understand the urgency. I want to be honest with you — 
rushing the design process is the most common reason for extra 
revisions later. Here's what I can do to accelerate without 
compromising quality: [specific option]. Does that work?"
```

## Communication Anti-Patterns

Things RCS never does:

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Hurts |
|--------------|-------------|
| Ghost a client (no response > 2 days) | Destroys trust; creates anxiety |
| Send unpolished work "just to show progress" | Sets wrong quality expectations |
| Promise timelines without checking capacity | Creates commitments we can't keep |
| Commit on calls without written follow-up | Leads to disputes |
| Use jargon clients don't understand | Makes clients feel talked down to |
| Apologize excessively without a solution | Signals lack of competence |
| Over-communicate trivially | Trains clients to ignore our emails |
