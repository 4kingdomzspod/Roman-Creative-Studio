## Purpose

Establish clear, consistent communication standards for all interactions at Roman Creative Studio — with clients, prospects, contractors, and vendors. Communication quality IS part of the premium experience.

## Communication Philosophy

1. **Proactive over reactive.** Clients should never have to ask "what's the status?"
2. **Clarity over brevity.** Write clearly, even if it takes one more sentence.
3. **Professional always.** Every email, every message — even on a bad day.
4. **Document decisions.** If it was decided verbally, confirm in writing. If it isn't written, it didn't happen.
5. **Respect their time.** Keep meetings focused. Keep emails actionable.

## Channel Standards

### Email (Primary Channel)
- **Subject line:** Always include project/client name. Example: `Bright Smile Dental — Design V2 Ready for Review`
- **Response time:** Within 1 business day (Mon–Fri, 9am–6pm EST). Same-day during active projects.
- **Signature:** Alexander / Roman Creative Studio / Alexander@romancreativestudio.co / romancreativestudio.co
- **Approvals:** Always request written confirmation: "Please reply to confirm approval."
- **Decisions:** Summarize verbal decisions in follow-up email: "As discussed on our call today..."

### Phone / Video Call
- All calls scheduled in advance via Calendly
- Video preferred (builds trust, enables screen sharing)
- Calls start on time; send message within 5 min if running late
- All calls end with: stated next steps, owner, due date
- Follow every call with meeting notes email within 24 hours

### Slack (Future — once team > 1)
- Internal only. Never use Slack for client communication.
- Response time: within 2 hours during business hours
- Use threads for replies. No Slack after 7pm unless emergency.

## Difficult Conversation Scripts

### Scope Creep
> "That's a great idea — it's outside the scope we agreed on, but I can put together a quick quote for adding it. Want me to do that so you can decide?"

### Difficult Feedback
> "Thanks for the feedback — can you help me understand what specific aspect isn't working, and what direction you'd like to go instead?"

### Missed Deadlines (by client)
> "I wanted to flag that the [content/approval/assets] we needed by [date] hasn't arrived yet. If we receive it by [new date], we can still hit your [milestone/launch date]. If after that, the new target date would be [adjusted date]."

### Requests to Rush
> "I completely understand the urgency. Here's what I can do to accelerate without compromising quality: [specific option]."

## Communication Anti-Patterns

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Hurts |
|--------------|-------------|
| Ghost a client (no response > 2 days) | Destroys trust |
| Send unpolished work "just to show progress" | Sets wrong quality expectations |
| Promise timelines without checking capacity | Creates commitments we can't keep |
| Commit on calls without written follow-up | Leads to disputes |
| Over-communicate trivially | Trains clients to ignore our emails |
