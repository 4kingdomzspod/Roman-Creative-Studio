## Purpose

Standardize the RCS discovery call process to maximize conversion, qualify leads efficiently, and capture the strategic intelligence needed to write a winning proposal.

## Discovery Call Philosophy

> "We don't sell websites. We diagnose digital problems and prescribe growth solutions."

- **Listen 70%, speak 30%**
- Understand the business, not just the website
- Uncover the real problem (not just the stated problem)
- Establish authority by asking questions nobody else asks

---

## Pre-Call Workflow

1. Lead qualifies (CRM: Qualified Lead) → Calendly link sent
2. Calendly auto-sends: confirmation email, calendar invite, pre-call questionnaire (48 hrs before)
3. Founder reviews pre-call questionnaire before the meeting

## Pre-Call Questionnaire (8 Questions)

1. Business name and website URL
2. In one sentence, what does your business do?
3. Who is your ideal customer? (Be specific)
4. What is the #1 problem you need your website to solve?
5. What does success look like 6 months after launch?
6. What have you already tried, and why didn't it work?
7. Budget range: [ ] Under $3,000 / [ ] $3,000–$6,000 / [ ] $6,000–$12,000 / [ ] $12,000+ / [ ] Not sure
8. Is there a deadline or launch event we should know about?

## Call Structure (30 Minutes)

### Opening (2 min)
"Today I want to understand your business deeply so I can tell you honestly whether we're the right fit — and if we are, what I'd recommend."

### Business Discovery (10 min)
1. Tell me about your business — what do you do, who do you serve, what makes you different?
2. How long have you been operating? How many clients/customers per month?
3. How do most of your new clients find you today?
4. What percentage of revenue comes through your website vs. referrals?
5. If your website were performing at its best, what would change in your business?

### Problem Diagnosis (8 min)
6. Walk me through your current website — what do you like and what frustrates you?
7. When someone visits your website, what do you want them to do first?
8. Do you know your current conversion rate?
9. Have you done any SEO, ads, or other marketing? What results?
10. Who are your top 2-3 competitors? How does their online presence compare?

### Goals & Timeline (5 min)
11. What are the top 3 outcomes you need from this project?
12. Is there a hard deadline?
13. Who else is involved in the decision to move forward?
14. What would make you hesitant to move forward, even if you loved the proposal?

### Next Steps (5 min)
- High-level tier recommendation (BUILD $3,500 / GROW $6,500 / SCALE $12k+)
- "I'll have a tailored proposal to you within 48 hours."
- Confirm best email and communication method
- Confirm decision-making authority

## Post-Call Notes Template

```
DISCOVERY CALL NOTES
Date / Client / Company / URL / Industry / Duration

BUSINESS SNAPSHOT: what they do, revenue model, team size, current traffic/leads
CURRENT STATE: website status, biggest complaint, current marketing, what they've tried
DESIRED STATE: primary goal, success metric, timeline, hard deadline
COMPETITOR INTEL: top 2-3 competitors, how they compare
BUDGET & AUTHORITY: budget range, decision maker on call (Y/N), other stakeholders
TECHNICAL NOTES: hosting, domain registrar, CMS/platform, integrations needed
RECOMMENDED TIER: [ ] BUILD ($3,500) [ ] GROW ($6,500) [ ] SCALE ($12k+)
PROPOSAL NOTES: key proof points, specific outcomes to promise, objections to preempt
NEXT STEPS: proposal due, follow-up date
```

## No-Show Protocol
1. At 5 min grace: send Zoom link reminder
2. At 15 min: "Looks like something came up — happy to reschedule"
3. CRM: No-Show flag. 48-hour follow-up. After 2 no-shows → Cold Nurture.

## Disqualification Criteria
- Budget confirmed below $3,000 and refuses to discuss value
- Needs completed website in under 2 weeks
- Has worked with 3+ agencies and fired all of them for design reasons
- Cannot provide any content and expects RCS to fabricate everything
- Clearly price-shopping for cheapest option
- Red flags: demeaning tone, unrealistic guarantees, refusal to sign contract
