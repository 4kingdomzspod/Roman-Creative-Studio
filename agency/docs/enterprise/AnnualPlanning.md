## Purpose

Translate the 3-year strategic plan into a concrete, executable 12-month operating plan. Annual planning bridges strategy and execution — it answers "what exactly are we doing this year, with these resources, in this sequence?"

**Business Value:** Without an annual plan, the year is lived reactively. With one, the team knows what matters, resources are allocated deliberately, and results are measurable.

---

## Annual Planning Process

### Step 1: Review Prior Year (December, Week 1)

**Financial Scorecard**
- Revenue: Actual vs. plan (by stream)
- Profit margin: Actual vs. target
- MRR: Start vs. end of year
- Cash flow: Was there adequate cash throughout?
- Owner compensation: Achieved?

**Operational Scorecard**
- OKRs achieved vs. total
- Projects delivered on time: %
- Client NPS: Score and trend
- Team satisfaction: Survey results
- Products/features launched vs. planned

**What Worked:**
- Top 3 successes of the year
- What drove those successes?
- How do we repeat or build on them?

**What Didn't Work:**
- Top 3 failures or underperformances
- Root cause (not excuses)
- What would we do differently?

**What We Learned:**
- Market insights
- Client insights
- Team insights
- Technology insights

### Step 6: Annual OKR Setting (December, Week 3)

See OKRFramework.md for full process. Annual planning outputs these Company-Level OKRs:

**Format:**
```
Objective: [Ambitious, qualitative goal]
Key Results:
  KR1: [Measurable, binary outcome]
  KR2: [Measurable, binary outcome]
  KR3: [Measurable, binary outcome]
Owner: [Person accountable]
Timeline: [By end of year / specific quarter]
```

**Example Year 1 Company OKRs:**

Objective 1: Establish RCS as a viable, growing creative agency
- KR1: Generate $60,000 in agency revenue
- KR2: Achieve NPS of 8.0+ from project clients
- KR3: Complete 6 projects across BUILD and GROW tiers

Objective 2: Build the foundation for recurring revenue
- KR1: Reach $1,500/mo in Care Plan MRR
- KR2: Launch 3 digital products generating combined $500/mo
- KR3: Build email list to 500 subscribers

Objective 3: Systematize the business for scale
- KR1: Document 100% of core agency processes in Notion
- KR2: Automate 5 repetitive workflows via Zapier
- KR3: Achieve project on-time delivery rate of 85%+

## Annual Plan Document

The output of annual planning is a single 10–15 page document containing:
1. Prior Year Review (2 pages)
2. Annual Revenue Plan (1 page)
3. Annual Headcount Plan (1 page)
4. Annual Technology Plan (1 page)
5. Annual Marketing Plan (1 page)
6. Company OKRs (1 page)
7. Annual Risk Register (1 page)
8. Annual Calendar (1–2 pages)

**Distribution:** Shared with all leadership team members. Summary version shared with all team.

## Review Domains (Annual)

Each of these domains receives a dedicated section in annual planning:

### Mission Review
- Is our stated mission still accurate?
- Do our services, products, and actions align with our mission?
- Any language that needs updating?

### Vision Review
- Are we still on track to the 3-year vision?
- What new information changes our direction?
- Any course corrections needed?

### Innovation Review
- What experiments did we run? Results?
- What new technologies did we adopt or evaluate?
- What's on the innovation radar for next year?

### Community Impact Review
- How many clients' businesses meaningfully improved?
- What accessibility work did we do beyond client requirements?
- What mentorship, education, or community contribution did we make?
- What do we commit to in the coming year?
