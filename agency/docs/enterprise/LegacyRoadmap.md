## Purpose

This document answers the question that matters more than any quarterly OKR: *Why does Roman Creative Studio exist, and what will it have contributed to the world by the time it's done?*

This is not a business plan. It is a legacy plan. It defines what success looks like not in one year or three years, but across a generation — and it ensures every decision made today is aligned with the institution we are building.

## Business Value

- Provides the "north star" that guides all strategic decisions
- Ensures short-term growth never comes at the cost of long-term integrity
- Creates alignment between financial success and meaningful contribution
- Serves as the founder's letter to every future team member and partner
- Grounds the company in purpose when markets, trends, and conditions change

## Section 1 — The Foundation Statement

Roman Creative Studio was built on a conviction: that small businesses, local entrepreneurs, faith communities, and underserved founders deserve the same quality of digital presence as Fortune 500 companies — and that design, accessibility, and technology should never be gatekept by budget.

Every system in this Operating System — every process, every product, every policy — exists to serve that conviction. We build beautiful, accessible, high-performing digital experiences for people who are building something that matters.

The legacy of Roman Creative Studio is not measured in revenue. It is measured in:
- Small businesses that thrived because their website finally worked
- Communities that were reached because their church or nonprofit had a professional presence
- Designers and developers who learned their craft through our courses and found their career
- Entrepreneurs who built agencies because we showed them how
- People with disabilities who could access the web because we insisted on accessibility

## Section 8 — Social Impact Commitment

### Philosophy

Roman Creative Studio is a for-profit company that takes its social responsibilities seriously. We believe business can be a force for good — not through charity alone, but through the way we conduct every transaction, build every product, and welcome every community member.

### 8.1 Accessibility Advocacy

Accessibility is not a feature we add. It is the foundation we build on.

**Commitments:**
- WCAG 2.1 AA minimum on every client deliverable — non-negotiable
- Accessibility audit offered free of charge to nonprofits and faith communities annually
- Public accessibility resources available at no cost on romancreativestudio.co
- Advocacy for accessible web design in every speaking opportunity and publication
- Partnership with disability advocacy organizations to test and validate our standards

**Target:** By Year 5, RCS accessibility resources have been used by 1,000+ designers and developers.

### 8.2 Mentorship Program

We exist in part because someone believed in us. We pay that forward.

**Structure:**
- Year 1–2: Informal mentorship; 2–3 mentees at a time via community or direct outreach
- Year 3+: Formal mentorship program through Agency Circle community
- Year 5+: Annual cohort of 10–20 mentees (designers, developers, agency founders)
- Year 10+: Named mentorship scholarship with selection process

**Priority groups:** Young designers of color, first-generation entrepreneurs, creatives from underserved communities, career changers entering the design/tech field.

### 8.3 Education Access

Knowledge should not be gatekept by ability to pay.

**Commitments:**
- Free tier for all community platforms — no one locked out entirely for cost
- Scholarship program for courses: 1 free seat per cohort for qualifying applicants
- First module of every course free — proof of value before payment required
- Public library of tutorials, templates, and resources that never require a purchase
- Annual "Free Week": one week per year where premium resources are unlocked for all

**Target:** By Year 5, RCS free resources have been used by 10,000+ people.

### 8.4 Small Business Support

Our clients are the backbone of their communities. We treat them that way.

**Commitments:**
- One pro-bono or deeply discounted project per quarter for qualifying small businesses or nonprofits
- "Good Neighbor" pricing for local churches, community organizations, and nonprofits: 25% discount
- Free consultation for any small business owner — no sales pressure, genuine help
- Resource center with free guides for businesses not yet ready to invest in professional services

**Target:** By Year 3, 12+ pro-bono or discounted projects delivered.

### 8.5 Open Source Contribution

We benefit from open-source tools every day. We give back.

**Commitments:**
- Year 1–2: Publish at least one free tool, template, or utility in GitHub
- Year 3+: Maintain at least one active open-source repository with documentation
- Year 5+: Contribute to or sponsor an accessibility-focused open-source project
- Year 10+: Open-source one internal tool that other agencies can use

**License philosophy:** MIT or Apache 2.0 for utilities; Creative Commons for design resources.

### 8.6 Nonprofit & Faith Community Priority

Nonprofits and faith communities serve the people our clients serve. We honor that work.

**Commitments:**
- Reserved capacity: minimum 10% of project slots per year for nonprofit/faith clients
- Flexible payment terms for nonprofits: extended payment plans with no penalty
- Template discounts: nonprofit pricing on all template and digital product purchases
- Annual "Church & Community" project: one fully pro-bono website per year for a qualifying faith or community organization

### 8.7 Community Workshops

We bring knowledge to the communities that need it most.

**Structure:**
- Year 1–2: Online workshops via social or email list; 2–4 per year
- Year 3+: Monthly free webinars on web design, accessibility, and small business digital strategy
- Year 5+: In-person community workshops in partnership with local organizations (churches, chambers of commerce, community centers)
- Year 10+: Annual free conference or summit track dedicated to underserved business communities

### 8.8 Scholarship Program

Investment in people is the highest-return investment we can make.

**Structure:**
- Year 2: First scholarship — 1 free course seat for one qualifying applicant
- Year 3: Expand to 3–5 scholarships per cohort across courses
- Year 5: Named scholarship fund with defined selection criteria
- Year 10: Annual scholarship of $2,500–$5,000 for a designer/developer from an underrepresented background entering the field

**Selection criteria:** Financial need, demonstrated commitment to craft, alignment with RCS values (accessibility, community, excellence).

## Section 10 — The Founder's Letter

*This letter is written to the future: to the team members who will carry this work forward, to the clients we have not yet met, to the community members who will find their calling through something we built, and to Alexander's future self.*

---

I started Roman Creative Studio because I believed something that wasn't obvious: that the quality of your digital presence should not be determined by the size of your budget.

Small businesses, churches, nonprofits, and first-generation entrepreneurs deserve beautiful, fast, and accessible websites — not as a luxury, but as a baseline. I built this company to make that true for as many people as possible.

Every document in this Operating System, every process, every product, every standard was built in service of that conviction. Not because it was the easiest path, but because it was the right one.

To the team members reading this: you joined something that was built on integrity. That means we do the work right even when no one is checking. It means we keep our promises even when it costs us. It means we treat every client — the $3,500 website and the $12,000 platform — with the same level of care and attention.

To the clients past and future: you trusted us with something that matters. A website is not just code and pixels. It is your business, your community, your livelihood. We do not take that lightly.

To whoever is reading this decades from now: this company was built to last. Not through rigidity, but through clarity of purpose. The tactics will change. The technology will change. The team will change. But the commitment — to beauty, to accessibility, to care, to excellence — that does not change.

Build something worth building. Build it well. Build it for the people who need it most.

*— Alexander Roman*
