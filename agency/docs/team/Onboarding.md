## Purpose

Provide a complete, repeatable onboarding experience that gives every new hire or contractor the knowledge, tools, and confidence to contribute to Roman Creative Studio from Day 1.

**Business Value:** Reduces ramp time. Protects culture. Eliminates repeated explanations from CEO. Creates consistent first impressions for every team member.

**Owner:** CEO / Operations Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** Training.md, CompanyCulture.md, EmployeeHandbook.md, ContractorHandbook.md, KnowledgeBase.md

---

## Onboarding Principles

1. **Warm before overwhelming.** Day 1 is culture and welcome, not task assignments.
2. **Documentation-first.** Every system, tool, and process is pre-documented. No verbal-only explanations.
3. **30/60/90 goals set in advance.** New hires know what success looks like before their first day.
4. **Buddy system.** Every hire gets a designated peer mentor for the first 30 days.
5. **Feedback loops early.** Check-in at Day 7, Day 30, Day 90. Ask what's working and what's confusing.

## First Month (Days 16–30) — Full Contribution

**Goal:** New hire operates at 70–80% of expected productivity. Contributing to real work with minimal supervision.

**Expectations:**
- Independently using all primary tools
- Completing assigned tasks on time
- Asking questions via Slack (not waiting until meetings)
- Participating in relevant meetings (speaking up, not just listening)
- Updating project management tool daily

**30-Day Review:** Formal 30-minute review with CEO or manager.

**30-Day Review Topics:**
- Reviewing original 30/60/90 plan
- What's going well?
- What needs improvement?
- Are there tools or processes causing friction?
- Updated expectations for Days 31–90
- One goal for the next 30 days

---

## Days 31–90 — Ramping to Full Speed

**Goal:** New hire reaches 100% expected productivity. Begins identifying improvements to their area.

**Month 2 (Days 31–60):**
- Fully independent on primary tasks
- Begins owning end-to-end deliverables (with review)
- Weekly 1:1 with manager for feedback and coaching
- Contributes to at least 1 documentation improvement

**Month 3 (Days 61–90):**
- Full ownership of role responsibilities
- Has identified at least 1 process improvement
- Peer review relationship established with at least 1 team member
- Contributes to team retrospective

**90-Day Review:** Formal 60-minute review with CEO or manager.

**90-Day Review Topics:**
- 30/60/90 plan: Did we hit goals?
- Calibrate KPI expectations for remainder of year
- Discuss career path and growth interests
- Confirm culture fit and engagement
- Set goals for next quarter
- Discuss any adjustments to role scope

## Contractor Onboarding (Abbreviated)

For freelancers and contractors, onboarding is shorter and more focused:

**Day 1 (2–3 hours):**
- Welcome call with CEO (30 min)
- Contracts and NDA signed (pre-start)
- Project brief reviewed
- Tools provisioned (project-specific only)
- Communication norms explained (Slack channel, async-first)
- Feedback/revision process explained
- First deliverable and deadline confirmed

**Ongoing:**
- Weekly async update expected
- Feedback given within 48 hours of submission
- Scope changes communicated in writing
