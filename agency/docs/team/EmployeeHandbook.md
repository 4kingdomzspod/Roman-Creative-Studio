## Purpose

The Employee Handbook is the official guide for all full-time and part-time employees of Roman Creative Studio. It documents policies, expectations, benefits, and procedures that govern the employment relationship.

## Employment

### At-Will Employment
All employment at Roman Creative Studio is at-will (where applicable by law). This means either the employee or the company may end the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, with reasonable notice.

### Work Hours
- **Core hours:** 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM in employee's local timezone (overlap required for collaboration)
- **Total expected hours:** 40 hours/week (full-time) or as specified in offer letter (part-time)
- **Flexible:** Employees may arrange hours outside core hours with manager approval
- **Overtime:** Overtime must be pre-approved by CEO. Unauthorized overtime is not compensated.

### Work Location
Roman Creative Studio is a remote-first company. Employees may work from any location with:
- Reliable internet connection (>25 Mbps download, >10 Mbps upload)
- Professional background for video calls (or virtual background)
- Quiet environment during client and team meetings

## Compensation

### Pay Schedule
- **Full-time employees:** Semi-monthly (1st and 15th of each month)
- **Part-time employees:** Monthly (1st of each month)
- **Method:** Direct deposit (ACH) to designated bank account

### Salary Reviews
- **Annual:** Every January 1 (cost of living adjustment, minimum 3%)
- **Performance-based:** Following promotion or exceptional performance period
- **Market review:** Every 2 years to ensure competitive compensation

### Expenses
- Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission
- Submit via Google Form with receipts
- Pre-approval required for expenses >$100 not in budget
- Education budget: see Training.md

## Performance Standards

### General Expectations
- Deliver assigned work on time and at the agreed quality standard
- Communicate proactively about blockers, delays, or concerns
- Attend all required meetings and respond to messages within 4 business hours during core hours
- Meet or exceed KPIs defined in your role description
- Participate in performance reviews
- Complete assigned training modules

### Performance Improvement Process
If an employee's performance falls below standard:

1. **Informal conversation (verbal):** Manager addresses concern directly, documents in 1:1 notes
2. **Written feedback:** If pattern continues, written feedback document shared with employee
3. **Performance Improvement Plan (PIP):** 30–60 day structured plan with specific, measurable goals
4. **PIP outcome review:** Goals met = continued employment; goals not met = termination

Some behaviors (harassment, dishonesty, confidentiality breach) result in immediate termination without prior steps.

## Code of Conduct

### Professional Standards
- Treat all colleagues, clients, and partners with respect
- Represent Roman Creative Studio professionally in all communications
- Maintain confidentiality of client information, business data, and trade secrets
- Disclose any conflict of interest to CEO immediately
- Do not use company tools or time for personal business ventures

### Conflict of Interest
Employees may not:
- Work for direct competitors while employed at RCS without written CEO approval
- Solicit RCS clients for personal or other business purposes
- Use confidential RCS information for personal gain
- Accept gifts from clients or vendors valued at >$50 without disclosure

### Social Media Policy
- Employees may discuss their role at RCS on personal social media
- Do not disclose confidential client information or project details
- Do not post negative commentary about clients, RCS, or team members publicly
- Positive representation of RCS and client work is encouraged (with client permission for portfolio sharing)

### Moonlighting Policy
Employees may take on freelance or side projects provided:
- They don't conflict with RCS client work
- They don't use RCS tools, time, or resources
- They don't involve direct competitors or active RCS prospects
- CEO is notified of any significant side work (>10 hours/week)

## Safety & Wellbeing

### Mental Health
- Roman Creative Studio supports mental health as a genuine health issue
- Mental health days are available and treated as sick days (no judgment)
- Workload concerns should be raised with CEO immediately; we will accommodate
- We do not glorify overwork. A sustainable pace produces better work.

### Ergonomics
- Remote employees are responsible for their own ergonomic workspace
- Home office stipend available (Stage 4+) to support proper setup
- If experiencing ergonomic issues, notify CEO; we will help with solutions

## Confidentiality & IP

### Confidentiality
All employees must maintain strict confidentiality of:
- Client identities, projects, and business information
- RCS proprietary processes, pricing, and business data
- Team member salary and performance information
- Financial data of Roman Creative Studio

Confidentiality obligations survive termination of employment.

### Intellectual Property
All work created using RCS time, tools, or resources is the exclusive property of Roman Creative Studio (or assigned to clients per their contract). This includes:
- Design files, code, copy, and creative work
- Processes, systems, and documentation
- Client relationships developed through RCS
