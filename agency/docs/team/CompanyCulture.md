## Purpose

Define the culture of Roman Creative Studio — the values, behaviors, and standards that define how we work, how we treat each other, and how we treat our clients. Culture is not a poster on the wall. It is demonstrated every day in every decision.

**Business Value:** Culture is the operating system of the company. Great culture attracts great people, retains them longer, and produces better work for clients. It is the primary competitive advantage that cannot be copied.

## Core Values

### 1. Craftsmanship
> *We never ship work we're not proud of.*

Every pixel, every line of code, every word of copy is reviewed. Good enough is not enough. We take time to get it right. Shortcuts that compromise quality are not acceptable, even under deadline pressure.

**What this looks like:**
- We review our own work before submitting it
- We speak up when something isn't right, even if it means more time
- We maintain our quality standards regardless of project size or budget
- We treat every client's brand as if it were our own

---

### 2. Ownership
> *We own problems, not just tasks.*

If you see something wrong, you fix it or flag it. You don't wait to be asked. You don't pass it to someone else and consider it done. You own your work end-to-end.

**What this looks like:**
- If a project is going off track, you raise it early — never hide problems
- If you make a mistake, you acknowledge it immediately and fix it
- If something is outside your scope but affects the client, you communicate it
- You don't say "that's not my job" unless it truly isn't

---

### 3. Client First
> *The client's success is our success.*

We measure our work by what it does for our clients, not by what we find impressive. A beautiful design that doesn't convert is a failure. A technically perfect site that launches late has let a client down.

**What this looks like:**
- We ask what success looks like for the client before we design anything
- We communicate proactively about anything that might affect their experience
- We advocate for the user in every design decision
- We treat client feedback as valuable data, not an obstacle

---

### 4. Continuous Learning
> *We stay curious. The industry moves fast; we move faster.*

Digital design and development evolve constantly. What worked two years ago may be obsolete. We invest in our own education, share what we learn, and build a team that grows individually and collectively.

**What this looks like:**
- Every team member has an education budget and is expected to use it
- We share learnings in team channels, not hoard them
- We read, watch, and attend events in our industry
- We document what we learn so the whole team benefits

---

### 5. Honesty & Integrity
> *We say what we mean. We keep our promises.*

With clients, with each other, and with ourselves. If a deadline is unrealistic, we say so early. If a design direction isn't working, we say so directly. If we made a mistake, we own it immediately.

**What this looks like:**
- We never overpromise to win a deal
- We never hide problems from clients or leadership
- We give honest feedback, not comfortable feedback
- We keep our commitments — or communicate immediately when we can't

---

### 6. Respect
> *Every person matters. Every perspective is heard.*

From the CEO to the newest contractor, every voice has value at Roman Creative Studio. We don't tolerate hierarchy that silences good ideas. We don't tolerate behavior that makes anyone feel unsafe, invisible, or undervalued.

**What this looks like:**
- We listen to understand, not to respond
- We treat every client, partner, and vendor with the same respect we'd want
- We give credit generously and take blame honestly
- We address disrespect immediately and directly, never passively

---

### 7. Innovation
> *We look for a better way.*

We don't do things because "that's how it's always been done." We question processes, test new tools, prototype ideas, and build systems that improve over time.

**What this looks like:**
- We allocate time to explore and experiment (Innovation Lab)
- We document improvements so they become standards, not one-off solutions
- We try new tools with clear evaluation criteria before committing
- We reward ideas that improve our work, even if they don't pan out

---

### 8. Growth Mindset
> *Failure is data. Feedback is a gift.*

We don't protect our egos at the expense of improvement. A project that didn't go well is a learning opportunity. Critical feedback is valuable. We talk about what went wrong without blame.

**What this looks like:**
- Project retrospectives focus on process, not people
- We share what didn't work as openly as we share wins
- We ask for feedback on our own work, not just give it
- We don't repeat the same mistakes

## Culture Behaviors We Protect

These are not aspirational. These are the minimum standard for being part of Roman Creative Studio:

| Behavior | Description |
|----------|-------------|
| Communicate proactively | Never go silent on a problem or delay. Communicate early and often. |
| Deliver what you commit to | If you said you'd do it by Thursday, it's done by Thursday. |
| Ask for help early | It's a strength to ask. Struggling silently hurts everyone. |
| Document as you go | Don't be a single point of failure. Write it down. |
| Treat all feedback as data | No defensiveness. No pouting. Listen, process, respond. |
| Share your wins and learnings | Knowledge shared is knowledge multiplied. |
| Protect the client experience | Clients trust us. Honor that trust in every interaction. |

## Behaviors That Are Not Tolerated

| Behavior | Response |
|----------|----------|
| Hiding problems or delays | Immediate conversation; pattern results in dismissal |
| Disrespecting teammates or clients | Direct address; no second chances for harassment |
| Delivering work below quality standards repeatedly | Performance plan; if no improvement, dismissal |
| Taking credit for others' work | Immediate correction; pattern results in dismissal |
| Breaking client confidentiality | Immediate review; likely dismissal |
| Dishonesty about work status | Immediate conversation; pattern = dismissal |

## Remote Work Culture (Stage 1–3)

Roman Creative Studio operates remotely. Our culture must thrive without physical proximity.

**Remote work standards:**
- **Async-first.** Slack messages don't require immediate responses. Expect replies within 4 business hours.
- **Camera on for client calls.** Optional but encouraged for internal team calls.
- **Over-communicate.** When in doubt, share the update. Silence breeds uncertainty.
- **Document everything.** Remote work collapses without documentation.
- **Boundaries matter.** Respect people's working hours. Don't expect 9 PM responses.
- **Celebrate remotely.** Use #wins channel actively. Recognize effort and outcomes publicly.

## How We Make Decisions

| Decision Type | Process |
|-------------|--------|
| Individual task decisions | Person owns the task decides |
| Project approach | Designer/Developer proposes, PM reviews, CEO approves if >$1,000 impact |
| Tool adoption | Proposal in Slack #ideas, CEO approves if >$50/month |
| Hiring decisions | CEO leads; Director input required Stage 3+ |
| Pricing decisions | CEO approves all; Sales can flex 10% within approved range |
| Major company direction | CEO; team input requested, not required |
