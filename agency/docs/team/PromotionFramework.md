## Purpose

Define the objective, transparent criteria by which team members at Roman Creative Studio are considered for promotion — eliminating favoritism, recency bias, and ambiguity.

**Business Value:** Retains top performers. Creates a culture of fairness. Motivates ambition. Reduces politicking and resentment.

**Owner:** CEO / Department Directors  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** CareerPaths.md, PerformanceReviews.md, RecognitionProgram.md, RoleDescriptions.md

---

## Promotion Philosophy

1. **Promote on demonstrated performance, not potential alone.** A candidate must already be performing at the next level before receiving the title and compensation.
2. **Promotions are transparent.** Criteria are documented. Candidates know exactly what is required.
3. **Promotions are not guaranteed by tenure.** Time at level is a minimum bar, not a guarantee.
4. **Multiple paths.** IC track and management track are equally valued. Choosing not to manage is not penalized.
5. **Compensation follows immediately.** A promotion without a compensation increase is not a real promotion.

## Promotion Eligibility Requirements

### Minimum Time-at-Level Requirements

| Level | Minimum Time at Current Level |
|-------|------------------------------|
| Junior → Mid | 12 months |
| Mid → Senior | 18 months |
| Senior → Lead/Director | 24 months |
| Director → Executive | 36 months |

**Note:** These are minimums. Strong performers who meet all other criteria can be promoted at minimums. Typical time is 6–12 months longer.

## Promotion Evaluation Criteria

Every promotion is evaluated across 6 dimensions. Each rated 1–5.

### Dimension 1: Core Skill Mastery (weight 30%)
- Has the candidate mastered the skills required for their current level?
- Are they demonstrating skills expected of the next level without being asked?
- Role-specific technical and craft skills

**Score 5:** Consistently exceeds current level expectations; demonstrating next-level skills daily  
**Score 3:** Meets current level expectations reliably  
**Score 1:** Still developing current level competencies

---

### Dimension 2: Output Quality (weight 25%)
- Is the work excellent, not just acceptable?
- Do clients, teammates, and leadership recognize quality?
- Are KPIs met or exceeded?

**Score 5:** Work quality is recognized by clients and peers as exceptional; zero quality issues for 6+ months  
**Score 3:** Consistent quality that meets RCS standards  
**Score 1:** Quality inconsistent; requires regular correction

---

### Dimension 3: Ownership & Initiative (weight 20%)
- Does the candidate identify problems and fix them without being asked?
- Do they own outcomes, not just tasks?
- Do they improve things around them?

**Score 5:** Proactively identifies and solves problems beyond their role; improves systems without prompt  
**Score 3:** Reliably executes assigned work; occasionally identifies improvements  
**Score 1:** Executes tasks when assigned; rarely goes beyond assigned scope

---

### Dimension 4: Communication & Collaboration (weight 10%)
- Does the candidate communicate clearly and on time?
- Do they make teammates and clients better?
- Do they give and receive feedback constructively?

**Score 5:** Elevates the communication quality of everyone around them; models the standard  
**Score 3:** Communicates clearly; rarely causes confusion  
**Score 1:** Communication gaps cause friction or delays

---

### Dimension 5: Leadership Signal (weight 10%)
- Are they already informally leading? (mentoring, setting standards, guiding peers)
- Is this proportional to the level they're being promoted to?

**Score 5 (management track):** Already effectively leading others without the title  
**Score 5 (IC track):** Already seen as a technical authority and mentor by peers  
**Score 3:** Beginning to show leadership in small ways  
**Score 1:** No observable leadership signal

---

### Dimension 6: Culture & Values Alignment (weight 5%)
- Does the candidate embody Roman Creative Studio's values and principles?
- Would promoting this person strengthen or weaken the culture?

**Score 5:** A culture exemplar; others look to them for how to behave  
**Score 3:** Strong culture alignment; no concerns  
**Score 1:** Values gaps; cultural friction with teammates

## Promotion Score Calculation

```
Promotion Score = 
  (D1 × 0.30) + (D2 × 0.25) + (D3 × 0.20) + 
  (D4 × 0.10) + (D5 × 0.10) + (D6 × 0.05)

Maximum Score: 5.0

Promotion threshold: 3.8+
Promotion eligible but not ready: 3.0–3.79
Not ready: <3.0
```

## Promotion Process

### Step 1: Self-Assessment (2 weeks before review)
- Candidate completes Promotion Self-Assessment form
- Rate themselves on all 6 dimensions with evidence
- Describe 3 specific examples of next-level performance
- State their case for promotion in writing

### Step 2: Manager Assessment
- Manager independently scores all 6 dimensions
- Provides specific examples for each score
- Gathers peer input (optional, Stage 3+)

### Step 3: Calibration Meeting
- CEO + relevant Director review assessment
- Discuss any dimension where candidate and manager scores diverge >1 point
- Determine final score

### Step 4: Decision & Communication
**If score ≥ 3.8:**
- Promotion approved
- Compensation adjustment prepared
- Written promotion letter prepared
- Manager delivers decision in 1:1 (positive framing)
- Announce to team with candidate's permission
- Update all documentation (org chart, role, compensation records)

**If score 3.0–3.79:**
- Manager delivers honest development feedback in 1:1
- Document 3–5 specific things needed for promotion
- Set 6-month development plan with milestones
- Schedule follow-up review at 6 months

**If score <3.0:**
- Manager delivers honest performance feedback in 1:1
- Discuss whether a Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is needed
- Determine if role is a fit issue or development issue
- Set clear 90-day improvement plan

## Compensation Adjustment Policy

| Promotion Type | Compensation Increase |
|---------------|----------------------|
| Junior → Mid | 12–18% |
| Mid → Senior | 15–22% |
| Senior → Lead | 15–20% |
| Lead → Director | 20–30% |
| Director → Executive | 25–35% |

**Effective date:** First of the month following promotion approval  
**Backpay:** None. Compensation reflects going-forward performance at new level.

---

## Out-of-Cycle Promotion Policy

Promotions can happen outside the standard annual cycle when:
1. A role expansion occurs that clearly warrants a level change
2. A team member prevents a critical business failure through exceptional performance
3. Business growth creates a department head vacancy filled by internal promotion

Out-of-cycle promotions require CEO sign-off regardless of company size.

---

## Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails |
|-------------|-------------|
| Promoting to retain (retention promotion) | Promotes someone not ready; undermines team trust |
| Promoting based on tenure alone | Creates entitled culture; ignores performance |
| Promoting based on likability | Bias; destroys fairness |
| "Working manager" without real team | Title inflation with no actual leadership |
| Promoting to fill a gap vs. earned | Sets candidate up to fail in new role |
