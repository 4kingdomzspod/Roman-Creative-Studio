## Welcome

Thank you for partnering with Roman Creative Studio. We work with contractors who represent the same standards we hold internally: exceptional craft, professional communication, and genuine care for outcomes.

This handbook tells you how we work, what we expect, and how we'll support you.

## Engagement Structure

### Types of Contractor Relationships

| Type | Description | Duration |
|------|-------------|----------|
| Project-based | Hired for a specific deliverable (e.g., website copy, logo) | 1–6 weeks |
| Hourly retainer | Recurring hours available on demand | Monthly, as needed |
| Embedded (part-time) | Regular weekly hours on ongoing projects | 3–12 months |
| Advisory | Specialized expertise, not deliverable-based | Varies |

### Independent Contractor Classification
You are an independent contractor, not an employee. This means:
- You control your own schedule (within agreed deadlines)
- You may work with other clients simultaneously (unless exclusivity clause applies)
- You are responsible for your own taxes and insurance
- We will provide a 1099 if your annual total exceeds $600
- We will not withhold taxes, benefits, or provide workers' compensation

## Payment

### Rate & Structure
- Your rate is documented in your Contractor Agreement
- Project-based: invoiced upon deliverable milestones (50% start, 50% completion, or as agreed)
- Hourly: invoiced monthly, by the 1st, for hours worked in prior month

### Invoicing
- Send invoices to: Alexander@romancreativestudio.co
- Invoice must include: your name/business name, date, services rendered, hours (if hourly), amount, payment terms
- Payment is processed within **14 days** of invoice receipt
- Method: ACH bank transfer or check

### Late Payment
If payment is late beyond 14 days:
1. Email Alexander@romancreativestudio.co with invoice attached
2. If unresolved after 7 more days, escalate via phone
3. RCS commits to resolving payment concerns within 21 days of invoice date

## Communication Expectations

### Response Time
- Reply to Slack messages within **4 business hours** during your stated working hours
- Reply to email within **24 hours** on business days
- For urgent project needs, Slack is preferred over email

### Project Updates
- Provide an async update at least **once per week** on active projects
- If blocked or delayed, communicate immediately — don't wait until the deadline passes
- Use Slack for quick questions; use Notion/Google Docs for detailed deliverables

### Availability
- Inform Alexander of availability gaps (vacations, reduced hours) with >1 week notice when possible
- If you cannot meet a deadline, communicate at least 5 business days in advance

## Work Quality Standards

### Our Quality Bar
Roman Creative Studio is a premium agency. Every deliverable that goes to a client represents our brand. Work submitted to us must meet the same standard we would hold ourselves to.

**Minimum quality expectations:**
- Submit only work you have proofread or QA'd yourself
- Deliverables should require no more than 2 rounds of significant revisions
- If you're unsure about a direction, ask before completing the full deliverable

### Revision Policy
- Up to **2 rounds of revisions** are included in project-based contracts
- A revision round is a single set of consolidated feedback, not multiple separate requests
- Additional revision rounds beyond 2: billed at your hourly rate (pro-rated)
- Revisions due to misunderstanding the brief: covered by us
- Revisions due to client changing their mind after approval: billed to project (we handle)

## Intellectual Property

### Ownership
- All work created for Roman Creative Studio projects is the property of RCS or the end client (per client contract)
- You retain no IP rights to work created for RCS unless explicitly stated in your agreement
- You may add work to your portfolio **only with written permission** from RCS
- Portfolio use requires client work to be attributed as "produced for Roman Creative Studio" (not the client directly)

### Portfolio Permission
After project completion, submit a portfolio request to Alexander@romancreativestudio.co. We aim to grant permission for most projects within 14 days. Some projects may be restricted by client NDA.

## Confidentiality

You will have access to confidential information including:
- Client identities, business plans, and website content
- RCS business data, pricing, and processes
- Unreleased design work and development code

**You may not:**
- Discuss RCS clients or projects publicly (including social media)
- Share project files, credentials, or documentation with third parties
- Use client contact information for your own prospecting
- Retain confidential materials after project completion

Confidentiality obligations survive the end of your contract.

## End of Engagement

### Project Completion
- Submit all final files in agreed formats
- Transfer all project-related files to the RCS Google Drive folder or GitHub repo
- Do not retain copies of client materials after project close
- Confirm all access has been revoked or request RCS to revoke

### Early Termination
Either party may terminate the contract with **14 days written notice** (or per your agreement terms). Work completed up to termination date is compensated per your rate. We will not withhold payment for properly completed work.

## Working With Us: Best Practices

1. **Ask questions before starting.** A 10-minute clarification call saves days of wrong-direction work.
2. **Show early drafts.** Don't disappear for a week and return with finished work. Share early, course-correct early.
3. **Communicate blockers immediately.** If you're stuck waiting on client feedback or missing assets, say so in Slack.
4. **Keep your files organized.** Use our naming conventions (provided in project brief).
5. **Be professional in all communications.** Contractors represent Roman Creative Studio when engaging on joint projects.
