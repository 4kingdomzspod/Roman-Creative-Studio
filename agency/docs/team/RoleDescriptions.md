## Purpose

Complete role definitions for every position at Roman Creative Studio — from solo founder to enterprise executive. Each role includes mission, responsibilities, daily/weekly tasks, KPIs, required skills, and growth opportunities.

## ROLE-01: Chief Executive Officer (CEO)

**Mission:** Lead Roman Creative Studio's vision, strategy, culture, and long-term growth. Every major decision flows through this role.

**Current Occupant:** Alexander Roman

**Responsibilities:**
- Set company vision, values, and 3–5 year strategy
- Own P&L and financial health of the business
- Build and lead the leadership team
- Own high-value client relationships
- Make final decisions on pricing, hiring, and product
- Represent the brand publicly (speaking, content, media)
- Approve major contracts, legal agreements, and investments
- Drive culture and organizational health

**Daily Tasks (Stage 1):**
- Client project work (design, development, delivery)
- Lead follow-up and proposal activity
- Email and communication management
- Financial tracking (invoices, expenses)

**Daily Tasks (Stage 3+):**
- Review KPI dashboard (15 min)
- 1:1 or department touchpoint
- Strategic decisions and approvals
- Client relationship management (top 20%)
- Content or thought leadership (30 min)

**Weekly Tasks:**
- All-hands or leadership meeting
- Pipeline review
- Financial review
- 1:1 with each direct report
- Content creation (1 blog or podcast episode)

**KPIs:**
- Annual revenue vs. target
- MRR growth rate
- Net Promoter Score
- Team health and retention
- Pipeline value and close rate

**Required Skills:** Strategic thinking, financial literacy, sales and persuasion, creative leadership, communication, delegation, systems thinking

**Growth Path:** CEO → Executive Chairman / Board Member → Investor

## ROLE-02: Creative Director

**Mission:** Set and maintain the creative standard across all client deliverables. Every design that leaves Roman Creative Studio must meet this standard.

**Stage available:** Stage 3+

**Responsibilities:**
- Oversee all client design work (UI, UX, Brand)
- Develop and enforce the internal design system
- Lead design critiques and review sessions
- Mentor junior and mid-level designers
- Collaborate with Engineering on design handoff quality
- Own accessibility compliance across all creative work
- Contribute to proposal and pitch design
- Drive design innovation and trend awareness

**Daily Tasks:**
- Design review (client work in progress)
- Figma file QA
- Designer check-in (Slack)
- Active design work (50% of time in Stage 3)

**Weekly Tasks:**
- Design critique session with team (60 min)
- 1:1 with each designer
- Review active project status
- Contribute to or review 1 marketing asset

**KPIs:**
- First-round client approval rate: >60%
- Average revision rounds: <2.5
- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance: 100%
- Designer retention: 100% over 12 months
- Design-to-development handoff score: >4/5

**Required Skills:** Figma (expert), typography, UX principles, accessibility, brand systems, team leadership, feedback delivery, Adobe Creative Cloud

**Growth Path:** Creative Director → Chief Creative Officer (CCO)

**Growth Path:** Frontend Dev → Senior Frontend → Engineering Lead → Technical Director

**Stage available:** Stage 4+

**Responsibilities:**

- Bug documentation and severity classification

- Bugs caught pre-launch vs. post-launch: >90% caught in QA
- Accessibility pass rate pre-delivery: 100%
- Performance score at delivery: >90 Lighthouse
- QA checklist completion rate: 100%
- Client-reported bugs (30 days post-launch): <2

**Required Skills:** Accessibility testing tools, Lighthouse, BrowserStack, bug documentation, systematic thinking, attention to detail

**Growth Path:** QA Specialist → Senior QA → QA Lead → Engineering Director
