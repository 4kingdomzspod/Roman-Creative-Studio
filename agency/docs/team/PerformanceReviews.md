## Purpose

Define the complete performance review system at Roman Creative Studio — cadence, format, evaluation criteria, and outcomes — so every team member knows exactly how their performance is measured and what happens as a result.

**Business Value:** Prevents underperformance from compounding. Creates a meritocracy. Reduces turnover by giving people clarity on how to grow. Protects RCS from legal risk in termination situations.

**Owner:** CEO / Department Directors  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** CareerPaths.md, PromotionFramework.md, LeadershipPrinciples.md, Offboarding.md

## 30-Day Check-In

**Purpose:** Ensure the new hire is adjusting well and identify any early friction.

**Format:** Conversational, not evaluative. No score.

**Questions:**
1. What's going better than expected so far?
2. What's been harder than expected?
3. Do you feel clear on your priorities and responsibilities?
4. Do you have the tools and access you need?
5. Is there anything you wish you knew before starting?
6. What's your biggest question about the role or company right now?

**Output:** Brief written summary (200 words max) saved to employee's Notion profile. No formal rating. Action items assigned with owners.

## 90-Day Review

**Purpose:** First formal evaluation. Sets expectations for the next 9 months.

**Self-Assessment (due 5 business days before review):**
- What have you accomplished in your first 90 days?
- What has been most challenging?
- What do you wish you had more support on?
- Rate your own performance (1–5) on each competency below with brief rationale.
- What are your goals for the next 90 days?

**Manager Assessment (completed before review):**
- Written evaluation on all 9 competencies (see below)
- Documented examples for each rating
- Provisional promotion timeline (if applicable)
- Areas of excellence vs. areas for development

**Review Meeting Agenda:**
- Self-assessment discussion (20 min)
- Manager assessment discussion (20 min)
- Alignment on strengths and development areas (10 min)
- Goals for next 90 days (10 min)

**Output:** Signed review document saved in employee file. Goals documented in Notion.

## Annual Performance Review

**Timing:** January of each year (following first full year of employment)  
**Self-assessment due:** December 15  
**Manager assessment due:** December 22  
**Review meetings:** First two weeks of January

### Part 1: Accomplishments Review
- 3–5 most significant accomplishments of the year
- Key projects and outcomes (with data where possible)
- Team contributions beyond individual role
- Documentation and knowledge sharing contributions

### Part 2: Competency Evaluation

Rated 1–5 on each dimension:

| # | Competency | Description |
|---|-----------|-------------|
| 1 | **Quality of Work** | Consistently meets or exceeds RCS quality standards |
| 2 | **Speed & Efficiency** | Delivers work on time and uses time effectively |
| 3 | **Communication** | Clear, timely, professional in all interactions |
| 4 | **Ownership & Initiative** | Identifies and solves problems without being asked |
| 5 | **Continuous Learning** | Actively develops skills; applies learning to work |
| 6 | **Problem Solving** | Finds solutions; doesn't just escalate problems |
| 7 | **Teamwork & Collaboration** | Makes teammates better; positive influence |
| 8 | **Client Focus** | Treats client success as the ultimate measure |
| 9 | **Leadership Signal** | Leads informally; mentors others; drives standards |

**Rating Scale:**
| Score | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| 5 | Exceptional: Consistently far exceeds expectations |
| 4 | Strong: Regularly exceeds expectations |
| 3 | Meets expectations: Reliable, consistent performance |
| 2 | Below expectations: Inconsistent; needs improvement |
| 1 | Unsatisfactory: Significant gap; requires immediate action |

### Part 3: KPI Achievement

Review each KPI defined in the role description:
- Target vs. actual for the year
- Trend (improving, flat, declining)
- Context for any misses (within/outside control)

### Part 4: Development Plan

- Top 2 strengths to amplify in coming year
- Top 2 development areas with specific action plan
- Training goals (from Training.md education budget)
- Career path discussion: where does this person want to go?
- Promotion eligibility discussion (if applicable)

### Part 5: Compensation Decision

| Overall Score | Compensation Adjustment |
|--------------|------------------------|
| 4.5–5.0 | Market + 8–15% merit increase |
| 3.5–4.4 | Cost of living (3–5%) + 3–6% merit increase |
| 3.0–3.4 | Cost of living (3%) only |
| 2.0–2.9 | No increase; placed on development plan |
| <2.0 | PIP initiated; no increase |

## Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)

**When triggered:**
- Annual score below 2.0
- Consistent pattern of missed deadlines (3+ in 60 days)
- Quality issues despite coaching (3+ documented corrections)
- Behavioral issues (communication failures, attitude problems)
- Repeated client complaints

**PIP Structure:**

```
PIP Document includes:
  1. Summary of concerns (specific, documented examples)
  2. Expected standard (what "good" looks like)
  3. Specific, measurable improvement goals (3–5 goals)
  4. Timeline (30 or 60 days)
  5. Support provided by RCS (coaching, training, tools)
  6. Check-in schedule (weekly)
  7. Consequences if goals not met
  8. Employee signature acknowledging receipt
```

**PIP check-ins:** Weekly 30-minute 1:1 during PIP period  
**Mid-PIP assessment:** At 50% of PIP duration  
**PIP outcome:**
- Goals met: Return to normal review cycle, note improvement
- Goals partially met: PIP extended 30 days
- Goals not met: Employment termination (with documentation)

## Documentation & Records

- All performance reviews stored in a secure, private Notion employee file
- Review documents require both manager and employee signature (digital)
- PIP documents require CEO signature regardless of company size
- Employee has access to their own review history
- Employee files retained for 7 years post-employment
