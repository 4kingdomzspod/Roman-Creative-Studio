# Lead Magnet System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the lead magnet strategy for Roman Creative Studio — the conversion assets that capture email addresses from qualified visitors who are not yet ready to book a Discovery Call. A lead magnet bridges the gap between a visitor who is interested and a client who is ready to invest.

---

## Lead Magnet Philosophy

> **A lead magnet is not a bribe. It is a demonstration of value that earns the right to continue the conversation.**

RCS lead magnets are designed to:
1. Attract the right visitor (business owners, not students or competitors)
2. Demonstrate RCS expertise immediately
3. Address a specific pain point the visitor already has
4. Create a natural transition toward a Discovery Call

## Lead Magnet Catalog

### Lead Magnet 1 — Website Audit Checklist
**Title:** `"The 27-Point Website Audit Checklist: Is Your Website Costing You Customers?"`

**Format:** PDF, 2–3 pages

**Who it attracts:** Business owners who suspect their website is underperforming

**What it covers:**
- Conversion section (7 checks): CTA presence, headline clarity, mobile experience, load speed, social proof, contact form, phone number visibility
- SEO section (8 checks): Title tags, meta descriptions, H1 presence, image alt text, sitemap, Google Business Profile, page speed, HTTPS
- Trust section (5 checks): Professional design quality, testimonials, About page, address/contact info, SSL
- Content section (4 checks): Value proposition clarity, industry-specific content, case studies/portfolio, blog presence
- Technical section (3 checks): Console errors, broken links, Core Web Vitals

**Last page:** `"Score your website. If you scored under 20/27, your website needs attention. Book a free Discovery Call and we'll walk through your results together."`

**Conversion path:** Download → Welcome email sequence → Discovery Call CTA at Day 3, 7

## Lead Capture System

### Placement Rules

Lead magnets are promoted in the following locations:

| Placement | Lead Magnet | Trigger |
|---|---|---|
| Blog article footer (relevant category) | Matching lead magnet | End of article CTA |
| Industry landing page | Industry-specific guide | Above footer section |
| Homepage (secondary CTA) | Website Audit Checklist | Hero section secondary button |
| Instagram bio link | All (rotating or dedicated landing page) | Bio link |
| LinkedIn featured section | Audit Checklist + top industry guide | Profile visit |
| Exit-intent popup (optional) | Website Audit Checklist | Exit intent trigger |
| TikTok / Reels bio link | Lead magnet landing page | Video CTA reference |

### Lead Capture Form Requirements
- **Fields:** First name + email address only. No phone number on initial capture. No company name. No message field.
- **Button text:** `"Get the Free Guide"` / `"Download the Checklist"` / `"Send Me the Guide"` — never just `"Submit"`.
- **Privacy copy:** One line below the button: `"No spam. Unsubscribe anytime."`
- **Delivery:** Automated email sent immediately with the PDF attached or linked.
- **Redirect:** Thank you page with 2 options: (1) Book a Discovery Call now / (2) Read a related article.

## Post-Download Follow-Up System

Every lead magnet download triggers an automated email sequence (documented fully in `EmailMarketingSystem.md`).

**Standard post-download sequence structure:**

| Day | Email | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Day 0 (immediate) | Delivery email | Deliver the asset + set expectations |
| Day 2 | Value email | Teach one additional insight related to the lead magnet topic |
| Day 4 | Proof email | Share a relevant case study or client result |
| Day 7 | Conversion email | Direct CTA to book Discovery Call |
| Day 14 | Nurture email | Share a relevant blog article |
| Day 21 | Soft CTA email | Re-engage with a different angle or lead magnet |

After Day 21, the lead moves to the long-term monthly nurture sequence.

## Lead Magnet Design Standards

- **Cover page:** RCS brand colors. Title. Tagline. `romancreativestudio.co` URL.
- **Interior pages:** Clean, readable layout. Cormorant Garamond for section headers (if rendered in PDF). Inter for body text. Gold accent `#D4AF37` for section dividers or callout boxes.
- **Last page:** Always includes: RCS logo, contact email `Alexander@romancreativestudio.co`, website `romancreativestudio.co`, Discovery Call CTA.
- **File format:** PDF. Optimized for screen viewing (not print). Maximum 2MB file size.
- **Filename:** `RCS-[Lead-Magnet-Name]-Guide.pdf` (no spaces in filename).
