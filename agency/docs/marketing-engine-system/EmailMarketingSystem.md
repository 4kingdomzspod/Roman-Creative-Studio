# Email Marketing System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the complete email marketing system for Roman Creative Studio. Email is the highest-ROI marketing channel available when used correctly. An email list is an owned asset — unlike social media followers, it cannot be taken away by an algorithm change.

RCS email marketing serves two functions: **nurture** (building trust with subscribers over time) and **conversion** (moving ready subscribers toward a Discovery Call).

---

## Email Marketing Philosophy

> **Email is not a broadcast channel. It is a one-to-one conversation at scale. Every email must either teach something useful or invite a specific action — never both at once, and never neither.**

RCS email rules:
1. Every email has one purpose. One topic. One CTA.
2. Emails provide genuine value before asking for anything.
3. Frequency is consistent — readers know when to expect emails.
4. Unsubscribing is easy and never penalized.
5. No purchased lists. No cold mass emails. Subscribers are people who requested to hear from RCS.

## Email Sequence Architecture

### Sequence 1 — Welcome Sequence
**Trigger:** New subscriber (lead magnet download or newsletter sign-up)
**Duration:** 5 emails over 7 days
**Purpose:** Introduce RCS, set expectations, deliver immediate value, and move the subscriber toward a Discovery Call

---

#### Welcome Email 1 — Delivery + First Impression
**Send:** Immediately on subscribe
**Subject:** `Your [Lead Magnet Name] is here — plus one more thing`

**Body structure:**
- Deliver the asset (PDF link or attachment)
- 2–3 sentences on what to look for in the guide
- Brief RCS introduction (2 sentences max): who we are, who we serve
- One sentence setting expectations: `"Over the next few days I'll share a few things that might change how you think about your website."`
- No CTA in this email (delivering value only)

**Tone:** Warm, direct, professional. Not corporate. Not salesy.

---

#### Welcome Email 2 — The Problem
**Send:** Day 2
**Subject:** `The real reason most [industry] websites don't generate leads`

**Body structure:**
- Identify the #1 mistake business owners make with their website (tied to the lead magnet topic)
- Short, specific, and educational (150–200 words)
- No pitch in the body
- Soft CTA at the end: `"Tomorrow I'll share what high-performing [industry] websites do differently."`

---

#### Welcome Email 3 — The Solution
**Send:** Day 3
**Subject:** `What high-converting [industry] websites do differently`

**Body structure:**
- 3 specific things that separate websites that convert from websites that don't
- Industry-specific examples where possible
- 150–200 words
- Soft CTA: `"I'll show you a real example tomorrow."`

---

#### Welcome Email 4 — Proof
**Send:** Day 5
**Subject:** `How [client type] went from [problem] to [result]`

**Body structure:**
- Mini case study (3–4 short paragraphs)
- Before: what the client's situation was
- Solution: what RCS built and why
- Result: specific outcome
- Link to full case study on website
- Soft CTA: `"If your [industry] website isn't generating [desired outcome], I'd like to take a look."`

---

#### Welcome Email 5 — Invitation
**Send:** Day 7
**Subject:** `Want me to review your website? (No obligation)`

**Body structure:**
- Direct, confident offer to review their website on a free Discovery Call
- 2–3 sentences on what the call covers
- Clear expectation: no hard sell, no commitment required
- Primary CTA: `[Book Your Free Discovery Call →]`
- Secondary text: `"Not ready yet? No problem — I'll keep sharing useful content on [day] each week."`

### Sequence 2 — Educational Nurture Sequence
**Trigger:** Subscriber who did not book a Discovery Call after the Welcome Sequence
**Duration:** Ongoing weekly send (Thursday)
**Purpose:** Keep RCS top of mind, continue building authority, and convert when the subscriber becomes ready

**Format:** Weekly newsletter

**Newsletter structure:**
```
Subject: [Educational or curiosity-driven subject line]

Opening line: [Hook — one compelling sentence]

Main content: [One useful tip, insight, or mini case study — 200–300 words]

Resource link: [This week's blog article — "Read the full article →"]

CTA: [Book a Discovery Call] or [Download a guide]

Sign-off: Alexander, Roman Creative Studio
         Alexander@romancreativestudio.co
         romancreativestudio.co
```

**Content rotation (4-week cycle):**
| Week | Theme | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Educational tip | Education |
| Week 2 | Case study / result | Proof |
| Week 3 | Authority insight / opinion | Authority |
| Week 4 | Conversion offer | Conversion |

---

### Sequence 3 — Authority Sequence
**Trigger:** Subscriber has been on the list for 30+ days without booking a Discovery Call
**Duration:** 3 emails over 2 weeks
**Purpose:** Reinforce RCS authority and differentiation before re-attempting conversion

#### Authority Email 1 — The Standard
**Subject:** `Why we don't take every project`
**Body:** Explain RCS's qualification process and why it benefits clients. Position scarcity and selectivity as quality signals.

#### Authority Email 2 — The Method
**Subject:** `Our process: why it works when others don't`
**Body:** Walk through 3–4 steps of the RCS project process with the reason behind each. Show depth of thinking.

#### Authority Email 3 — The Difference
**Subject:** `Template agencies vs. what we actually build`
**Body:** Contrast the RCS approach against commodity web design. Specific differences. Outcome-oriented. No competitor names.

---

### Sequence 4 — Conversion Sequence
**Trigger:** Subscriber who has engaged (opened 3+ emails) but not booked
**Duration:** 3 emails over 7 days
**Purpose:** Convert engaged, warm subscribers who need a final nudge

#### Conversion Email 1 — The Direct Ask
**Subject:** `Quick question about your website`
**Body:** Simple, direct. Ask if they are still working to improve their website. Offer the Discovery Call. Short (under 100 words).

#### Conversion Email 2 — The Fear of Inaction
**Subject:** `What staying with your current website is actually costing you`
**Body:** Frame the cost of NOT acting. Calculate the opportunity cost of one lost lead per month. Educational, not manipulative.

#### Conversion Email 3 — The Last Offer
**Subject:** `Last chance to book this month`
**Body:** Limited availability messaging (honest only — do not manufacture scarcity). Clear CTA. After this email, subscriber moves back to weekly nurture.

| Unsubscribe link | In every email footer (legal requirement) |
| Plain text version | Always enabled |

---

## Subject Line Rules

- Write for curiosity or direct value — not clickbait
- Use the reader's industry or problem when possible: `"Is your dental website losing patients?"`
- Numbers work: `"3 things your website is missing"`
- Questions work: `"Why does your competitor rank higher than you?"`
- Avoid spam trigger words: `FREE`, `GUARANTEED`, `LIMITED TIME!!`, `ACT NOW`
- Avoid all caps
- Never use deceptive subject lines (`"Re: Your inquiry"` when there was no inquiry)

## Email Performance Targets

| Metric | Target |
|---|---|
| Open rate | ≥ 40% |
| Click-through rate | ≥ 3% |
| Unsubscribe rate | ≤ 0.5% per send |
| Spam complaint rate | ≤ 0.1% per send |
| Discovery Call bookings from email | Track monthly |

**Review cadence:** Monthly performance review. A/B test subject lines quarterly.

---

## List Health Practices

- Re-engagement campaign for subscribers inactive for 90+ days: 2-email sequence asking if they want to stay subscribed
- Remove unconfirmed and bounced emails monthly
- Segment list by: lead magnet downloaded, industry, engagement level
- Never send the same email to unengaged subscribers as to engaged ones
- Comply with CAN-SPAM (US) and GDPR (EU) requirements: visible unsubscribe, physical address in footer
