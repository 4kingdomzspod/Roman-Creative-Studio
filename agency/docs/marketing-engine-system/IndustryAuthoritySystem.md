# Industry Authority System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the industry-specific authority strategy for Roman Creative Studio. Rather than positioning as a generalist agency, RCS builds deep, documented expertise in each target industry. Each industry becomes a fully developed content silo with its own landing page, blog articles, lead magnet, social content, and SEO keyword cluster.

Owning an industry niche on Google is faster and more durable than competing as a generalist.

---

## Industry Domination Framework

For each industry, RCS builds a complete authority stack:

```
Industry Authority Stack
  ├─ 1. Dedicated landing page (SEO hub)
  ├─ 2. Supporting blog articles (3 minimum)
  ├─ 3. Industry-specific lead magnet (PDF guide)
  ├─ 4. Social content series (carousels + reels)
  ├─ 5. Email sequence (industry-specific nurture)
  └─ 6. Case study (once a project is completed)
```

The landing page is the hub. Everything else links back to it and feeds traffic into it.

## Industry 1 — Dental

**Landing page:** `/industries/dental-website-design/`
**Primary keyword:** `dental website design`
**H1:** `Dental Website Design That Converts Visitors Into New Patients`

**Core pain points:**
- Losing new patient inquiries to competitors with better websites
- Website does not reflect the quality of care delivered
- Patients can't easily book appointments online
- Google rankings are poor despite having a website
- Not HIPAA-aligned in digital presentation

**Key features to emphasize:**
- Online appointment booking integration
- Before/after patient showcase
- Staff and practice trust signals
- Fast load speed (patients leave slow sites)
- Mobile-first (most dental searches are mobile)
- Google Business Profile alignment

**Blog articles (minimum 3):**
1. `"5 Features Every Dental Website Must Have to Convert New Patients"`
2. `"How to Get More Patients From Your Dental Website"`
3. `"Dental Website Design: What Top Practices Do Differently"`

**Lead magnet:** `The Dental Practice Website Guide`
**Social angle:** Before/after dental website reveals. `"Your dental website should be booking appointments while you sleep."`

## Industry Authority Build Sequence

Do not attempt all 10 industries simultaneously. Build one industry authority stack at a time.

**Recommended priority order:**
1. Dental (largest search volume, highest project value)
2. Church (unique niche, less agency competition)
3. Restaurant (high search volume, visual content strength)
4. Local Business (broadest reach, local SEO support)
5. Real Estate (high project value)
6. Healthcare (high trust requirement, premium fees)
7. Startup (strong LinkedIn audience)
8. Fitness (Instagram / TikTok content opportunity)
9. Construction (underserved niche)
10. Beauty (high booking intent)

**Build timeline per industry:** 4–6 weeks per full industry authority stack (landing page + 3 blog articles + lead magnet + social content batch).
