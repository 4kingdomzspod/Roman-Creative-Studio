# Case Study System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the case study production system for Roman Creative Studio. Every completed project becomes a marketing asset. A case study is not a project archive — it is a conversion tool. It removes doubt, demonstrates competence, and provides social proof for the exact type of client RCS wants to attract next.

**Rule: Every project must become marketing content. No exceptions.**

---

## Case Study Philosophy

> **A prospect does not buy your process. They buy the evidence that your process works for someone like them.**

The most effective case studies:
- Feature a client in the same industry as the prospect reading it
- Include specific, measurable outcomes (not vague praise)
- Tell a before/after story that mirrors the prospect's current situation
- End with a clear invitation to experience the same results

---

## Case Study Structure (6 Sections)

Every RCS case study follows this structure. The structure is non-negotiable — it is designed to move a skeptical reader from doubt to confidence.

---

### Section 1 — The Headline
**Purpose:** Immediately communicate the result so the reader knows this case study is worth reading.

**Formula:**
```
How [Client Type] [Achieved Specific Result] With a [Service Type] by Roman Creative Studio
```

**Examples:**
- `"How a Houston Dental Practice Increased New Patient Inquiries by 40% After a Website Redesign"`
- `"How [Church Name] Built an Online Presence That Now Reaches 300+ Weekly Visitors"`
- `"From Zero Online Presence to Fully Booked: How [Restaurant Name] Transformed Their Digital Storefront"`

**Rules:**
- The headline must include a specific result or outcome where available
- Never use vague headlines (`"Client Spotlight: [Client Name]"`)
- If exact metrics are not available, use directional language (`"significantly more inquiries"`, `"doubled their online bookings"`)

---

### Section 2 — The Client Snapshot
**Purpose:** Help the reader quickly identify if this client is like them.

**Format (short metadata block):**
```
Client:      [Business name or "[City] Dental Practice" if anonymized]
Industry:    [Dental / Church / Restaurant / etc.]
Service:     [Website Redesign / Brand Identity + Website / SEO Optimization / etc.]
Timeline:    [e.g., "Completed in 6 weeks"]
Result:      [One-line outcome summary]
```

---

### Section 3 — The Challenge (Before)
**Purpose:** Describe the client's situation before engaging RCS. The prospect should read this section and think: `"That's exactly my situation."`

**What to cover:**
- What was wrong with the existing website (or what was missing entirely)
- What problem it was causing the business (lost leads, poor first impressions, no SEO presence)
- Why previous attempts to fix it had not worked (template, cheap freelancer, outdated DIY site)
- The emotional or business cost of the problem (missing revenue, professional embarrassment, losing patients to competitors)

**Length:** 150–250 words

**Tone:** Factual, empathetic, specific. Not exaggerated. Not editorial.

**Example opening:**
> `"[Practice name] had been serving patients in [city] for 12 years. Their clinical reputation was excellent. But their website told a different story — a template site last updated in 2019, no online booking, and a homepage that ranked for nothing. When new patients searched for a dentist nearby, they weren't finding [practice name]. They were finding competitors."`

---

### Section 4 — The Solution (What RCS Built)
**Purpose:** Describe the strategy and execution. Show that RCS applied a thoughtful process, not a template.

**What to cover:**
- The strategy phase: what RCS diagnosed and why
- The design decisions and why they were made
- The specific features built and their purpose
- The SEO and conversion architecture implemented
- Any challenges encountered and how they were resolved

**Length:** 250–400 words

**Include:**
- 2–3 full-resolution screenshots (desktop and mobile)
- Before/after comparison image (if applicable and approved by client)
- Specific technical decisions with plain-English explanations

**Tone:** Expert, precise, confident. Show the thinking behind the decisions.

**Example:**
> `"The strategy phase revealed three core problems: no clear conversion path, no mobile optimization, and no SEO foundation. We rebuilt the information architecture first — mapping the patient's decision journey from awareness to booking appointment. The new homepage was designed with one primary goal: get visitors to book an appointment or call the practice within 30 seconds of arriving."`

---

### Section 5 — The Results (After)
**Purpose:** Demonstrate quantifiable, specific outcomes. This section converts skeptical readers.

**Rules:**
- Only publish metrics with explicit client permission and verification
- Prefer specific numbers: `"Increased monthly inquiries from 4 to 17"` over `"Significantly more inquiries"`
- If specific numbers are not available, use verified directional outcomes with client attribution
- Do NOT fabricate or estimate metrics

**Result types to capture (at 30 and 90 days post-launch):**
- Change in inquiry volume (phone calls, contact form submissions)
- Change in appointment bookings
- Google ranking improvements (specific keywords)
- Page speed improvement (Core Web Vitals before/after)
- Traffic increase (Google Analytics comparison)
- Bounce rate improvement
- Client quote (specific to the outcome)

**Format:**
```
Results at 90 Days:
• New patient inquiries: +40% vs. prior 90-day period
• Google ranking: #1 for "dentist [city]" (previously page 3)
• Mobile page speed: 34 → 91 (PageSpeed Insights)
• Bounce rate: 74% → 48%
```

**Client quote (required):**
> `"I wasn't sure a website redesign would make a real difference. Within 3 weeks of launch I had booked 3 new patients who found us through Google. The investment paid for itself."`
> — [Dr. Name], [Practice Name]

---

### Section 6 — The CTA
**Purpose:** Convert the reader from impressed visitor to active lead.

**Standard CTA block:**
```
## Ready to Build a [Industry] Website That Gets Results?

If you're a [dental practice / church / restaurant / etc.] looking for a website
that actually generates [patients / visitors / reservations / leads], we'd like
to hear about your project.

[Book a Free Discovery Call →]
```

**CTA rules:**
- Industry-specific language in the CTA (mirrors the case study subject)
- One primary button: `"Book a Free Discovery Call"`
- One secondary text link: `"View more [industry] projects →"` (links to filtered portfolio)

## Case Study Production Timeline

| Step | Timing | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Project launch day | Request metrics baseline (Google Analytics screenshot, current rankings) |
| 2 | 30 days post-launch | First results check-in (see `ReferralExperienceSystem.md`) |
| 3 | 60–90 days post-launch | Collect final metrics (traffic, inquiries, rankings) |
| 4 | 90 days post-launch | Write full case study draft |
| 5 | Draft complete | Send to client for review and approval |
| 6 | Client approval received | Publish on website + distribute across all channels |

---

## Case Study Consent Requirements

- Written consent required before publishing any case study (see `ReferralExperienceSystem.md` for consent email template)
- Client must approve all metrics published
- Client must approve all screenshots and visuals used
- Offer anonymization option if client prefers privacy (e.g., `"[City] Dental Practice"` instead of the practice name)
- Client may request removal at any time; case study is taken down within 48 hours

---

## Case Study Distribution Plan

Once published, every case study is distributed through:

| Channel | Format | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Website portfolio page | Full case study page | Publish day |
| Blog | Case study breakdown article | Publish day |
| Email newsletter | Case study summary + link | Next Thursday send |
| LinkedIn | Long-form case study post (condensed) | Publish day or day after |
| Instagram | Before/after carousel (6–8 slides) | Within 3 days |
| TikTok / Reel | Project reveal short video | Within 5 days |
| Industry page | Testimonial + result callout + link | Within 1 week |

**Rule:** A case study that sits on the portfolio page and nowhere else is a wasted asset. Every case study gets a full distribution cycle.
