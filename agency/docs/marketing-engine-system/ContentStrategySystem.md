# Content Strategy System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the structured content pillar framework for Roman Creative Studio. Every piece of content RCS publishes — blog post, social caption, email, video, lead magnet — belongs to a defined pillar with a defined purpose and a defined conversion path.

Content without structure is noise. Content with structure compounds into authority.

---

## The Four Content Pillars

### Pillar 1 — Education
**Purpose:** Teach business owners what they do not know about web design, SEO, and digital presence. Build trust before a sales conversation begins.

**Core principle:** Give real value. Do not withhold information to protect services. The business owner who learns from RCS content becomes the business owner who hires RCS.

**Content types:**
- How-to blog articles (`"How to Improve Your Dental Website Conversion Rate"`)
- Step-by-step guides (`"The 7-Step Process for Launching a New Business Website"`)
- Explainer videos and YouTube Shorts
- LinkedIn long-form posts
- Email educational sequences
- Industry-specific website audits (lead magnet format)

**Frequency:** 2× per week (blog + social)

**Conversion path:** Educational content → Internal link to service page or industry page → CTA to book Discovery Call

---

### Pillar 2 — Authority
**Purpose:** Demonstrate RCS expertise, process, and thinking. Show that RCS operates at a higher level than competitors. Attract premium clients who recognize and value expertise.

**Core principle:** Authority is demonstrated through specificity. Generic opinions do not build authority. Specific, researched, opinionated content does.

**Content types:**
- Opinion pieces (`"Why Most Small Business Websites Fail to Generate Leads"`)
- Process breakdowns (`"How RCS Approaches a Website Redesign"`)
- Industry trend analysis
- Behind-the-scenes design thinking
- LinkedIn thought leadership posts
- Instagram carousel: design principles, process insights
- Podcast appearances / guest articles (when applicable)

**Frequency:** 1× per week (high-quality, longer-form)

**Conversion path:** Authority content → Link to portfolio or case study → CTA to book Discovery Call

---

### Pillar 3 — Proof
**Purpose:** Remove doubt. Show real results for real clients. Every case study, testimonial, and before/after is a purchase accelerator.

**Core principle:** Generic testimonials (`"Great to work with!"`). are worthless. Specific, outcome-based proof (`"Booked 3 new patients in the first week after launch"`) converts.

**Content types:**
- Full case study blog posts
- Case study social carousels (before/after visuals)
- Client testimonial feature posts
- Metrics-driven result callouts
- Portfolio showcase posts
- Project launch announcement posts
- Email: client success stories

**Frequency:** 1× per completed project (minimum); 1× per week if portfolio is large enough

**Conversion path:** Proof content → Link to full case study → CTA to start a project

---

### Pillar 4 — Conversion
**Purpose:** Directly invite qualified visitors to take the next step. Every conversion piece has one job: book a Discovery Call or capture an email address.

**Core principle:** Conversion content is not pushy. It is clear, specific, and confident. It frames the offer in terms of the client's outcome, not RCS's services.

**Content types:**
- Service offer posts (`"We are now accepting new website projects for Q3"`)
- Free audit offers
- Lead magnet promotion posts
- Limited availability announcements
- Direct CTA emails
- `"Book a Free Discovery Call"` landing page campaigns
- Industry-specific offer posts (`"Is your dental website costing you patients?"` + audit CTA)

**Frequency:** 1× per week (rotated across platforms)

**Conversion path:** Conversion content → Direct link to Discovery Call booking page

## Content Repurposing Workflow

Every blog article spawns a minimum of 5 additional content pieces:

```
Blog Article (1,500 words)
  └─ LinkedIn long-form post (condensed, first-person)
  └─ Instagram carousel (key points visualized, 6–8 slides)
  └─ TikTok / Reel script (hook + 3 points + CTA, 60 sec)
  └─ Email newsletter section (summary + link to full article)
  └─ SEO snippet (meta description repurposed as social caption)
```

Full repurposing system documented in `ContentRepurposingEngine.md`.

## Content Quality Rules

1. **One idea per piece.** Do not dilute content with multiple messages. One article, one argument. One post, one point.
2. **Specific over generic.** `"Dental websites need faster load times"` is better than `"Websites should be fast."`
3. **Outcome language.** Write in terms of what the client gains, not what RCS does.
4. **No filler.** Every paragraph earns its place. Remove any sentence that restates what the previous sentence already said.
5. **Every piece has a next step.** No content is published without a CTA, internal link, or lead magnet reference.
6. **Brand voice always.** Review against the SalesMessagingSystem tone table before publishing. No apologetic, vague, or desperate language.

---

## Conversion Integration Rules

- Every blog post includes at least 2 internal links to service or industry pages
- Every blog post ends with a CTA section linking to the Discovery Call booking page
- Every social post links to a blog article, service page, or lead magnet in the bio/caption
- Every email includes a primary CTA (book Discovery Call) and a secondary CTA (read the blog / download the guide)
- Lead magnets are always gated behind an email capture form

## Content Prohibited List

- Posting for the sake of posting
- Inspirational quotes with no marketing relevance
- `"Happy Monday!"` or similar non-content filler
- Generic design tips with no RCS positioning
- Competitor criticism (positions RCS as insecure)
- Posts without a defined conversion path
- Anything that sounds like it came from a generic agency template
