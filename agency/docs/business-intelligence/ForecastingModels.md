## Purpose

Document the forecasting models for Roman Creative Studio across revenue, leads, capacity, hiring, projects, cash flow, recurring revenue, service demand, and industry expansion.

---

## Forecasting Principles

1. **Bottom-up, not top-down.** Start with real pipeline data.
2. **Three scenarios always.** Conservative, Expected, Optimistic.
3. **Explicit assumptions.** Every model documents what it assumes.
4. **Monthly recalibration.** Actuals entered monthly; model adjusted if off >15%.
5. **No fabricated data.** Starting values are zero or stated assumptions.
