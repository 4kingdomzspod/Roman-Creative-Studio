# Kickoff System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 6, Document 3

---

### Why Kickoff Defines Everything

The kickoff phase is the most important phase of the project — not the design, not the launch. What happens in the first 5–7 days after signing determines:

- Whether the client feels confident in their decision
- Whether RCS has the information needed to do great work
- Whether the timeline is realistic and shared
- Whether the relationship is professional or chaotic
- Whether problems appear at the end or get prevented at the beginning

A poorly run kickoff creates ambiguity that compounds into scope creep, revision loops, timeline overruns, and unhappy clients. A well-run kickoff creates alignment that makes the rest of the project feel smooth.

### 1.2 Onboarding Questionnaire (Sent Same Day as Deposit)

The questionnaire is the intelligence-gathering instrument for the entire project. It must be completed before the kickoff call.

**Platform:** Typeform, Notion form, or Google Form
**Deadline:** Requested within 48 hours of receiving it
**If not received:** Follow up once at 48 hours, once at 72 hours, then hold kickoff until received

**Questionnaire sections** (per Phase 5 ClientOnboardingTransition, Section 3, Step 3):
- Business Foundation
- Goals
- Brand & Style
- Content & Assets
- Technical
- Timeline

## Section 3 — Kickoff Call Structure

**Duration:** 60 minutes
**Format:** Video call (camera on both parties)
**Prerequisite:** Questionnaire completed before the call

### Kickoff Call Agenda

```
1. WELCOME & FRAMING (5 min)
   - Thank them for the trust
   - Set the tone: structured, collaborative, no surprises
   - Confirm the agenda

2. QUESTIONNAIRE REVIEW (15 min)
   - Walk through answers together
   - Clarify anything vague or incomplete
   - Dig deeper on goals and audience
   - Surface any hidden requirements

3. SCOPE & TIMELINE WALK-THROUGH (10 min)
   - Confirm the page list from the contract
   - Walk through each phase of the project (by name + dates)
   - Identify any hard deadlines on their side

4. PROCESS EXPLANATION (10 min)
   - How approvals work (approval gates, not open-ended)
   - How revisions work (consolidated rounds, not scattered messages)
   - How communication works (email for decisions, call for complex topics)
   - Client responsibility: timely feedback keeps the project on track

5. CONTENT & ASSET PLAN (10 min)
   - Review asset checklist together
   - Confirm what's available and what's missing
   - Set hard deadlines for asset delivery (specific dates, not "soon")
   - Photography plan: existing / stock / sourcing

6. QUESTIONS (10 min)
   - Open floor for any questions about the process
   - Clarify anything that seems unclear
```

## Section 5 — Kickoff Failure Prevention

### Common Kickoff Failures and How to Prevent Them

| Failure | Root Cause | Prevention |
|---|---|---|
| Client doesn't send assets | Didn't understand the urgency | Set hard deadline dates in writing; hold project until received |
| Client expects constant updates | Unclear communication cadence | Explain update schedule explicitly in kickoff call |
| Scope expansion after kickoff | Didn't confirm page list carefully | Review page list line by line; get written confirmation |
| Multiple reviewers giving conflicting feedback | No single point of contact established | Name one POC in kickoff; document it |
| Client disappears during project | No urgency set | Emphasize: "your delays shift the timeline" |
| Unclear photography plan | Skipped in kickoff | Make photography plan a required kickoff output |
