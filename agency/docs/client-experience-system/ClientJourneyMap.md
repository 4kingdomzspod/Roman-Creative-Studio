# Full Client Journey Map
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 6, Document 2

---

### Purpose

The Client Journey Map is the complete lifecycle of every RCS client relationship — from the first inquiry to the moment they refer a colleague. Every stage is defined with a clear purpose, entry point, exit criteria, deliverables, and client communication requirement.

No stage is skipped. No stage is improvised.

---

## The 17-Stage Client Journey

---

### Stage 1: Inquiry / Lead

**Purpose:** First contact — prospect becomes an identified lead

**Entry criteria:** Prospect submits contact form, books a call, or initiates contact via LinkedIn/email

**Exit criteria:** Lead is qualified or disqualified (per Phase 5 ClientQualificationSystem)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Lead logged with source, business name, industry
- [ ] Initial response sent within 4 business hours
- [ ] Discovery call scheduled (if qualified)

**Client communication:**
```
Subject: Thanks for reaching out — let's connect

Hi [Name],

Thanks for getting in touch. I'd love to learn about your business
and what you're looking to accomplish.

Here's a link to book a 30-minute discovery call: [Calendly]

Looking forward to it.

Alexander, Roman Creative Studio
```

---

### Stage 2: Discovery Call

**Purpose:** Diagnose the problem, assess fit, establish trust, move toward proposal

**Entry criteria:** Lead is minimally qualified (real business, has a genuine problem)

**Exit criteria:** Call completed, decision made to proceed or decline

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Discovery call completed
- [ ] Call notes documented (per Phase 5 DiscoveryCallFramework)
- [ ] Qualification score assessed
- [ ] Next step defined (proposal / decline / hold)

**Client communication:** Post-call summary email (see Phase 5 FollowUpSystem)

---

### Stage 3: Proposal Sent

**Purpose:** Present the solution, investment, and process for client decision

**Entry criteria:** Prospect is qualified, budget-aligned, DM on call

**Exit criteria:** Proposal reviewed together on review call

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Proposal built (per Phase 5 ProposalSystem)
- [ ] Proposal emailed 24–48 hours before review call
- [ ] Review call completed
- [ ] Objections handled

**Client communication:** Proposal review call + email delivery

---

### Stage 4: Contract Signed

**Purpose:** Legal confirmation of scope, investment, timeline, and terms

**Entry criteria:** Prospect has verbally agreed to move forward

**Exit criteria:** Signed contract received and deposit paid

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Project agreement sent via DocuSign / PandaDoc
- [ ] Signed copy filed
- [ ] Deposit invoice sent

**Client communication:** Contract email with clear signing instructions

---

### Stage 5: Payment Received

**Purpose:** Financial commitment confirmed — project becomes active

**Entry criteria:** Signed contract on file

**Exit criteria:** Deposit confirmed, project officially started

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Payment confirmation sent within 1 hour
- [ ] Receipt emailed
- [ ] Client added to project management system
- [ ] Onboarding questionnaire sent

**Client communication:** Payment confirmation + welcome sequence (see KickoffSystem)

---

### Stage 6: Kickoff

**Purpose:** Align on goals, timeline, process, expectations, and content requirements

**Entry criteria:** Deposit paid, questionnaire sent

**Exit criteria:** Kickoff call completed, project timeline delivered in writing

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Onboarding questionnaire completed by client
- [ ] Kickoff call completed (60 min)
- [ ] Project timeline sent within 24 hours of kickoff call
- [ ] Communication channel confirmed
- [ ] Asset collection list sent

**Client communication:** Welcome email + kickoff meeting + written timeline

---

### Stage 7: Research Phase

**Purpose:** Gather competitive intelligence, audience understanding, and content assets

**Entry criteria:** Kickoff call completed, questionnaire received

**Exit criteria:** Research document complete, all required assets collected

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Competitor website audit (top 3 competitors)
- [ ] Audience profile notes
- [ ] SEO keyword research (primary + secondary)
- [ ] Content assets collected (logos, photos, existing copy)
- [ ] Technology requirements confirmed

**Client communication:** Asset collection requests (with hard deadlines)

---

### Stage 8: Strategy Phase

**Purpose:** Define the site architecture, content strategy, and conversion plan before design begins

**Entry criteria:** Research complete, assets received

**Exit criteria:** Strategy document approved by client

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Site map (all pages, hierarchy)
- [ ] Conversion flow document (per Phase 4)
- [ ] SEO keyword mapping (page-by-page)
- [ ] Content outline per page
- [ ] Strategy document sent to client for approval

**Client communication:** Strategy document delivery email + approval request

```
Subject: [Business Name] — Strategy Document for Review

Hi [Name],

The strategy document for your project is ready for review.

This covers the site architecture, page-by-page content plan,
and the conversion strategy we discussed in kickoff.

Please review and let me know your thoughts by [date].
I'll need your approval to begin wireframes.

Alexander
```

---

### Stage 9: Design Phase

**Purpose:** Build the visual system and page layouts based on approved strategy

**Entry criteria:** Strategy document approved

**Exit criteria:** Design mockups approved by client

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Wireframes (low-fidelity structure) — approved
- [ ] High-fidelity design mockups (homepage + key pages)
- [ ] Design review call with client
- [ ] Up to 2 rounds of revisions
- [ ] Final design approval in writing

**Client communication:** Design delivery email + scheduled review call

---

### Stage 10: Development Phase

**Purpose:** Build the approved design into a functional website

**Entry criteria:** Final design approved in writing

**Exit criteria:** Staging site ready for client review

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] All pages built in code
- [ ] Mobile/tablet/desktop responsiveness implemented
- [ ] Forms functional and tested
- [ ] SEO structure implemented (titles, metas, H1s, schema)
- [ ] Analytics setup (GA4, Search Console)
- [ ] Staging link sent to client

**Client communication:** Development milestone email when staging site is ready

---

### Stage 11: QA & Testing

**Purpose:** Identify and fix all issues before the client sees the staging site

**Entry criteria:** Development complete

**Exit criteria:** Internal QA checklist 100% passed

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Full QA checklist completed (see QASystem.md)
- [ ] All bugs documented and resolved
- [ ] Cross-browser testing completed
- [ ] Performance targets confirmed
- [ ] Accessibility audit passed

**Client communication:** None visible — internal phase only. Client receives staging site only after QA passes.

---

### Stage 12: Revisions Phase

**Purpose:** Incorporate client feedback from staging review

**Entry criteria:** Staging site delivered to client, feedback received

**Exit criteria:** All feedback addressed, client approves staging for launch

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Client feedback collected (consolidated, per RevisionSystem)
- [ ] Revisions implemented within agreed timeframe
- [ ] Updated staging site delivered
- [ ] Final approval received in writing

**Client communication:** Revision confirmation email + revised staging delivery

---

### Stage 13: Final Approval

**Purpose:** Get explicit written sign-off before launch — no launch without this

**Entry criteria:** All revisions complete, client has reviewed updated staging

**Exit criteria:** Written approval email or signed approval form received

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Formal approval email requested
- [ ] Approval documented and filed
- [ ] Launch checklist initiated

**Client communication:**
```
Subject: Ready for Launch — [Business Name] Website

Hi [Name],

The website is ready to launch. All revisions have been implemented
and the staging site reflects your final approval feedback.

Before I proceed with launch, I need your formal sign-off.

Please reply with "Approved to launch" or let me know if there's
anything else you'd like to review.

Once I receive approval, I'll proceed with launch within 24 hours.

Alexander
```

---

### Stage 14: Launch

**Purpose:** Deploy the website to the live domain

**Entry criteria:** Written final approval received

**Exit criteria:** Live site confirmed functional, all post-launch checks passed

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Full launch checklist completed (see LaunchSystem.md)
- [ ] DNS migration / deployment completed
- [ ] Post-launch testing (all pages, forms, speed)
- [ ] Go-live confirmation sent to client

**Client communication:** Go-live confirmation email with key details

---

### Stage 15: Training / Handover

**Purpose:** Equip the client to manage their website independently

**Entry criteria:** Site live and confirmed functional

**Exit criteria:** Training completed, client has login credentials and support contact

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Training session scheduled and completed (60 min)
- [ ] Training recording sent (if screen-recorded)
- [ ] Quick reference guide delivered
- [ ] Login credentials transferred securely
- [ ] Support terms explained

**Client communication:** Training session + post-training email with reference materials

---

### Stage 16: Maintenance / Retention

**Purpose:** Convert the project client into a long-term recurring revenue relationship

**Entry criteria:** Training completed, 30-day support window active

**Exit criteria:** Client on a Care Plan or officially closed (with future check-in scheduled)

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] 30-day post-launch check-in completed
- [ ] Care Plan presented (see MaintenanceRetentionSystem.md)
- [ ] Monthly reporting begins (if on Care Plan)
- [ ] Quarterly strategy call scheduled (if on growth plan)

**Client communication:** 30-day check-in + Care Plan offer

---

### Stage 17: Referral Stage

**Purpose:** Leverage peak satisfaction to generate qualified referrals and social proof

**Entry criteria:** Client has seen measurable results (typically 30–90 days post-launch)

**Exit criteria:** Testimonial collected, referral ask made, case study initiated

**Deliverables:**
- [ ] Satisfaction check-in completed
- [ ] Testimonial requested and received
- [ ] Referral ask made (see Phase 5 ReferralSystem)
- [ ] Case study drafted (if client consents)

**Client communication:** Success check-in + testimonial request (see ReferralExperienceSystem.md)
