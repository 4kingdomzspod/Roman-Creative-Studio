## Overview

This document describes the 10-year vision for Roman Creative Studio — where we are going, what we are building, and who we are becoming by 2035. It is aspirational, not guaranteed. Its purpose is to give direction to annual planning, hiring decisions, and product investments.

**Guiding Question:** What does Roman Creative Studio look like at peak impact?

**Core Belief:** A creative agency that only sells time is perpetually limited. The future of RCS is a creative platform that combines premium services, scalable products, and community — generating impact and revenue beyond what any single team's hours can produce.

---

## 10-Year Vision Statement

By 2035, Roman Creative Studio is recognized as the premier creative technology platform for service businesses in America. We help thousands of businesses grow through great design, effective SEO, powerful AI tools, and the education needed to sustain their digital presence long-term.

We have evolved from a web design agency into a full creative ecosystem:
- **Agency:** Premium web design, brand strategy, and growth services for established businesses
- **Products:** A library of digital tools, templates, and AI assistants trusted by 10,000+ designers and business owners
- **Education:** Courses and programs that have trained 5,000+ designers and agency owners
- **Community:** 2,000+ active members building creative businesses together
- **SaaS:** 2–3 profitable software products serving agency and small business markets

## Strategic Pillars for 2035

### Pillar 1: Brand Authority
RCS must be recognized as a thought leader in creative business building, not just a vendor. This requires consistent content, speaking, publishing, and education over the full 10 years.

**Investments that build authority:**
- Consistent LinkedIn content
- YouTube channel (Year 2+)
- Podcast (Year 3+)
- Published book or major guide (Year 4–5)
- Speaking at design and business conferences

### Pillar 2: Recurring Revenue Architecture
Every year, the percentage of RCS revenue that recurs (MRR) must increase. By 2030, target: >60% of revenue is recurring.

**MRR Sources by 2030:**
- Care Plans: $10,000–$20,000/mo
- Community memberships: $20,000–$40,000/mo
- SaaS subscriptions: $20,000–$40,000/mo
- Course subscriptions: $5,000–$10,000/mo

### Pillar 3: Systems Over People
RCS must be able to operate without depending on any single person — including the founder. This means:
- Every process is documented
- Every decision has a decision framework
- Every role has a successor
- Technology automates what humans shouldn't have to do manually

### Pillar 4: Design Excellence
The agency must never sacrifice quality. As we scale, quality is maintained through:
- Clear design standards
- Rigorous QA process
- Training and development investment
- Turning away clients who are not a fit

### Pillar 5: Community as Core
The RCS community is not a feature — it is a core asset. Community creates:
- Retention (members stay for the people, not just the content)
- Referrals (warm recommendations from peers)
- Product feedback (build what the community needs)
- Alumni network (members who succeed become ambassadors)

This document is reviewed every January as part of annual planning:
1. Review the year's milestones — what was achieved vs. planned

3. Adjust Years 3–5 based on market and business evolution
4. Keep Years 6–10 as directional vision, not detailed plans
5. Update the financial projections based on actual trajectory
6. Recommit to the 5 strategic pillars
