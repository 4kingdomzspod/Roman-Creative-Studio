## Overview

The RCS Course Platform delivers high-quality education for web designers, agency owners, and creative entrepreneurs. These courses encode our methodology, our frameworks, and our hard-won expertise into structured learning experiences that generate passive income and establish RCS as a thought leader.

**Strategic Rationale:** Courses validate expertise, build trust with prospective clients, and create revenue that doesn't require trading time for money. A student who completes an RCS course is a warm lead for RCS services.

**Platform Philosophy:** Depth over breadth. One excellent course beats ten mediocre ones. We will not launch a course until we know we can deliver the best version in its category.

## Platform Architecture

### Year 1: Use Existing Platform
Launch on **Teachable** or **Kajabi** to avoid platform build costs:
- Teachable: $119/mo (no transaction fees on Pro)
- Kajabi: $149/mo (includes email marketing, landing pages)

**Recommendation:** Teachable for Year 1 (simpler, lower cost, still professional)

### Year 2: Evaluate Custom Platform
If student count exceeds 500 or revenue exceeds $100k/year, evaluate custom build:
```
Next.js 14 (frontend + API)
Supabase (auth, database, storage)
Mux (video hosting — $0.015/min viewed)
Stripe (payments + subscriptions)
Resend (transactional email)
Vercel (deployment)
```

**Custom Platform Build Cost Estimate:** $15,000–25,000 or 6–8 weeks internal build  
**Break-even vs. Teachable:** ~Month 30 at current growth

## Course Production Standards

### Video Production
- Minimum resolution: 1080p
- Audio: External microphone required (Shure MV7 or equivalent)
- Background: Clean, branded RCS background or neutral environment
- Captions: Required on all videos (auto-generated + human-reviewed)
- Length: 8–15 minutes per lesson (optimal completion rate)
- Intro/Outro: Branded RCS sequence (5 seconds each)

### Written Materials
- Workbooks: Designed in Figma, exported as interactive PDF
- Templates: Functional formats (Notion, spreadsheets, HTML files)
- Checklists: Printable, one item per line, checkbox on left

### Technical Requirements
- All code examples: Tested in Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge
- All links: Verified on publish
- Downloads: Virus scanned, hosted in Supabase Storage
- Accessibility: Captions + transcript for all videos; image descriptions

## Launch Strategy

### Pre-Launch (6 weeks before)
- Build email waitlist (target: 200+ signups before launch)
- Content marketing: Free lesson or lead magnet related to course
- LinkedIn content series on course topic
- Launch partner list (affiliates who get 30% commission)

### Launch Week
- Day 1: Email announcement to full list
- Day 2–3: Case study / testimonial content
- Day 4: FAQ / objection handling
- Day 5: "Last chance" + bonus reminder
- Day 7: Cart close (for time-limited launches)

### Post-Launch
- Collect testimonials from first 20 students
- Student success stories for social proof
- Gather feedback for v2 curriculum update
- Affiliate recruitment from satisfied students
