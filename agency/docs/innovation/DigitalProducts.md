## Product Category 1: Website Templates

### Overview

**Audience:** Web designers, DIY small business owners  
**Format:** Figma (design) + HTML/CSS (static) + Next.js (optional)  
**Price:** $79–$149  
**Pages:** Home, About, Services, Gallery, Contact, FAQ, Testimonials  
**Key features:** Schema markup included, WCAG AA compliant, PageSpeed-optimized  
**Marketplace:** Gumroad, Creative Market, ThemeForest

**Scalability:** Industry variants (WEB-02 through WEB-10) reuse same base

## Distribution Strategy

### Primary Channels

| Channel | Product Type | Commission | Notes |
|---------|-------------|------------|-------|
| Gumroad | All digital | 10% | Best for solopreneurs; simple setup |
| Lemon Squeezy | All digital | 5% + Stripe fees | Better checkout UX; built-in affiliates |
| Creative Market | Design assets | 30% | High-traffic design audience |
| Figma Community | Figma templates | 0% (free) | Traffic driver; leads to paid |
| Envato/ThemeForest | Web templates | 33–55% | High volume, lower margin |
| Own website | All digital | 0% (Stripe only) | Best margin; requires SEO/traffic |

### Launch Sequence (for each product)
1. List on Gumroad first (fastest setup)
2. Cross-post to Figma Community (if design asset)
3. Add to own website resource page
4. Announce to email list
5. Publish blog post demonstrating product
6. Add to Creative Market (after 30 days of sales data)
