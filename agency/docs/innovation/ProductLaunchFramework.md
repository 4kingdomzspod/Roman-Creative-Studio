## Overview

The RCS Product Launch Framework is the repeatable system for taking a validated product from "ready to build" to "people are buying it." It covers everything from pre-launch positioning to post-launch optimization.

**Principle:** A mediocre product launched well outperforms an excellent product launched poorly. We invest equally in launch execution as we do in product quality.

---

## Launch Types

### Type A: Soft Launch
**When:** Product is new, audience is small, or we want to gather feedback before scaling
**Goal:** Get first 10–50 paying customers; gather testimonials; validate pricing
**Effort:** Low (email list + social posts)
**Risk:** Low

### Type B: Standard Launch
**When:** Product is validated, audience exists, we're ready to drive volume
**Goal:** 50–200 sales in launch week
**Effort:** Medium (full content campaign + email sequence)
**Risk:** Medium

### Type C: Flagship Launch
**When:** Major product (cohort, SaaS, book), large audience, significant revenue target
**Goal:** $10,000+ in launch week
**Effort:** High (full campaign, affiliates, paid ads)
**Risk:** Higher (time and money invested)

## Pre-Launch Phase (6–8 Weeks Before)

### Week 8: Positioning & Messaging
- [ ] Define target customer avatar (1 specific person, not a demographic)
- [ ] Define the primary problem this product solves
- [ ] Write the value proposition (one sentence)
- [ ] Name the product and define the tagline
- [ ] Define pricing and whether there's an early-bird discount
- [ ] Define launch goal (number of units / revenue)

### Week 7: Assets Created
- [ ] Product sales page (built in Webflow or Next.js)
- [ ] Product demo video or screenshots
- [ ] Email sequence: pre-launch (3–5 emails)
- [ ] Social content calendar for launch week
- [ ] Affiliate assets (if applicable): copy, images, unique link

### Week 6: Audience Building Begins
- [ ] Publish first piece of content related to product topic
- [ ] Create waitlist page (Mailerlite form embedded)
- [ ] Launch LinkedIn content series (3 posts/week for 4 weeks)
- [ ] Reach out to potential affiliate partners
- [ ] Begin collecting social proof (testimonials, screenshots, early feedback)

### Week 4–5: Warm-Up
- [ ] Email subscribers a preview or free resource related to product
- [ ] Behind-the-scenes content: show the product being built/finished
- [ ] Gather and format testimonials from beta users
- [ ] Confirm all payment and delivery systems tested end-to-end
- [ ] Set up analytics: Google Analytics event tracking + conversion goal

### Week 2–3: Final Prep
- [ ] Email waitlist: launch date announcement
- [ ] Social posts: countdown and teaser content
- [ ] Confirm affiliates have their materials and are ready
- [ ] Stress-test checkout and delivery (test purchase + refund)
- [ ] Prepare post-launch email sequence (2–3 emails for non-buyers)

### Week 1: Launch Week Prep
- [ ] All emails scheduled in MailerLite
- [ ] All social posts scheduled
- [ ] Team briefed: who handles what during launch week
- [ ] Emergency runbook: what to do if checkout breaks, if email fails, if product has an error

## Launch Week Execution

### Day 1 (Monday): Launch Day
- [ ] 8:00 AM: Launch email to full list ("It's here!")
- [ ] 9:00 AM: LinkedIn post (product announcement)
- [ ] Monitor: Checkout, payment processing, delivery
- [ ] Respond to all questions and feedback same-day
- [ ] End of day: Sales count, conversion rate, top traffic source

### Day 2–3: Momentum
- [ ] Email segment: Opened but didn't buy — "Have a question?" email
- [ ] Social: Behind-the-scenes or testimonial content
- [ ] Reply to every comment and DM publicly

### Day 4–5: Mid-Week Push
- [ ] Email: Case study or detailed breakdown of what's inside
- [ ] Social: FAQ post ("What you've been asking about [product]")
- [ ] Check affiliate performance; encourage active affiliates

### Day 6: Last Chance (if time-limited launch)
- [ ] Email: "Closes [tomorrow/tonight]" with reminder of what they get
- [ ] Social: Urgency post

### Day 7 (Sunday): Close or Transition
- [ ] For time-limited: Close cart at midnight; send "cart closed" email
- [ ] For evergreen: Transition to standard sales page
- [ ] End-of-week summary: Total sales, revenue, conversion rate, top channels

## Post-Launch Phase (Weeks 2–4)

### Immediate (Days 1–3 After Launch)
- [ ] Send "thank you" sequence to buyers (3 emails: thank you + access + quick win)
- [ ] Send "we missed you" sequence to non-buyers (2 emails + waitlist for next cohort/version)
- [ ] Collect early feedback: send quick survey to buyers after Day 3
- [ ] Pay affiliates within 7 days

### Week 2–4: Optimization
- [ ] Compile all customer feedback into categories
- [ ] Identify top 3 improvements for v1.1
- [ ] Identify top 3 objections that prevented purchase
- [ ] Update sales page based on learnings
- [ ] Identify 2–3 testimonials for permanent inclusion on sales page

### Month 1 Review
- Revenue: Actual vs. goal
- Units sold vs. goal
- Conversion rate (sales page visitors to buyers)
- Top traffic source
- Refund rate
- Customer satisfaction (survey score)
- Top 3 improvements needed

## Sales Page Template Structure

```
1. HEADLINE
   — Benefit-focused, specific, max 10 words

2. SUBHEADLINE
   — Clarify who it's for and what they get, max 20 words

3. HERO VISUAL
   — Product mockup, demo screenshot, or key result image

4. THE PROBLEM
   — Articulate the pain point better than the customer can
   — Make them feel understood (not sold to)

5. INTRODUCING [PRODUCT]
   — Bridge from problem to solution
   — One paragraph, outcome-focused

6. WHAT'S INSIDE
   — Feature list with benefit framing
   — Visuals of the product (screenshots, demos)

7. SOCIAL PROOF
   — 3–5 testimonials: name, photo, specific result

8. WHO THIS IS FOR
   — 3 bullet points: "This is for you if..."
   — Optional: "This is NOT for you if..."

9. THE INVESTMENT
   — Price with value framing ("For less than one hour of consulting...")
   — What's included (reiterate)
   — Guarantee (if applicable)
   — BUY BUTTON

10. FAQ
    — 5–8 objection-handling questions

11. FINAL CTA
    — Last BUY BUTTON with short urgency/benefit reminder
```

## Email Launch Sequence Templates

### Pre-Launch Sequence (4 emails, 2 weeks before)

**Email 1 (2 weeks before):** "Something is coming"
- Tease the topic; don't reveal product yet
- CTA: Share a free resource related to the topic

**Email 2 (10 days before):** "Here's the problem we're solving"
- Articulate the pain point in depth
- CTA: "Hit reply if this resonates — I want to hear your story"

**Email 3 (7 days before):** "What I built for you"
- Reveal the product; explain what it is and who it's for
- CTA: Get on the waitlist for early access

**Email 4 (1 day before):** "Opening tomorrow"
- Remind waitlist of what's coming and the early-bird offer (if any)
- CTA: "Watch your inbox tomorrow morning"

### Launch Day Email
**Subject:** "It's here: [Product Name]"
- 2 paragraphs: remind them of the problem, introduce the solution
- What's included (brief)
- Price + CTA button
- Guarantee (if applicable)
- 1 testimonial

### Follow-Up Email 1 (Day 3): For Non-Buyers
**Subject:** "Quick question about [topic]"
- Ask a question related to their pain point
- Soft mention of product as solution
- CTA: Buy or ask a question

### Follow-Up Email 2 (Day 5): FAQ Format
**Subject:** "Answering your questions about [Product Name]"
- 3–4 Q&A format objections
- CTA: Buy

### Closing Email (Day 7, if cart closes):
**Subject:** "Closing tonight at midnight"
- Short email; restate what they lose if they don't act
- CTA: Last chance to buy

## Guarantee Policy

Standard guarantee across all digital products:
- **30-day money-back guarantee**, no questions asked
- For courses: If you complete the course and don't get value, full refund within 30 days of completion
- For SaaS: Cancel anytime; no refund for used months (clearly stated in terms)

**Rationale:** A guarantee reduces buyer anxiety more than it increases refunds. Our expected refund rate with strong product quality: <3%.

## Implementation Notes

- All launches use MailerLite for email
- All digital product sales via Lemon Squeezy (primary) or Gumroad

- Stripe for any custom checkout or subscription
- GA4 with conversion events on every sales page
- Analytics review: every launch + 30 days post-launch
