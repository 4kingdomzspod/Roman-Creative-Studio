## Overview

This document defines the annual budgeting framework, monthly budget allocations, category definitions, and review process for Roman Creative Studio. Budgets are reviewed quarterly and updated annually each January.

**Budget Philosophy:** Spend intentionally. Every dollar spent should accelerate revenue, protect the business, or build capability. Zero-based approach: justify each category each year, not just carry forward from prior year.

**Budget Cycle:** January → December  
**Annual Review:** January 1 (set new year budget)  
**Quarterly Review:** April, July, October (adjust allocations)  
**Monthly Tracking:** First Monday of each month (actuals vs. budget)

### 9. Emergency Reserve
**Target:** $5,000 (6-month operating expenses at ~$833/month lean operations)  
**Monthly Contribution:** $250/month until target reached  
**Storage:** Separate high-yield savings account (HYSA)

**Eligible emergency uses:**
- Equipment failure requiring immediate replacement
- Unexpected legal expense
- Client non-payment creating cash flow gap
- Medical or personal emergency affecting business
- Critical software failure requiring paid alternative

**Not eligible:**
- Growth investments
- New software subscriptions
- Education purchases
- Personal expenses

**Replenishment:** Any withdrawal must be replenished within 90 days.

### 11. Tax Reserve
**Allocation:** 25–30% of every revenue dollar received  
**Storage:** Separate business savings account (never touch for operations)  
**Quarterly estimated tax payments:** April 15, June 15, September 15, January 15

**Tax reserve calculation:**
```
Tax Reserve = Revenue Received × 27.5%
  (25% self-employment + federal + state blended rate estimate)

Example: $5,000 project payment
  Reserve = $5,000 × 0.275 = $1,375
  Available for operations = $3,625
```

**Year 1 estimated tax liability:**
- Conservative scenario ($36k revenue): ~$7,200–$9,000 tax
- Expected scenario ($60k revenue): ~$14,000–$18,000 tax
- Optimistic scenario ($90k revenue): ~$22,000–$28,000 tax

**Recommendation:** Work with a CPA in Q3 Year 1 to refine estimates and set up quarterly payments.

## Budget Review Process

### Monthly Budget Review (15 min)
1. Pull actuals from Stripe + bank/QuickBooks
2. Compare to budget by category
3. Note any variances >20%
4. Adjust behavior if needed (not the budget — adjust at quarterly review)
5. Update monthly summary spreadsheet

### Quarterly Budget Review (45 min)
1. Review 3-month actuals vs. budget
2. Identify categories consistently over or under budget
3. Adjust quarterly allocations for next quarter
4. Review revenue trajectory — update annual forecast
5. Check emergency reserve and growth fund balances
6. Review software costs for unused subscriptions (cancel)
7. Set 3 financial intentions for next quarter

### Annual Budget Review (2 hours)
**Scheduled:** December 15 – January 5  
**Output:** New year budget document approved and filed

1. Full year actuals by category
2. Revenue vs. plan analysis
3. Budget efficiency ratio (revenue per dollar spent)
4. Zero-based review: justify every category for new year
5. Update all caps and monthly allocations
6. Set annual revenue target and owner compensation target
7. Review insurance, legal, and contractor needs
8. Plan major capital expenditures (equipment, software)
9. Set tax strategy with CPA
10. Document and file final budget

## Budget Principles

1. **Revenue first.** No expense is justified unless it either protects existing revenue or generates new revenue.
2. **Tax before profit.** Fund the tax reserve before drawing owner compensation.
3. **Emergency before growth.** Build emergency fund before growth investments.
4. **Audit subscriptions monthly.** Cancel anything unused for 30+ days.
5. **Delay hardware.** Use existing equipment until failure or clear ROI on upgrade.
6. **Track everything.** No estimate budgeting — every dollar categorized.
7. **Annual price review.** Review all vendor contracts January 1 — renegotiate or switch.
