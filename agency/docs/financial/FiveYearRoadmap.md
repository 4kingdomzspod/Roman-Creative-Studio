# Five-Year Financial Roadmap
# Roman Creative Studio — Financial Operating System
# Section 14 of 15 | ERD Version 1.0

---

## Purpose

Project the 5-year financial trajectory of Roman Creative Studio — revenue targets, MRR milestones, team size, profitability, and key inflection points across all growth scenarios.

**Business Value:** A 5-year roadmap converts ambition into a measurable plan. It forces alignment between hiring, pricing, and revenue goals. It creates accountability by defining what success looks like each year.

**Owner:** CEO / CFO  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** BusinessScenarios.md, MRRArchitecture.md, HiringRoadmap.md, ScalingRoadmap.md

---

## 5-Year Vision

**Year 1 (2026):** Launch + First Clients — Survive and establish proof of concept  
**Year 2 (2027):** Systems + First Hires — Build the machine, not just the output  
**Year 3 (2028):** Scale + Market Position — Become the go-to premium agency for local businesses in our region  
**Year 4 (2029):** Expansion + Products — Launch digital products; expand to national audience  
**Year 5 (2030):** Authority + Enterprise — National brand, agency of record for industries served
