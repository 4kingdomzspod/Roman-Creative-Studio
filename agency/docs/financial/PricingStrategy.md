## Purpose

Document the complete pricing philosophy, frameworks, policies, and decision-making architecture for Roman Creative Studio. Establish consistent, defensible, and profitable pricing across all service tiers and engagement types.

---

## Business Value

Pricing is the single highest-leverage financial decision a service business makes. A 10% price increase on a $5,000 project adds $500 in pure margin. A 10% increase in client count adds $500 but requires acquisition cost, sales time, and delivery effort. This document ensures RCS prices intentionally, not reactively.

---

## Pricing Philosophy

Roman Creative Studio uses **value-based pricing** as its primary framework. This means:

1. **Price reflects outcome, not effort.** A website that generates 20 new patient inquiries per month for a dental practice is worth far more than the 40 hours it took to build. The client pays for the business result, not Alexander's time.

2. **Price signals quality.** Clients who price-shop for the cheapest website are not RCS clients. Premium pricing pre-qualifies buyers who value quality and results.

3. **Pricing is part of brand positioning.** BUILD at $3,500 is not cheap — and it shouldn't be. The price communicates that RCS is not Fiverr or a website template.

4. **Pricing is reviewed annually.** As portfolio, reputation, and demand grow, prices increase. This is expected, communicated, and built into the business model.

5. **No race to the bottom.** RCS does not compete on price. When a prospect chooses a competitor based on price alone, they are not the right client.

## Framework 1 — Value-Based Project Pricing

### How Value-Based Pricing Works
```
Step 1: Understand the client's business outcome goal
  "We want to get 10 new patients per month from our website."

Step 2: Quantify the value of that outcome
  10 patients × $500 average value × 12 months = $60,000 annual value

Step 3: Price the project at a fraction of that value
  10% of annual value = $6,000 → GROW tier is the right fit

Step 4: Frame pricing in terms of the investment, not the cost
  "This investment pays for itself with 1 additional patient per month."
```

### Value Framing by Industry

| Industry | Avg Client Value | Annual Value Target | Suggested Tier |
|----------|-----------------|--------------------|---------------|
| Dental Practice | $800–$2,000/patient | $50,000–$200,000 | GROW or SCALE |
| Medical Practice | $500–$3,000/patient | $50,000–$300,000 | GROW or SCALE |
| Construction | $5,000–$50,000/project | Variable | BUILD or GROW |
| Restaurant | $30–$100/visit | $20,000–$100,000 | BUILD |
| Church | Mission value (non-revenue) | N/A | BUILD |
| Real Estate Agent | $5,000–$15,000/transaction | $50,000–$300,000 | GROW |
| Local Retail | $50–$500/customer | $25,000–$100,000 | BUILD |

## Framework 2 — Service Tier Pricing

### Published Pricing (Anchor Points)

```
BUILD — Starting at $3,500
  For: Local businesses launching their first professional website
  Scope: 5–7 pages, conversion-optimized, mobile-responsive
  Timeline: 3–4 weeks
  Deposit: 50% at contract signing

GROW — Starting at $6,500
  For: Growing businesses needing advanced features and SEO strategy
  Scope: 8–12 pages, advanced functionality, content strategy
  Timeline: 5–7 weeks
  Deposit: 50% at contract signing

SCALE — From $12,000
  For: Established businesses requiring complex, custom digital presence
  Scope: Custom; defined in discovery
  Timeline: 8–16 weeks
  Deposit: 50% at contract signing
```

### Internal Pricing Guide (Not Published)

```
BUILD floor: $2,800 (minimum to maintain margin)
BUILD ceiling: $4,500 (beyond this, GROW is appropriate)

GROW floor: $5,500
GROW ceiling: $9,000

SCALE floor: $12,000
SCALE ceiling: No ceiling (scope-dependent)
  Typical SCALE range: $12,000–$25,000
  Complex SCALE (multi-location, integrations): $25,000–$50,000
```

### Pricing Factors (Internal)
```
Price UP when:
  + Client has high revenue/growth potential
  + Short timeline requested
  + Multiple stakeholders involved
  + Complex integrations required
  + Premium industry (medical, legal, financial)
  + Client has history of scope expansion

Price DOWN (to floor, never below) when:
  - First engagement with a strategic referral partner
  - Long-term relationship building scenario
  - Church or nonprofit with clear mission alignment
  - Portfolio opportunity in a new industry segment
```

## Framework 3 — Monthly Retainer Pricing

### Retainer Philosophy
- Retainers are not "monthly project work" — they are ongoing partnership agreements
- Price reflects consistent relationship value, not hours worked per month
- Retainer clients receive priority scheduling and faster response times
- Retainers are month-to-month initially; annual pre-pay available at 10% discount

### Care Plan Pricing ($197/mo)
Covers: Security monitoring, software updates, monthly performance audit, uptime monitoring, up to 1 hour content updates, monthly report.

**Value articulation:** "We handle everything technical so you can focus on your business. One month of Care Plan is less than the cost of one hour of emergency web developer time when something breaks."

### SEO Retainer Pricing ($497/mo)
Covers: Everything in Care Plan + keyword rank tracking, monthly content recommendations, on-page SEO optimization (2 pages/month), backlink monitoring, monthly SEO report.

**Value articulation:** "The average dental practice that ranks #1 for 'dentist [city]' gets 30–50 new patients per year from search alone. This retainer is the investment to get there."

### Growth Partner Pricing ($997/mo)
Covers: Everything in SEO Retainer + monthly strategy call (30 min), conversion rate analysis, quarterly roadmap, content brief creation, analytics deep-dive, Google Business Profile management.

**Value articulation:** "This is your outsourced digital director. You get agency-level strategy without agency-level overhead."

### Annual Pre-Pay Option
```
Care Plan:    $1,970/year (save $394 vs monthly) = 10% discount
SEO Retainer: $4,970/year (save $994 vs monthly) = ~17% discount
Growth Partner: $9,970/year (save $1,994 vs monthly) = ~17% discount
```

## Framework 5 — Hourly Work (When Appropriate)

RCS is a value-based agency. Hourly work is the exception, not the rule. Appropriate use cases:

```
Appropriate for hourly billing:
  - Emergency support (outside Care Plan scope)
  - Ad hoc consulting sessions
  - Training sessions
  - Small, undefined tasks (< 2 hours)
  - Ongoing Care Plan clients requesting out-of-scope work

NOT appropriate for hourly billing:
  - New client projects (always scope and price)
  - SCALE projects (too complex for hourly)
  - Anything with creative deliverables (design, copywriting)
```

**Hourly Rate:** $175–$250/hour depending on complexity  
*Minimum billing increment: 30 minutes*  
*Minimum engagement: 1 hour*

## Framework 6 — Rush & Emergency Pricing

### Rush Fee Policy
```
Standard timeline: Defined per tier (3–16 weeks)

Rush (delivery 50% faster than standard):
  + 25% rush fee on total project price

Emergency (delivery within 5 business days, if capacity allows):
  + 50% rush fee on total project price

Emergency Support (outside Care Plan, business hours):
  $250/hour, 2-hour minimum

Emergency Support (outside business hours / weekends):
  $350/hour, 2-hour minimum
```

**Rush fee rationale:** Rush projects displace other client work, require prioritization, and create stress. Rush fees compensate for opportunity cost and capacity disruption — not just time.

## Revision Policy

```
BUILD Package — Revision Policy:
  Design phase: 2 revision rounds included
  Development phase: 1 revision round included
  Additional revisions: $125/hour

GROW Package:
  Design phase: 3 revision rounds included
  Development phase: 2 revision rounds included
  Additional revisions: $150/hour

SCALE Package:
  Design phase: Unlimited (within scope and timeline)
  Development phase: 3 revision rounds included
  Additional revisions: $175/hour

Definition of a "revision round":
  One compiled list of changes submitted as a single batch.
  Individual change requests submitted one at a time do not reset the round count.
```

**Revision policy rationale:** Unlimited revisions destroy margins and encourage indecision. Revision limits create healthy decision-making and respect everyone's time.

## Late Payment Policy

```
Net 7 payment terms on all invoices.

Day 8 (1 day overdue):
  Friendly reminder email sent automatically (Resend)

Day 14 (7 days overdue):
  Personal follow-up from Alexander
  Work on next milestone paused

Day 21 (14 days overdue):
  Formal late payment notice
  1.5% late fee applied to outstanding balance
  All active work suspended

Day 30+ (23+ days overdue):
  Collections process initiated
  Project deliverables withheld until payment received
  Client account flagged; no new work until balance cleared
```

## Refund Policy

```
Deposit (50%):
  Non-refundable after project kickoff meeting is held
  Full refund if RCS cancels the project
  Partial refund (50% of deposit) if cancelled before kickoff

Milestone Payments:
  Non-refundable once milestone deliverable is approved in writing

Care Plans:
  Cancel anytime; no refunds for current billing period
  Pro-rata refund if RCS cancels the relationship

Refund philosophy:
  Refunds are rare and handled case-by-case.
  RCS's goal is to make refunds unnecessary through excellent work.
  When things go wrong, the first response is always to fix it — not to issue a refund.
```

## Discount Policy

```
RCS does not offer discounts in the traditional sense.

When a prospect asks for a discount:
  Option A: Reduce scope to meet budget (BUILD instead of GROW)
  Option B: Extend timeline (no rush = no rush fee premium needed)
  Option C: Annual Care Plan pre-pay (built-in discount)
  Option D: Decline if budget is fundamentally misaligned

Exception — Strategic Discounting:
  A first project with a high-value strategic partner (marketing agency,
  accounting firm with 50 SMB clients) may warrant a 10–15% introductory
  rate in exchange for a committed referral relationship. Document this
  as a partnership agreement, not a discount.

Never:
  - Discount based on prospect pushing back
  - Offer speculative discounts ("what if I refer someone?")
  - Negotiate against published prices without scope justification
```
