# Referral System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 5, Document 11

---

### Why Referrals Are the Best Leads

Referral leads are pre-sold, pre-qualified, and pre-trusting. They arrive already believing RCS is the right choice because someone they respect said so. The close rate for referral leads is 3–4× higher than cold leads and the sales cycle is significantly shorter.

Referrals do not happen by accident at a premium agency level. They are engineered: by delivering outstanding work, at the right moment, with the right ask, and with a system that makes referring easy.

---

## Section 1 — When to Ask for Referrals

### The Timing Rule

Ask for a referral at the **peak satisfaction moment** — not at the end of the project, not in a routine email, and never before value has been delivered.

**Peak satisfaction moments:**

| Moment | Why It Works |
|---|---|
| 48–72 hours after launch | Client is most excited. The site is live, they're showing it to people. |
| First time they report a result ("We got 3 leads this week!") | The ROI is real. Gratitude is highest. |
| 30 days post-launch check-in | Enough time to see early results. Relationship is warm. |
| After a specific win (new patient, new client, booking spike) | The emotional high is a natural trigger for sharing. |

**Never ask:**
- During an unresolved issue or complaint
- At the final invoice
- In a generic annual check-in email blast
- Before value has been demonstrated

## Section 2 — How to Ask

### 2.1 The Direct Ask (In Conversation)

When a client expresses satisfaction in a call or message:

```
"I'm really glad to hear that. Honestly, the best thing that can
happen from a project like this is that you see the results and
you want to tell other business owners you know.

Is there anyone in your network — other [dentists/business owners/pastors]
— who you think could benefit from what we built for you?
I'd love to help them the same way."
```

**Why this works:** It's direct without being transactional. It positions the referral as an act of helping their colleagues, not a favor to RCS.

### 2.2 The Written Ask (Email)

**Subject:** "A quick ask + thank you"

```
Hi [First Name],

I hope [Business Name]'s new site is continuing to perform for you.

I have a quick ask: if you know any other [dentists/business owners/
church leaders/restaurant owners] who you think could benefit from
what we built together, I'd love an introduction.

A simple "you should talk to Alexander at Roman Creative Studio" is
more than enough — I'll take it from there.

In return, I want to make sure you know: anyone you refer gets my
best attention, and I'll treat the introduction as a reflection of
your recommendation.

Appreciate you either way.

Alexander
```

## Section 3 — Referral Incentive Structure

### 3.1 The RCS Referral Philosophy

RCS does not use cash commissions for referrals from clients. Cash commissions cheapen the act of recommending a trusted service — they turn a genuine endorsement into a transaction.

Instead, RCS offers:

**Option A: Upgrade Credit**
A meaningful credit applied to future RCS services (Care Plan, additional features, blog content, SEO work). Not a cash payment.

```
"For every qualified client you refer who completes a project with us,
you receive a $[X] credit toward any future RCS service — Care Plan,
content, or site updates. No expiry."
```

**Option B: Priority Access**
Referred clients of RCS clients receive priority scheduling and dedicated attention. Not a discount — a status acknowledgment.

**Option C: Partnership Acknowledgment**
For prolific referrers (multiple successful introductions), a formal "Strategic Partner" acknowledgment:
```
"You're one of our most valued advocates — and I want to recognize that.
[Specific gesture: co-promotion on Instagram, featured in a case study,
annual priority client status.]"
```

### 3.2 What RCS Does NOT Offer

- Discounts on existing projects for referring
- Cash payments to client contacts
- "Refer 5, get 1 free" volume structures
- Referral programs marketed publicly (referrals should feel personal, not promotional)

## Section 4 — Referral Messaging Templates

### Template 1: Introduction Email from Client to Prospect

Make it easy for clients to refer by providing a ready-to-forward message:

```
[Client sends to their contact:]

"Hey [Name],

I recently worked with Alexander at Roman Creative Studio on my
website and it was an outstanding experience. The new site looks
professional and we've already seen [specific result].

Given what you've been saying about [your website / your digital
presence], I thought it was worth an introduction.

I'm CC'ing Alexander here — feel free to reach out directly."
```

Sending a pre-written version to the referring client removes friction from the act of referring.

### Template 2: RCS Response to a Warm Introduction

```
Subject: Great to meet you, [Name]

Hi [Name],

Thanks for the introduction, [Client Name] — much appreciated.

[Name], I'd love to learn about your business and what you're
trying to accomplish digitally. [Client Name] is a great example
of what's possible when the strategy is right.

Would a 20-minute call this week make sense? Here's my link:
[Calendly]

Looking forward to connecting.

Alexander
Roman Creative Studio
romancreativestudio.co
```

## Section 5 — Referral Partner System

Beyond client referrals, cultivate relationships with professional networks who serve the same clients:

**Ideal referral partners:**

| Partner Type | Why They Refer |
|---|---|
| Dental equipment/supply reps | They visit dozens of dental offices; websites often come up |
| Church consultants / coaches | They work with churches on growth strategy |
| Business coaches / consultants | Their clients often need better digital presence |
| Accountants serving small businesses | They see businesses at growth inflection points |
| Commercial real estate agents | New businesses moving into spaces need websites |
| Marketing agencies (different specialty) | Non-competing agencies cross-refer |

**Referral partner approach:**
```
"I work with [their client type] on web presence and digital strategy.
I often have clients who need [their service]. I'd love to know
if there's a way we can refer each other when it makes sense."
```

**Rule:** Referral partnerships are reciprocal. If you're asking someone to refer clients to you, be prepared to refer clients to them.

## Section 6 — Referral Tracking

Track all referral activity in a simple log:

```
REFERRAL LOG

Date:           [Date of referral]
Source:         [Referring client / partner name]
Referral:       [Name of referred prospect]
Business:       [Business name]
Industry:       [Industry vertical]
Status:         [Discovery call / Proposal / Closed / Not qualified]
Result:         [Project value if closed]
Incentive sent: [Date credit applied / acknowledgment made]
```

Review monthly. Identify top referral sources and acknowledge them proactively.
