Services

Roman Creative Studio provides web design, brand identity, SEO, conversion optimization, and related digital services. Specific project scope, deliverables, timelines, and investment amounts are defined in individual project proposals and agreements.

Project Agreements

All projects begin with a signed project agreement and a deposit payment. The project agreement supersedes any general terms stated here and governs the specific engagement. Until a project agreement is signed and a deposit is received, no project work commences.

Payment

Standard payment structure is 50% deposit upon agreement signing, 25% at design approval, and 25% upon project completion. Late payments may result in project pauses. All fees are in USD unless otherwise specified in the project agreement.

Intellectual Property

Upon receipt of final payment, the client receives full ownership of all custom design and code created for their project. Roman Creative Studio retains the right to display the completed project in its portfolio, case studies, and marketing materials unless the client requests otherwise in writing.

Revisions

Each project includes a defined number of revision rounds as specified in the project agreement. Additional revisions beyond the included rounds are billed at the standard hourly rate or as a change order. Revisions must be submitted in writing via email.
