The Reality

Most Church Websites Push People Away Without Realizing It

If a first-time visitor can’t quickly answer these questions, they’ll move on.

What does your church believe?

Most sites bury doctrine in a 400-word page. Visitors want it in 30 seconds or they’re gone.

What will Sunday morning feel like?

People are anxious about showing up somewhere new. Your website should eliminate that anxiety visually.

Is there something for my kids?

Families decide based on children’s ministry. If it’s not prominent, you’re losing entire households.

How do I watch online or give?

Streaming and digital giving should take one click from your homepage. Not three pages deep.
