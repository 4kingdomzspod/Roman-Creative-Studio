# Cold Email Templates

Use these as a starting point, not a script to send verbatim. Personalize every `{{bracketed}}` field before sending — a cold email that's obviously copy-pasted gets deleted. Keep the tone direct and low-pressure: we're offering to look at their site and share what we see, not pushing a hard sell.

**Do not add stats, client counts, or results that aren't true.** If a line doesn't apply to a specific business, cut it — don't fill the gap with something invented.

---

## Notes for whoever is sending these

- Replace every `{{field}}` before sending. An email with a literal `{{Business Name}}` in it is worse than not sending one at all.
- The "one specific observation" line in Template A is the most important sentence in the email — it's the difference between a personalized note and spam. Don't send Template A without actually looking at the site first.
- Keep claims limited to what's true today: two completed client projects (Iconic Rentals, Bouquetsscl), a free 30-minute call, and the services we actually offer (custom websites, lead generation, mobile optimization, SEO, ongoing support). Don't add "we've helped X businesses" or similar until that's actually true and documented.
- No guarantees ("we'll double your leads," etc.) — ever.
