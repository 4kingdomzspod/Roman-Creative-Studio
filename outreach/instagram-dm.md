# Instagram DM Templates

DMs should read like a real person sending a real message, not a sales script. Shorter than email, casual, and specific to their account. Personalize every `{{bracketed}}` field. If you can't fill in the specific detail honestly, don't send the message.

**No fake compliments, no invented stats, no "I've helped X businesses like yours."**

---

## Template A — Cold DM to a business account

Hey! Came across {{Business Name}} — {{something specific and true: a recent post, their bio, a service they mention}}. Do you have a website already? I build custom sites for local businesses (mobile-first, built to actually generate calls/bookings, not just look nice). Happy to take a quick look at what you've got and share a thought or two if that's useful — no pitch, just an honest look.

---

## Template B — Follow-up after they view a story / engage with a post

Hey {{First Name}}, thanks for the follow/engagement! Quick one — I noticed {{Business Name}} {{specific detail}}. I build websites for local service businesses (a couple of examples: romancreativestudio.co/portfolio). If you're ever thinking about a new site or a refresh, happy to chat. No pressure at all.

---

## Template C — Short and direct

Hi! I design websites for local businesses — saw {{Business Name}} and wanted to reach out. If a new site (or a second opinion on your current one) is ever on your radar, I'd be glad to help. Link in bio if you want to see examples or grab a free call.

---

## Notes

- Keep it to 2–4 sentences. Long DMs get ignored.
- Link to `romancreativestudio.co` or `romancreativestudio.co/book.html` only if it fits naturally — don't force a link into every message.
- If they reply with interest, move the conversation to email or a booked call as soon as it makes sense — DMs aren't built for a real sales conversation.
- Same rule as everywhere else: no fabricated numbers, no promised results, no "guaranteed" language.
