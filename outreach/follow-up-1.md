# Follow-Up #1

**When to send:** 3–4 business days after the initial cold email or DM, if there's been no response. See `OUTREACH_PLAYBOOK.md` for the full cadence.

**Goal:** Stay top of mind without being pushy. Shorter than the original message — this is a nudge, not a second pitch.

---

## Email version

**Subject:** Following up — {{Business Name}}

Hi {{First Name}},

Just following up on my note from last week in case it got buried — totally understand if things are busy.

If a new website (or a look at improving your current one) is something you're thinking about, I'd still be glad to help. If not, no worries at all — feel free to let me know and I won't follow up again.

A couple of completed projects if you want to see examples of the work: https://romancreativestudio.co/portfolio.html

{{Your Name}}
Roman Creative Studio
Alexander@romancreativestudio.co

---

## Instagram DM version

Hey {{First Name}}, just circling back in case my last message got lost in the shuffle. Still happy to take a look at {{Business Name}}'s site if that's ever useful — no pressure if now's not the right time.

---

## Notes

- Don't repeat the full pitch. Reference that you reached out before and leave the door open.
- Give them an easy way to opt out ("let me know and I won't follow up again") — it reads as respectful, not desperate, and it's honest.
- Still no invented urgency ("spots filling up," "limited time") — nothing here is scarce, so don't pretend it is.
