Bouquetsscl

A handmade bouquet business based in Avon Park, FL. This is a completed, real website RCS built and launched for the client.

View Live Site ↗ Discuss a Similar Project →
Business & Industry

Overview

Bouquetsscl is a handmade floral arrangement business based in Avon Park, FL. Roman Creative Studio designed and built the company's website — a single-page site presenting the product catalog, custom order options, and past work in one place.

The Challenge

Client Goal

The client needed a professional online presence beyond social media. The goal was to showcase the floral products and make it easier for customers to discover arrangements and request orders.

What We Built

RCS Solution

We built a single-page website with a full pre-made arrangement catalog and pricing, a custom order interface for one-off requests, a photo gallery of past work, an About section introducing the business, an FAQ section, and a contact flow for general inquiries.
