Client Project

Iconic Rentals

A luxury yacht charter and exotic car rental company in Miami, FL. This is a completed, real website RCS built and launched for the client.

View Live Site ↗ Discuss a Similar Project →
Business & Industry

Overview

Iconic Rentals is a Miami-based luxury concierge business offering private yacht charters and exotic & luxury car rentals. Roman Creative Studio designed and built the company's website — a single-page site presenting both sides of the business (yacht charters and the exotic car fleet) with a unified reservation and inquiry experience.

The Challenge

Client Goal

The client needed a professional website presence beyond social media. The goal was to showcase the luxury yacht and exotic car rental services and give potential customers a better way to inquire — something a Instagram profile alone couldn't provide.

What We Built

RCS Solution

We built a single-page website that presents the yacht charter and exotic car rental sides of the business side by side, with dedicated fleet presentation, a photo and video gallery, an FAQ section, and a reservation form connected to a live database so inquiries are captured and stored automatically instead of relying on DMs or phone tag.
