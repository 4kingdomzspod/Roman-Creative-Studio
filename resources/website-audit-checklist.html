Website Audit Tool

The 25-Point Website
Audit Checklist

Run through every item on this list to find exactly what’s holding your website back from generating leads, booking appointments, and growing your practice.

How to use this checklist: Go through each item and check it off if your site passes. Items marked CRITICAL have the biggest impact on whether visitors book or leave. When you’re done, count your score — the scoring guide is at the bottom.
1
Technical Performance
5 items
  • Page loads in under 3 seconds on mobile
    Test at pagespeed.web.dev. 53% of mobile visitors leave if a page takes longer than 3 seconds. Aim for under 2.5s.
    Critical
  • Site is fully mobile-responsive
    Over 60% of healthcare searches happen on mobile. Tap all buttons and forms on a real phone, not just a resized browser window.
    Critical
  • Site runs on HTTPS (SSL certificate active)
    Browsers show a “Not Secure” warning on HTTP sites, which kills trust instantly. Your URL should start with https://.
    Critical
  • No broken links or 404 errors
    Use a free tool like Dead Link Checker. Broken links hurt SEO and erode trust with visitors.
    Important
  • Images are compressed and optimized
    Large uncompressed images are the #1 cause of slow load times. Use WebP format where possible. Max 200KB per image.
    Important
2
Design & User Experience
5 items
  • A clear call-to-action is visible above the fold
    Visitors should know exactly what to do within 3 seconds of landing. “Book an Appointment” or “Call Now” should be visible without scrolling.
    Critical
  • Navigation has 5–7 items maximum
    Too many nav items cause decision paralysis. Every extra option reduces the chance a visitor takes the action you want.
    Important
  • Phone number is clickable and in the header
    Mobile visitors should be able to call you with one tap. A phone number in the header alone increases calls by 25–40%.
    Critical
  • Real photos are used (not generic stock)
    Real photos of your office, team, and patients build trust 10x faster than stock photography. Visitors can tell the difference immediately.
    Important
  • Sufficient color contrast (text is readable)
    Grey text on white backgrounds is one of the most common design mistakes. Test at webaim.org/resources/contrastchecker. Aim for 4.5:1 ratio.
    Quick Fix
3
Content & Messaging
5 items
  • Homepage headline answers “what do you do and for whom”
    Example: “Dental Care for Families in Austin, TX” is clearer than “Welcome to Our Practice.” Clarity always beats cleverness.
    Critical
  • Services are explained in plain language
    Avoid clinical jargon. Write as if you’re explaining a service to a patient at the front desk. Benefits over features.
    Important
  • Social proof appears near every CTA
    Patient reviews, star ratings, or “Joined by 500+ patients” near your booking buttons dramatically increase conversions. Don’t put reviews only on a dedicated page.
    Critical
  • A clear About/Team page exists with real bios
    People choose healthcare providers based on trust. A real photo and genuine bio from the doctor or pastor goes further than any design element.
    Important
  • No filler text or “Welcome to our website” copy
    Every sentence should do a job: answer a question, build trust, or move the visitor toward booking. Delete anything that doesn’t pass this test.
    Quick Fix
4
SEO Foundation
5 items
  • Every page has a unique title tag (under 60 characters)
    Title tags are the #1 on-page SEO factor. Include your primary keyword and city. Example: “Family Dentist in Austin, TX | Bright Smile Dental”
    Critical
  • Google Business Profile is claimed and complete
    GBP is your most powerful local SEO tool. Ensure name, address, phone, hours, photos, and category are all complete and accurate.
    Critical
  • NAP (Name, Address, Phone) is consistent everywhere
    Your business name, address, and phone number must be identical on your website, GBP, Yelp, and all directories. Inconsistency confuses Google.
    Important
  • Google Search Console is set up and verified
    GSC shows you exactly what search terms bring visitors to your site. Free, and essential for tracking SEO performance.
    Quick Fix
  • Images have descriptive alt text
    Alt text helps Google understand your images and improves accessibility. “Dr-Smith-Austin-family-dentist.jpg” is better than “IMG_4521.jpg”.
    Quick Fix
5
Lead Generation & Conversion
5 items
  • Online booking or contact form is working and tested
    Fill out your own form right now. Is it easy? Does the confirmation email arrive? A broken form silently kills your lead flow.
    Critical
  • Contact form asks for 5 fields or fewer
    Every extra field reduces form completion by ~10%. Only ask for what you absolutely need: name, phone/email, and a brief message.
    Important
  • Google Analytics (or similar) is installed
    You can’t improve what you don’t measure. At minimum, you need to know how many visitors you get and which pages they visit.
    Important
  • CTA buttons use action words (not just “Submit”)
    “Book My Appointment” converts 3–4x better than “Submit”. “Get My Free Consultation” beats “Contact Us.” Specificity and ownership language work.
    Quick Fix
  • A FAQ section exists to handle common objections
    Answering “Do you take insurance?”, “How long does an appointment take?”, and “Is parking available?” on your website reduces phone tag and pre-qualifies leads.
    Important

How Did You Score?

Count how many items you checked off. Use the guide below to understand where you stand — and what to prioritize next.

0–12
Needs Work
Your site is likely losing leads every day. Focus on the Critical items first — those 8 fixes alone can double your conversion rate.
13–19
Getting There
Solid foundation with clear room to grow. Tackle the remaining “Important” items to close the gap and pull ahead of local competitors.
20–25
Strong Position
Your site is performing well. Focus on speed, A/B testing CTAs, and content to push from good to exceptional.
