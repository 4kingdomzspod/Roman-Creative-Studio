Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 5, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Roman Creative Studio ("we," "us," or "our") collects through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have. This is a general, plain-language policy — if your organization requires policy language tailored to a specific jurisdiction or regulation, have it reviewed by legal counsel before relying on it.

Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, email address, phone number, business or organization name, and any message details you submit through our Contact or Booking forms.
  • Automatically collected information — standard technical data your browser sends when you visit the site, such as pages viewed and general device/browser information. We do not currently run analytics or advertising tracking on this website; if that changes, this policy will be updated.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to respond to inquiries, schedule calls, prepare proposals, and communicate with you about your project. We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Cookies

This site may use minimal cookies or local storage required for basic functionality (such as remembering that you closed a menu). We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

Third-Party Services

This website loads fonts from Google Fonts to display our typography. Google Fonts may process standard connection information (such as IP address) as part of delivering those font files. We do not embed third-party analytics or advertising scripts on this site at this time.

Data Security

We take reasonable administrative and technical precautions to protect the information you share with us. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Your Choices

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below. You can also unsubscribe from any email communications at any time.

Children's Privacy

This website is intended for business use and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, contact us at Alexander@romancreativestudio.co or through our Contact page.
