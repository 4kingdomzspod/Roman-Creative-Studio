# Industry Pages Strategy

## Why Industry Pages Are Critical

Industry-specific pages are the highest-ROI content Roman Creative Studio can create. Here's why:

1. **High search intent** — "dentist website design" is searched by someone who knows what they want and is close to buying
2. **Low competition at the local/regional level** — most agencies don't build industry-specific pages
3. **Faster trust building** — when a dental practice owner sees a page built specifically for them, they feel understood immediately
4. **Compounding SEO** — each page grows organic traffic independently

## Industry Page Checklist

For each new industry page:

- [ ] Research: 3 top competitor pages in this niche
- [ ] Identify: primary keyword, 3–5 secondary keywords
- [ ] Write: industry-specific headline targeting #1 pain or goal
- [ ] Write: "Problem" section (what costs them without a good site)
- [ ] Write: "Solution" section (what we build for them specifically)
- [ ] List: 4–6 features specific to this industry
- [ ] Add: relevant testimonial (or placeholder until one exists)
- [ ] Add: pricing preview (starting price)
- [ ] Write: 4–6 FAQ specific to this industry
- [ ] Add: strong CTA with industry-specific copy
- [ ] Set: title tag, meta description, H1 with keyword
- [ ] Internal link: homepage, pricing, contact, process
- [ ] Dark theme: verify all colors match brand system
- [ ] Mobile: test at 375px
