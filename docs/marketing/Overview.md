# Marketing Overview

## Marketing Philosophy

Roman Creative Studio markets the way we tell our clients to market: through **genuine value, consistent presence, and strategic positioning** — not through ads or gimmicks.

Our marketing does three things:
1. **Attracts** the right clients through content they find useful
2. **Builds trust** by demonstrating expertise before the sales conversation
3. **Converts** by making the next step obvious and low-friction

## SEO Strategy

### Target Keyword Categories

**Service keywords (high intent):**
- "[industry] website design" (dentist, church, restaurant, contractor, etc.)
- "web design for [industry]"
- "[industry] website redesign"

**Problem-aware keywords (mid-funnel):**
- "why is my website not getting leads"
- "how to improve website conversion rate"
- "local SEO for small business"

**Educational keywords (top of funnel):**
- "how much does a website cost"
- "what makes a good dental website"
- "church website best practices"

### SEO Infrastructure Needed
- `sitemap.xml` (missing — create immediately)
- `robots.txt` (missing — create immediately)
- JSON-LD structured data on homepage and service pages
- Google Search Console verified
- Google Analytics 4 tracking verified

## Email Marketing

### List Building Sources
- Resource downloads (website audit, SEO guide, conversion guide)
- Blog newsletter signup
- Contact form opt-in ("Want tips on growing your business online?")

### Email Types
- **Welcome sequence** (3 emails over 7 days for new subscribers)
- **Monthly newsletter** (1 tip, 1 case study, 1 CTA)
- **Launch announcements** (new industry pages, new resources)
- **Re-engagement** (for cold subscribers)
