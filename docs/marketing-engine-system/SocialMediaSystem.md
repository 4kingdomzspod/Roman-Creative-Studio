# Social Media System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the social media strategy for Roman Creative Studio across all active platforms. Social media at RCS is not an entertainment channel. It is an authority distribution engine — a system for getting the right content in front of the right people consistently enough that RCS becomes the obvious choice when they are ready to invest in their website.

---

## Social Media Philosophy

> **Social media is not for going viral. It is for building authority with the 3% of your audience who are actively looking to buy, and nurturing the 97% who will be ready later.**

RCS social content serves three functions:

1. **Authority signals** — Position RCS as the premium expert in the room
2. **Trust building** — Show real work, real results, and real process
3. **Lead funnel support** — Move followers toward a website visit, a lead magnet download, or a Discovery Call booking

Content that is entertaining but does none of these three is not posted.

## Instagram Strategy

### Audience
Small business owners aged 28–55. Visually oriented. Decision-makers for their business. Use Instagram to discover vendors, agencies, and service providers.

### Content Mix (Weekly)
| Format | Frequency | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Portfolio / project showcase | 1× per week | Proof |
| Educational carousel | 1× per week | Education |
| Process / behind-the-scenes | 1× per week | Authority |
| Client result / testimonial | 1× per week | Proof |
| Conversion / offer post | 1× per week | Conversion |

### Content Style Rules
- **Portfolio posts:** Clean, full-screen mockups. Desktop and mobile views. Brand colors visible. No clutter.
- **Educational carousels:** 6–10 slides. Slide 1 = hook (bold statement or question). Slides 2–8 = value content. Final slide = CTA (`"Book a free Discovery Call — link in bio"`).
- **Captions:** Start with a hook sentence (not a greeting). Maximum 3 short paragraphs. End with CTA or question. No hashtag stuffing — 3–5 targeted hashtags only.
- **Visual consistency:** All posts use RCS brand palette (gold `#D4AF37`, charcoal `#0C0E11`, warm white `#F0EFE9`). Cormorant Garamond for display text over 30px. Inter for body text.
- **Reels:** 15–60 seconds. Hook in first 2 seconds. Design reveal, before/after, or tip format. Vertical (9:16).

### Instagram Hashtag Strategy
Use a mix of:
- Niche-specific: `#dentalwebdesign` `#churchwebsite` `#restaurantmarketing`
- Service-specific: `#webdesign` `#brandidentity` `#conversionoptimization`
- Business-specific: `#smallbusinesswebsite` `#businessgrowth`
- Local (if applicable): `#[city]webdesign` `#[city]smallbusiness`

Maximum 5 hashtags per post. Never use mass generic hashtags (`#love`, `#instagood`).

### Instagram Bio
```

Brand identity · Websites · SEO
Book a free audit ↓
[link to Discovery Call booking page]
```

## LinkedIn Strategy

### Audience
Business professionals, founders, practice owners, executives. Higher average deal value from LinkedIn leads. Research-phase decision-makers who will not buy immediately but will remember RCS.

### Content Mix (Weekly)
| Format | Frequency | Pillar |
|---|---|---|
| Long-form thought leadership post | 1× per week | Authority |
| Educational post (tips / insights) | 1× per week | Education |
| Case study or result feature | 1× per week | Proof |

### Content Style Rules
- **Opening line:** Stops the scroll. Make a bold statement, cite a specific stat, or ask a sharp question. Never start with `"I'm excited to share..."` or `"Great news!"`
- **Format:** Short paragraphs (1–2 sentences). Line breaks between every paragraph. No wall of text.
- **Length:** 200–800 words for long-form posts. 50–150 words for engagement posts.
- **No external links in post body.** LinkedIn suppresses reach for posts with outbound links. Put links in the first comment.
- **Close with a question or CTA.** `"What's the biggest challenge your website gives you?"` or `"If your website isn't generating leads, let's talk — link in comments."`
- **Tone:** Expert-to-peer. Not salesy. Not corporate. Direct and confident.

### LinkedIn Profile Optimization (Alexander)
- **Headline:** `Founder, Roman Creative Studio | Premium Web Design + Conversion Strategy for Service Businesses`
- **About section:** 3–5 paragraphs. Who RCS serves, what results it delivers, why the approach is different. Ends with CTA to book Discovery Call.
- **Featured section:** Link to portfolio, top blog article, and free website audit offer
- **Banner image:** RCS branded visual with tagline

## TikTok Strategy

### Audience
Broader discovery audience. Younger business owners (25–40). Early-stage companies. High volume of impressions possible with educational content.

### Content Mix (3× per week)
| Format | Pillar |
|---|---|
| Design tip / education short | Education |
| Before/after reveal | Proof |
| Behind-the-scenes process | Authority |

### Content Style Rules
- **Hook in first 1.5 seconds.** The hook is everything. If the first line does not stop scrolling, the video is ignored.
- **Good hooks:** `"Your website is losing you customers and you don't know why."` / `"I redesigned this dental website and bookings went up 40%."` / `"3 things your website is missing right now."`
- **Format:** Talking head or screen record. Captions always on (80% of TikTok is watched without sound).
- **Length:** 30–90 seconds. No longer.
- **CTA:** End every video with `"Link in bio to book a free website audit."`
- **Do not hard-sell.** TikTok rewards educational and entertaining content. Treat every video as a free sample of RCS thinking.

## YouTube Shorts Strategy

### Audience
Research-oriented viewers. Business owners looking for tutorials, explanations, and guidance. YouTube searches for `"how to"` and `"what is"` questions.

### Content Mix (2× per week)
| Format | Pillar |
|---|---|
| Answer one specific question | Education |
| Mini case study or result reveal | Proof |

### Content Style Rules
- **Repurpose TikTok content.** YouTube Shorts and TikTok share the same 9:16 vertical format. Publish the same video to both (adjust captions/hashtags).
- **Title:** Optimized for YouTube search. Include the question being answered. Example: `"Does Your Website Need SEO? (Here's the Truth)"`
- **Description:** 2–3 sentences. Include link to full blog article and Discovery Call booking page.
- **Thumbnail:** Clean, readable at small size. Bold text overlay with the key point.

## Authority Positioning Rules (All Platforms)

1. **Never apologize for premium pricing.** Do not post content that suggests RCS is affordable, cheap, or budget-friendly.
2. **Never post desperation signals.** `"Accepting new clients!"` sounds desperate. `"Currently reviewing Q3 project briefs"` sounds in demand.
3. **Show the process, not just the output.** Behind-the-scenes content builds authority faster than portfolio posts alone.
4. **Quote real results.** Specific numbers (`"3 new patients in the first week"`) outperform vague claims (`"amazing results"`). Never fabricate metrics.
5. **Be consistent.** 3 posts per week every week for 6 months outperforms 20 posts in one week followed by silence.
6. **Position against the wrong approach, not against competitors.** `"This is why template websites fail service businesses"` is better than naming a competitor.

## Social Media Prohibited List

- Inspirational quotes unrelated to web design or business growth
- `"Happy Monday!"` or `"Hope everyone had a great weekend"` filler
- Asking for follows, likes, or shares directly (`"Drop a ❤️ if you agree"`)
- Posting the same caption across all platforms
- Engagement bait without substance
- Responding to trends that are not relevant to RCS positioning
- Content that positions RCS as a generalist or budget option
