# Content Repurposing Engine
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 7: Marketing Engine System

---

## Purpose of This Document

This document defines the content repurposing system for Roman Creative Studio. Every content asset produced by RCS — a blog article, case study, social post, email, or video — is a source material for multiple other assets. No content is single-use.

Repurposing content does not mean copying and pasting. It means adapting the core idea of one piece into different formats for different channels, reaching more of the right people with less original production effort.

---

## Repurposing Philosophy

> **Create once. Distribute everywhere. Most people need to encounter your message 7+ times before they act. Repurposing ensures they encounter it across multiple channels.**

A single blog article, built correctly, can generate:
- 1 LinkedIn long-form post
- 1 Instagram educational carousel
- 1 TikTok/Reel script
- 1 YouTube Short
- 1 email newsletter section
- 1 social caption (across multiple platforms)
- 1 SEO snippet (reused as a social caption or email opener)
- 1 lead magnet chapter (if the article is comprehensive enough)

That is 8 content pieces from 1 original asset.

## Master Repurposing Framework

### Source: Blog Article

```
Blog Article (Tuesday publish)
  │
  ├── LinkedIn long-form post
  │     └ Same week (Tuesday or Wednesday)
  │     └ Condensed to 300–600 words
  │     └ First-person, conversational tone
  │     └ Ends with: "Read the full article → [link in comments]"
  │
  ├── Instagram carousel
  │     └ Same week (Wednesday or Thursday)
  │     └ 6–10 slides
  │     └ Slide 1: Hook (bold statement or question)
  │     └ Slides 2–8: One key point per slide
  │     └ Final slide: CTA ("Book a free Discovery Call — link in bio")
  │
  ├── TikTok / Instagram Reel script
  │     └ Same week (Thursday or Friday)
  │     └ 30–90 seconds
  │     └ Hook (first 1.5 seconds): most surprising or provocative insight
  │     └ 3 main points (one sentence each)
  │     └ CTA: "Link in bio for the full guide"
  │
  ├── YouTube Short
  │     └ Same video as TikTok (or re-record for YouTube search optimization)
  │     └ Title optimized for YouTube search
  │     └ Description includes link to full blog article
  │
  ├── Email newsletter section
  │     └ Thursday send
  │     └ 2–3 sentence summary of the blog article's key insight
  │     └ Ends with: "Read the full article →"
  │
  └── Quote card (optional)
        └ Extract one powerful sentence from the article
        └ Design as a branded Instagram quote card
        └ Post as standalone image with caption referencing the article
```

### Source: Case Study

```
Case Study (published on portfolio page)
  │
  ├── Blog article (case study breakdown format)
  │     └ Full narrative version (1,200–1,800 words)
  │     └ SEO-optimized title targeting industry keyword
  │     └ Same 6-section structure as case study
  │
  ├── Instagram before/after carousel
  │     └ Slide 1: Project name or industry + result headline
  │     └ Slide 2: "The problem" (client's situation before)
  │     └ Slides 3–4: Before screenshots (old website)
  │     └ Slides 5–6: After screenshots (new website)
  │     └ Slide 7: Key result metric
  │     └ Slide 8: Client quote
  │     └ Final slide: CTA
  │
  ├── LinkedIn case study post
  │     └ Story format: problem → solution → result
  │     └ 400–600 words
  │     └ Ends with: "Full case study in comments"
  │
  ├── TikTok project reveal
  │     └ Screen recording or mockup video of new website
  │     └ Voiceover: problem → result
  │     └ 30–60 seconds
  │
  ├── Email feature
  │     └ Mini case study in Thursday newsletter
  │     └ 150–200 words
  │     └ Links to full case study
  │
  └── Industry page testimonial/proof block
        └ Extract client quote and result metric
        └ Add to relevant industry landing page
        └ Links to full case study
```

  │

## Repurposing Quality Rules

1. **Adapt, do not copy.** A LinkedIn post is not a pasted blog paragraph. It is the core insight reformatted for LinkedIn's reading behavior (short paragraphs, no external links in body).

2. **Platform-native formatting.** Instagram carousels are visual. LinkedIn posts are personal and conversational. TikTok is hooks and energy. YouTube Shorts are educational and searchable. Each platform requires its own format.

3. **Consistent message, varied delivery.** The underlying idea stays the same. The format, tone, and depth adapt to the platform.

4. **Update before repurposing evergreen content.** Before repurposing a 12-month-old article, verify the information is still accurate. Update stats and examples as needed.

5. **Track what you've repurposed.** Maintain a content repurposing log to avoid publishing the same core idea twice in a short window on the same platform.

## Content Repurposing Efficiency Targets

| Original Asset | Minimum Repurposed Pieces |
|---|---|

| Case study | 6 (blog + Instagram carousel + LinkedIn post + TikTok + email + industry page update) |
| Client testimonial | 3 (website + Instagram post + LinkedIn post) |
| Lead magnet | 4 (blog expansion + Instagram preview + TikTok teaser + LinkedIn promotion) |

**Target:** Zero single-use content. Every asset is repurposed into a minimum of 3 additional pieces.
