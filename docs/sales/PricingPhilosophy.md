# Pricing Philosophy

## The Principle

**Price is a reflection of value, not cost.**

We do not price based on how many hours something takes. We price based on the business outcome the work delivers. A website that helps a dental practice attract 10 new patients per month is worth far more than the hours it took to build.

---

## Pricing Tiers (Conceptual)

### Build Tier
**Starting at $3,000 – $8,000**

For businesses that need a solid professional website:
- Custom design (not a template)
- Mobile responsive, fast, accessible
- SEO-ready structure
- Contact form + basic integrations
- Training on how to update content
- 30 days of post-launch support

### Grow Tier
**Starting at $6,000 – $15,000**

For businesses that need a website built for conversion and growth:
- Everything in Build
- Conversion-optimized structure and copy guidance
- Local SEO foundation setup
- Analytics implementation (GA4 + Search Console)
- Landing pages for specific campaigns
- Extended support period

### Scale Tier
**Custom Pricing**

For businesses ready for ongoing digital partnership:
- Everything in Grow
- Monthly Website Care Plan
- SEO retainer
- AI automation implementation
- CRM integration
- Quarterly strategy reviews

## How to Present Price

1. **Never lead with price** — establish value first
2. **Give a range in discovery** if asked directly: "Projects like yours typically range from $X to $Y depending on scope"
3. **Anchor high in the proposal** — present the full solution first, then offer a phased approach
4. **Frame as investment, not cost**: "This investment typically pays for itself within [timeframe] through [specific outcome]"
5. **Never discount without reducing scope** — discounting without reducing scope devalues the work and sets a bad precedent

---

## When to Walk Away

Do not accept projects where:
- The budget doesn't allow us to do the work properly
- The client sees design as an expense to minimize rather than an investment
- Accepting would require cutting corners we're not willing to cut

A client paying half price who has the same expectations as a full-price client is a liability, not an asset.

## Payment Structure

- **50% deposit** required before any work begins
- **50% on delivery** (or milestone-based for larger projects)
- Recurring plans: monthly on a set billing date
- Payment methods: bank transfer, credit card (add processing fee if applicable)
