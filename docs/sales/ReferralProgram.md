# Referral Program

## Why Referrals Are the Best Lead Source

A referred lead:
- Arrives with pre-built trust
- Has a shorter sales cycle
- Is more likely to be a good fit (the referrer knows both parties)
- Has a higher lifetime value (they came in through a relationship, not an ad)

Referrals should be systematized, not left to chance.

---

## The Program

### For Clients Who Refer
Current and past clients who refer someone who signs a contract receive:

- **$250 account credit** applied to their next invoice or care plan
- **OR: 1 free month of their care plan** (for clients on recurring plans)

This is offered proactively at project close and in the 30-day follow-up.

---

## When to Ask for Referrals

The best times to ask:

1. **At launch** — client excitement is at its peak
2. **At 30-day check-in** — after they've seen early results
3. **When they give unsolicited positive feedback** — strike while the emotion is present
4. **Anniversary of project launch** — a natural touchpoint

---

## How to Ask

Don't make it transactional. Make it personal:

> *"I'm really glad the site is working well for you. If you know another business owner who's in the same situation you were in — ready to upgrade their digital presence — I'd love the introduction. I'll take great care of anyone you send my way."*

Follow up with the referral offer only if they seem receptive:

> *"And just so you know, I have a referral program — if someone you refer becomes a client, I'll credit $250 to your account."*

---

## Referral Tracking

| Field | Track |
|-------|-------|
| Referrer name | Who sent the lead |
| Referred lead name | Who was referred |
| Date of referral | When it was made |
| Status | Contacted / Discovery / Proposal / Closed / Lost |
| Reward issued | Yes / No / Date |

Track in a simple spreadsheet or CRM.

---

## Building a Referral Ecosystem

Beyond client referrals, build referral relationships with:

- **Complementary service providers:** Photographers, copywriters, marketing consultants, bookkeepers, business coaches — anyone who works with small businesses
- **Industry associations:** Dental associations, church networks, local chambers of commerce
- **Past employers / colleagues:** Anyone who knows the quality of the work

Offer reciprocal referrals where appropriate.
