# Sales System Overview

## Philosophy

Roman Creative Studio sells through trust, expertise, and a clear process — not pressure tactics. The sales system is designed to:

1. Qualify leads before investing time in a discovery call
2. Educate prospects so they arrive at the call ready to decide
3. Move from discovery to proposal to contract efficiently
4. Set expectations that the client will carry through the entire engagement

**The best sale is one where both parties are excited to start.**

---

## Sales Funnel

```
Awareness
  ↓ (SEO, referral, social, podcast)
Interest
  ↓ (website visit, resource download, portfolio browse)
Consideration
  ↓ (contact form, booking page)
Discovery Call
  ↓
Proposal
  ↓
Contract + Deposit
  ↓
Kickoff
```

---

## Lead Sources (Current / Target)

| Source | Priority | Notes |
|--------|----------|-------|
| Referrals | 🔴 Highest | Highest close rate, lowest friction |
| Organic SEO | 🔴 High | Long-term, compounding |
| Industry service pages | 🔴 High | Targeted, high-intent |
| Resource downloads | 🟠 Medium | Education-first, longer cycle |
| Social media | 🟠 Medium | Awareness and trust-building |
| Cold outreach | 🟡 Lower | Time-intensive, lower ROI |
| Paid ads | 🟡 Lower | Only after organic is proven |
