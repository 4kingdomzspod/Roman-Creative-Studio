# Client Training System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 6, Document 10

---

### Training Philosophy

At the end of every RCS project, the client leaves empowered — not dependent.

Dependency is not a retention strategy. Clients who feel trapped or helpless with their own website become resentful, not loyal. Clients who are trained, confident, and capable become advocates who refer others and stay on Care Plans because they want to — not because they have to.

A well-trained client is also a low-maintenance client. Fewer panicked emails. Fewer unnecessary support requests. Fewer misunderstandings about what the website can do.

---

## Section 1 — Training Session Overview

**Format:** 60-minute video call (screen-share, recorded)
**Timing:** Scheduled within 5 days of launch
**Prerequisites:** Site is live, client has their login credentials

### Session Agenda

```
1. OVERVIEW (5 min)
   - Remind them of the full site architecture
   - Which pages exist and where everything lives
   - How the site is structured for their goals

2. CONTENT EDITING (20 min)
   - How to update text content
   - How to swap images
   - How to update contact information, hours, services
   - What they can change vs. what should go through RCS

3. BLOG / NEWS (10 min) [if applicable]
   - How to create a new blog post
   - How to add a featured image
   - How to use headings correctly (H1/H2/H3 explained simply)
   - SEO basics for new content (title tag, meta description)

4. FORMS & LEAD NOTIFICATIONS (10 min)
   - How to find and use contact form submissions
   - Where email notifications go
   - How to respond to leads from the website

5. ANALYTICS BASICS (10 min)
   - How to access Google Analytics
   - What to look at: visitors, traffic sources, top pages
   - What a "good" month looks like vs. warning signs
   - How to access Google Search Console

6. SUPPORT & MAINTENANCE (5 min)
   - What the 30-day support window covers
   - What Care Plans cover
   - How to reach RCS for help
   - What to do in an emergency (site down, form broken)
```

---

## Section 2 — Training Recording Protocol

Every training session is recorded via Loom or Zoom:
- Recording starts at the beginning of the session
- Client is informed that it's being recorded
- Recording is shared with the client within 24 hours via email
- Recording is stored in the client project folder permanently

**Why record:**
- Client can re-watch when they forget something
- New team members who join the client's business can watch it
- Reduces "how do I do X again?" support requests by 70%+

## Section 4 — What Clients Should NOT Do Themselves

Establish these guardrails in the training session:

| Action | Why It's Risky | What to Do Instead |
|---|---|---|
| Editing the CSS or HTML directly | Can break layouts or design system | Send a request to RCS |
| Changing the domain/hosting settings | Can take the site offline | Call RCS first |
| Installing unvetted plugins/scripts | Security risk, performance impact | Consult RCS first |
| Deleting pages or removing content that was strategically placed | Removes conversion or SEO infrastructure | Discuss with RCS |
| Changing the site title or meta description site-wide | Can damage SEO rankings | Discuss with RCS |
