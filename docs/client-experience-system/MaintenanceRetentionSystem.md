# Maintenance & Retention System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 6, Document 11

---

### Retention Philosophy

Every project client is a potential long-term recurring revenue relationship. A website is not a one-time product — it is living infrastructure that requires ongoing attention to remain fast, secure, optimized, and strategically relevant.

The Care Plan is not a passive support contract. It is a proactive partnership that:
- Generates predictable monthly revenue for RCS
- Reduces the risk of client websites degrading over time
- Creates consistent touchpoints that generate referrals and additional projects
- Positions RCS as a long-term partner rather than a one-off vendor

**The goal:** Every project client exits the 30-day support window onto a Care Plan.

---

## Section 1 — Care Plan Tiers

### Essential Care

**For:** Clients who want their site maintained without active growth work

**Includes monthly:**
- Security monitoring and updates
- Dependency/plugin updates
- Uptime monitoring (alert if site goes down)
- Performance check (PageSpeed score monitored)
- Broken link scan
- 1 hour of minor content updates (text, images, hours)
- Monthly performance summary email

**Response time for issues:** 48 business hours

**Positioning:** "Your site stays healthy without you having to think about it."

---

### Professional Care

**For:** Clients who want maintenance plus proactive content and SEO support

**Includes everything in Essential, plus:**
- 3 hours of content updates per month (new pages, copy updates, blog formatting)
- Monthly SEO report (ranking positions for target keywords)
- Google Business Profile update (monthly post)
- Analytics report with observations (not just data — what it means)
- 1 strategy check-in call per quarter (30 min)

**Response time for issues:** 24 business hours

**Positioning:** "Your site grows with your business, not behind it."

---

### Growth Care

**For:** Clients actively investing in SEO and conversion as growth channels

**Includes everything in Professional, plus:**
- Ongoing SEO management (keyword tracking, content optimization, link opportunities)
- Monthly CRO observation (conversion rate monitoring with recommendations)
- 1 new blog article per month (SEO-targeted)
- Quarterly strategy call (60 min) with written recommendations
- Priority support (2-hour response for any issue)
- Annual full website audit

**Positioning:** "Your digital presence is actively working as a growth channel every month."

## Section 4 — Quarterly Strategy Call

For Professional and Growth Care clients: a 30–60 minute call every quarter.

**Agenda:**
```
1. Review last quarter's performance (5 min)
2. Review SEO progress and keyword rankings (10 min)
3. Discuss any business changes that affect the website (5 min)
4. Identify 1–2 strategic improvements for next quarter (10 min)
5. Content and campaign planning (if applicable) (10 min)
6. Confirm next quarter's priorities (5 min)
```

**Why this matters:** Quarterly calls surface project opportunities (new service pages, campaign pages, redesigns) that generate additional revenue. They also keep the relationship active and referral-ready.

---

## Section 5 — Retention System Rules

| Rule | Reason |
|---|---|
| Monthly reports sent by the 5th, no exceptions | Consistency builds trust |
| Report contains at least one specific observation | Data alone is not value; interpretation is |
| Quarterly calls scheduled proactively (RCS initiates) | Clients who aren't contacted don't renew |
| Any issue resolved before the client asks about it | Proactive maintenance is the value proposition |
| Care Plan hours tracked and reported | Transparency prevents billing disputes |
| Annual audit offered to all long-term clients | Demonstrates investment in their growth |

---

## Section 6 — Care Plan Cancellation Protocol

If a client cancels their Care Plan:

1. Acknowledge without resistance: "Understood. Here's what happens at cancellation."
2. Provide a clear offboarding: credentials, final backup, documentation
3. Schedule a 30-day close-out — during which any final items are wrapped up
4. Leave the door open: "We're here when you need us again."
5. Add to the long-term nurture list (check in every 6 months)

**Never make a client feel penalized for canceling.** How the relationship ends determines whether they refer others.
