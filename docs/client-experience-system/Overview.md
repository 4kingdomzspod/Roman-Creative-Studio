### System Purpose

Phase 6 is the delivery backbone of Roman Creative Studio.

Phase 5 closes the sale. Phase 6 delivers the result. Everything from the moment a client signs to the moment they refer their first colleague is governed by this system.

A premium price demands a premium experience. Not just a premium output — a premium *process*. Clients who pay $10,000+ for a project are not just buying a website. They are buying certainty: certainty that the work will be done right, on time, without surprises, and by someone who communicates like a professional.

This system engineers that certainty.

### The Client Experience Standard

At RCS, every client — regardless of project size — experiences:

**Clarity:** They always know the current status, the next step, and what's expected of them.

**Structure:** Every project follows the same documented process. There is no improvisation.

**Progress Visibility:** Milestones are communicated proactively. Clients do not need to ask "where are we?"

**Confidence:** The process itself signals professionalism. Clients feel safe trusting the work to RCS.

**Respect:** Their time, their business, and their investment are treated with precision and care.
