# Future Vision

## Where Roman Creative Studio Is Headed

This document captures the long-range vision for the company — the version we're building toward, not just the version we are today.

---

## Phase 1: Establish (Now → 2026)

**Goal:** Build a sustainable, profitable one-person agency with a clear brand, documented process, and a growing portfolio.

**Milestones:**
- [ ] 10 completed client projects
- [ ] 3 documented case studies with measurable results
- [ ] Website Care Plan revenue covering baseline operating costs
- [ ] Clear, documented sales and onboarding process
- [ ] 2–3 industry pages beyond dentist and church
- [ ] Consistent inbound leads from SEO and referral

---

## Phase 2: Grow (2026 → 2028)

**Goal:** Expand capacity without sacrificing quality. Build recurring revenue. Become the recognized expert in 2–3 target industries.

**Milestones:**
- [ ] First contractor or team member brought in for specific capabilities
- [ ] 25+ completed client projects
- [ ] Recurring revenue (care plans + retainers) = 40% of total revenue
- [ ] Published case studies for dental, church, and 1–2 additional industries
- [ ] SEO-driven inbound generating 5+ qualified leads per month
- [ ] Podcast or content series driving brand awareness
- [ ] Speaking or collaboration in at least 1 industry community (dental, church, etc.)

---

## Phase 3: Scale (2028 → 2030)

**Goal:** Build a true agency with a small, exceptional team. Launch digital products. Establish Roman Creative Studio as a known brand in the independent agency space.

**Milestones:**
- [ ] Core team of 3–5 (designers, developers, strategists)
- [ ] Clear specialization in 3–5 industries
- [ ] Digital product line (templates, courses, audit tools)
- [ ] 100+ completed client projects
- [ ] A referral engine that drives the majority of new business
- [ ] Speaking at industry events or conferences
- [ ] Potentially: a second geographic market or vertical expansion

---

## Team Vision

Roman Creative Studio will always prioritize quality over scale. A small, exceptional team will always outperform a large, mediocre one.

**Target team by Phase 3:**
- Lead Strategist / Founder (Alexander)
- Senior Designer
- Front-End Developer
- Content / SEO Specialist
- Client Experience Coordinator

We will never compromise quality by growing faster than we can maintain the standard.

---

## The North Star

In 10 years, Roman Creative Studio is the agency that serious small business owners recommend to each other without hesitation — because working with us changed something real in their business.

That outcome is the measuring stick for every decision we make along the way.
