# Elevator Pitch

**Roman Creative Studio Brand Bible — Vol. 1, Part A**
*Version 1.0 | Last Updated: June 2026*

---

## Purpose of This Document

An elevator pitch answers one question: *"What do you do?"* The answer should be clear, memorable, and appropriate to the context. This document provides five versions for different situations. Use the one that fits the moment — and practice each until it's natural, not rehearsed-sounding.

---

## Principles of a Good Pitch

- Lead with the outcome, not the service
- Name the audience, not just the offer
- End with something that invites a follow-up question
- Sound like a human, not a brochure
- Never say "passionate about" or "leverage"

---

## Version 1: The 15-Second Pitch

*For: brief introductions, networking events, when someone is clearly in a hurry.*

> **"We help small businesses build websites and digital strategies that actually bring in customers. Strategy first, design second — and we stay involved long after launch."**

**Why it works:**
- Names the client (small businesses)
- Names the outcome (bring in customers)
- Differentiates (strategy first; stays involved after launch)
- Fourteen seconds. Room to breathe.

---

## Version 2: The 30-Second Pitch

*For: standard networking introductions, when someone asks "what do you do?" and seems genuinely interested.*

> **"Roman Creative Studio is a digital agency for small and growing businesses. We build custom websites and digital strategies for companies that are serious about growth — dental practices, churches, local service businesses, startups. We start with strategy — understanding the business, the market, and the customers — before we touch design. And we offer ongoing care plans so our clients don't have to think about their digital presence once it's built."**

**Why it works:**
- Names the business
- Names specific audiences (more credible than "businesses")
- Explains the strategic process
- Mentions the ongoing relationship model

---

## Version 3: The 60-Second Pitch

*For: discovery calls, introductory meetings, presentations where you have time to build context.*

> **"Roman Creative Studio is a digital agency built specifically for small and growth-stage businesses. We help founders, practice owners, and operators build a digital presence that reflects the actual quality of their business — and performs in the market.**
>
> **Most small businesses are either invisible online, or they have a website that's actively working against them. It doesn't load fast, it doesn't look professional, it doesn't guide visitors to take action. We fix that.**
>
> **What makes us different is that we start with strategy, not design. Before we open a design file, we understand the business goals, the ideal customer, and what needs to be true for a visitor to take action. Every design decision traces back to that.**
>
> **We work primarily with dental practices, churches, local service businesses, and startups — industries where we've built specific expertise. And we don't disappear after launch. Our Care Plans keep us invested in the client's results on an ongoing basis.**
>
> **If you're working with a business that's ready to take their digital presence seriously, that's exactly who we're built for."**

---

## Version 4: The Networking Version

*For: casual networking events where you're meeting multiple people and want to start genuine conversations, not deliver a pitch.*

> **"I run a digital agency for small businesses — mostly websites and SEO for dental practices, churches, and local service companies. Basically, I help businesses that do great work look as good online as they are in person. What do you do?"**

**Why it works:**
- Conversational, not performative
- Specific enough to be memorable
- Ends with a question — redirects to them, which is how you make a real connection
- Doesn't overstay its welcome

---

## Version 5: The Discovery Call Opening

*For: the first 60 seconds of a discovery call, after brief pleasantries, before you move into questions.*

> **"Before I ask you a bunch of questions — let me give you a quick picture of how we work so you know what you're evaluating.**
>
> **Roman Creative Studio is a digital agency focused on strategy-first web design and digital presence for small businesses. The industries we know best are dental, church, and local service — though we work across a number of sectors.**
>
> **We're different from most designers in that we start with business goals before we touch design. We want to understand what success looks like for you before we propose anything. And we offer ongoing Care Plans after launch so the relationship doesn't end at delivery.**
>
> **But I'm getting ahead of myself. Tell me what's going on with your business and what made you reach out."**

**Why it works:**
- Establishes credibility in 30 seconds
- Differentiates (strategy-first, ongoing relationship)
- Hands control back to the prospect immediately
- Sets up the discovery conversation rather than becoming a pitch

---

## Practice Notes

- Each version should be practiced until it flows naturally without memorization
- Adapt language in the moment — a pitch that sounds memorized is less effective than one that sounds genuine
- If the listener's eyes light up at a specific phrase, note it. That's your best line.
- Silence after the pitch is okay. Don't fill it. Let them respond.

---

## Future Notes

Update these pitches as the service offering expands — particularly when education, products, or a second team member becomes part of the story. The pitch should always be current.
