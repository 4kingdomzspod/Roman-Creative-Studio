# Taglines

## Primary Tagline

**"We Build Digital Experiences That Grow Businesses"**

This is the primary tagline — used in the hero section of the homepage and in primary brand contexts.

**Why it works:**
- Action-oriented ("we build")
- Specific deliverable ("digital experiences")
- Clear outcome ("grow businesses")
- No jargon
- Works across all industries we serve

---

## Secondary Taglines

For specific contexts, campaigns, or industry pages:

**Strategy. Design. Technology.**  
*The three pillars — works as a subtitle or supporting line.*

**Build. Grow. Scale.**  
*Our three service phases — used in service-oriented contexts.*

**Your Business Deserves Better Online.**  
*More empathetic, for audiences who know they have a problem.*

**The Digital Foundation Your Business Deserves.**  
*Emphasizes quality and worthiness — good for premium positioning.*

**From First Impression to Lasting Growth.**

## Industry-Specific Taglines

### Dental
**"Your Next Patient Is Searching Right Now."**  
Creates urgency, speaks directly to the dental practice's primary growth goal.

### Church
**"Reach More People. Serve Your Community Better."**  
Mission-aligned, outcome-focused, non-commercial tone appropriate for churches.

### Local Business
**"Compete Online. Win Locally."**  
Direct, competitive framing for local businesses who are losing ground to competitors.

### Startups
**"Launch With the Credibility of an Established Brand."**  
Addresses the startup's core insecurity — looking too new.

---

## Usage Guidelines

- The **primary tagline** is the default for all hero sections and pitch decks
- **Secondary taglines** can be used in supporting copy, email subject lines, or social posts
- **Industry taglines** are for industry-specific pages and campaigns only
- Never use more than one tagline in the same visual context
- Taglines should not be italicized or placed in quotes on the website

---

## Testing and Evolution

Taglines should be evaluated periodically against:
- Clarity: Does a first-time visitor immediately understand what we do?
- Relevance: Does it speak to what our current ideal clients care about?
- Differentiation: Could a competitor use this exact tagline?

Update this document when taglines are tested, changed, or retired.
