# Messaging

## Core Message

**Roman Creative Studio builds digital experiences that grow businesses.**

This is the single sentence that captures everything we do. Every other message expands on it.

---

## Message Architecture

### Level 1: Primary Message (5 words)
> *We build digital experiences that grow businesses.*

Used: Hero sections, elevator pitch, tagline contexts.

### Level 2: Supporting Statement (1–2 sentences)
> *Strategy, design, and technology for small businesses that are serious about growth. We build the digital foundation your business deserves — and make sure it performs.*

Used: Below hero, introduction paragraphs, email signatures.

### Level 3: Full Value Proposition (paragraph)
> *Most small businesses lose clients online before the relationship even starts — because their website doesn't reflect the quality of what they actually offer. Roman Creative Studio changes that. We combine business strategy, premium design, and technical excellence to build digital presences that attract the right clients, build real trust, and drive consistent growth.*

Used: About sections, proposals, detailed introductions.

## Key Proof Points

When clients ask "why Roman Creative Studio?", these are the evidence points we reach for:

1. **Custom-built, not template-based** — every project starts with the client's specific business goals
2. **Strategy first** — we understand the business before we touch design
3. **Technical quality** — fast, accessible, SEO-optimized from the ground up
4. **Portfolio evidence** — real projects, real results
5. **Clear process** — clients know what to expect at every stage
6. **Ongoing partnership** — we're here after launch, not just until it

---

## Messages to Avoid

- "We're passionate about design" — everyone says this
- "Results-driven digital solutions" — meaningless corporate speak
- "One-stop shop" — commoditizes our work
- "Affordable" — positions us as a budget option
- "World-class" — unverifiable and overused
