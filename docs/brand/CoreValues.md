# Core Values

These are not aspirational statements. These are operational commitments — they define how we work, who we hire, and what we build.

---

## 1. Excellence Over Volume

We would rather do fewer projects at a higher standard than more projects at an average standard.

This means:
- We turn down clients who aren't a good fit
- We don't rush timelines to squeeze in another project
- Every deliverable is reviewed against a high bar before it leaves our hands
- We invest in our own skills, tools, and processes continuously

---

## 2. Integrity in Every Interaction

We say what we mean. We do what we say. We tell clients the truth even when it's uncomfortable.

This means:
- We don't oversell — we scope projects honestly
- We communicate problems early, not at the end
- We protect client information and never share work without permission
- We give credit where it's due and take responsibility when we fall short

---

## 3. Strategy Before Aesthetics

Beautiful design that doesn't serve the business goal is decoration, not work.

This means:
- Every design decision is tied to a business outcome
- We ask "why" before we ask "how"
- We push back on requests that look good but don't serve the client
- We measure success by results, not compliments

---

## 4. Genuine Partnership

We treat every client's business as if it were our own.

This means:
- We proactively share insights even when not asked
- We celebrate client wins publicly (with permission)
- We stay engaged after launch, not just until handoff
- We build long-term relationships, not transactional engagements

---

## 5. Continuous Growth

The digital landscape changes constantly. We stay ahead of it.

This means:
- We actively study what's working in design, SEO, and conversion
- We invest in better tools and processes
- We document what we learn so the whole operation improves
- We're willing to change our minds when we find a better approach

---

## 6. Faith and Purpose

Roman Creative Studio is built on the belief that work done with integrity and excellence is meaningful work.

This means:
- We approach every project as an opportunity to create something genuinely good
- We serve clients across industries, including faith communities, with the same excellence we bring to every engagement
- We make decisions with a long-term, purpose-driven perspective
