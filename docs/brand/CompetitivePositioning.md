# Competitive Positioning

## Market Overview

The digital agency space is crowded but poorly differentiated. Most agencies compete on:
- Portfolio aesthetics
- Price
- Speed of delivery

Roman Creative Studio competes on:
- **Strategic depth** — we understand the business behind the website
- **Industry expertise** — we speak the language of specific client sectors
- **Partnership model** — we're invested in long-term outcomes, not project fees

---

## Competitive Landscape

### Tier 1: Enterprise Agencies
**Examples:** Major city agencies, national digital firms  
**Price:** $25,000–$250,000+  
**Strengths:** Deep teams, strong process, brand recognition  
**Weaknesses:** Not accessible to small businesses; slow; over-engineered  
**Our position:** We deliver comparable quality at 20–30% of the price for clients who don't need (or can't afford) a 15-person team

### Tier 2: Regional / Mid-Market Agencies
**Examples:** Local agencies with 5–20 employees  
**Price:** $8,000–$30,000  
**Strengths:** Professional, process-oriented  
**Weaknesses:** Often generalist; account management overhead drives up cost without adding value  
**Our position:** Leaner operation = lower overhead = better value. Same or better quality without the layers.

### Tier 3: Freelancers and Template Shops
**Examples:** Upwork, Fiverr, local web designers, Squarespace consultants  
**Price:** $500–$5,000  
**Strengths:** Fast, cheap  
**Weaknesses:** No strategy, inconsistent quality, no ongoing relationship, template limitations  
**Our position:** We're not competing here. Clients who outgrow this tier graduate to us.

### Platform DIY (Not Direct Competition)
**Examples:** Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy Website Builder  
**Price:** $0–$500/year  
**Our position:** We serve clients who've tried this and found it insufficient, or who understand from the start that DIY won't meet their goals
