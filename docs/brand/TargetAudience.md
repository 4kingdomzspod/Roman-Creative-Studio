# Target Audience

## Primary Audience

**Small business owners and operators who are serious about growth and understand that their digital presence is a business asset, not an expense.**

---

## Audience Segments

### Segment 1: Established Local Businesses
- **Who:** Dentists, chiropractors, med spas, law firms, accountants, contractors, restaurants, salons
- **Stage:** Established, generating revenue, ready to professionalize
- **Pain:** Current website is outdated, embarrassing, or not converting
- **Goal:** Look credible, attract better clients, compete with larger competitors
- **Budget:** $3,000–$10,000 for a project
- **Decision driver:** Trust and portfolio quality

### Segment 2: Growth-Stage Entrepreneurs
- **Who:** Founders building their first serious business, coaches, consultants, agency owners
- **Stage:** Revenue coming in, ready to invest in brand
- **Pain:** Pieced-together online presence doesn't match the quality of their service
- **Goal:** Build authority, attract premium clients, scale
- **Budget:** $2,000–$8,000
- **Decision driver:** Vision alignment and results-focus

### Segment 3: Churches and Nonprofits
- **Who:** Pastors, church administrators, ministry leaders, nonprofit executives
- **Stage:** Established organization, serving a community
- **Pain:** Website doesn't serve their mission — hard to update, doesn't connect people
- **Goal:** Reach more people, serve the community better, modernize presence
- **Budget:** $3,000–$8,000 (often committee-approved)
- **Decision driver:** Values alignment and recommendation from trusted source

### Segment 4: Startups and New Businesses
- **Who:** Founders who are launching a new company or product
- **Stage:** Pre-revenue or early revenue
- **Pain:** Need to look credible and attract first customers
- **Goal:** Launch professionally, attract investors or early customers
- **Budget:** $2,000–$6,000
- **Decision driver:** Speed and quality of portfolio

---

## Who Is NOT Our Target Audience

- Businesses looking for the cheapest possible option
- Clients who want to do it themselves with our guidance
- Clients who aren't willing to provide necessary materials or feedback
- Businesses with no clear goal for their digital presence
- Clients who treat design as purely cosmetic rather than strategic

---

## Psychographic Profile (All Segments)

**Values:** Quality, professionalism, results, trust  
**Attitude toward technology:** Understands its importance, not necessarily technical  
**Decision-making:** Research-driven, reference-checking, value-conscious (not price-shopping)  
**Communication preference:** Direct, clear, professional — no fluff  
**Fear:** Wasting money on something that doesn't work  
**Aspiration:** A business presence they're proud to share
