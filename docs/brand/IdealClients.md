# Ideal Clients

**Roman Creative Studio Brand Bible — Vol. 1, Part A**
*Version 1.0 | Last Updated: June 2026*

---

## Purpose of This Document

Not every business that can pay us is an ideal client. This document defines the profile of the clients Roman Creative Studio is built for — and the profile of clients we should decline. Using this during the qualification process protects our time, our quality, and our reputation.

---

## The Ideal Client Profile

### They Are Serious About Growth
They're not looking for a website because they think they're supposed to have one. They're looking for a partner because they understand their digital presence is directly connected to their business outcomes. They have goals they can articulate.

### They Invest in Their Business
They understand that quality work has a price — and that the price of poor-quality work is higher. They are not comparing us to the cheapest option. They are evaluating value, not cost.

**Budget expectation:** Minimum $2,500 for project work. Care Plans from $150/month.

### They Communicate Well
They respond within 1–2 business days. They give honest feedback. They make decisions without requiring months of committee approval. They treat the project as a priority, even when life is busy.

### They Have a Clear Goal
They can answer: "What does success look like in 6 months?" It might be more patients, more leads, stronger credibility, or ranking on the first page of Google. But they know what they want.

### They Respect Professional Judgment
They hired us because we know what we're doing. They give input — that's their right — but they don't micromanage design decisions based on personal aesthetic preference alone. They can distinguish between "I don't like this" and "this doesn't serve our goal."

### They Are Building Something Real
They are not looking for a side project website or a temporary landing page. They are building a real business or organization and treating their digital presence accordingly.

### They Want a Partnership, Not a Transaction
They're interested in a long-term relationship — not a one-time vendor hire. The best clients are ones who grow with us: starting with a website, moving to a Care Plan, expanding to SEO, and treating Roman Creative Studio as their ongoing digital partner.

## Business Maturity

Ideal clients are typically in one of two stages:

**Established and ready to professionalize**
They've been in business 2+ years. Revenue is coming in. They've reached the point where their digital presence is holding them back, and they know it.

**Launching with serious intent**
They are new but capitalized and clear-eyed about the importance of starting right. They have a real offer, a real market, and a real budget allocated to building a professional foundation.

## Who Is Not an Ideal Client

### The Price Shopper
Their primary question is "how much?" They've gotten quotes from five people and will make their decision based on whoever is cheapest. This client is not evaluating value — they're minimizing cost. Our work cannot compete on that metric, and we shouldn't try.

### The Indecisive Approver
Every decision requires consensus from a group that never agrees. Feedback takes weeks. Revision rounds multiply. The project timeline stretches indefinitely. This client does not value our time.

### The Micromanager
They want to approve every font, pixel, and line of code. They don't trust professional judgment. They hired an expert but will override the expert at every turn. The project will not meet our quality standard — because it won't be ours.

### The Serial Vendor
They've worked with 4 agencies in 3 years. Each one "didn't deliver." The pattern suggests the problem is not the agencies. We should politely decline.

### The Unrealistic Timeline Client
They need it in a week. There is no flexibility. Compromising the timeline means compromising the quality. We don't do that.

### The Strategy-Resistant Client
They don't want to talk about goals, customers, or strategy. They just want it to "look modern." Beautiful without purpose is decoration. We're not a decoration service.

### The Scope Creep Client
After signing, they consistently add requirements without acknowledging they are changes. Their vision of the project is always larger than what was contracted. Managing this relationship becomes unsustainable.

## Qualification Questions

Use these in every discovery call to identify fit:

1. What is the primary goal for this project? What does success look like in 6 months?
2. What's driving the timing — is there a specific deadline?
3. Have you worked with a designer or agency before? What was that experience?
4. What's your budget range for this project?
5. Who else is involved in making this decision, and can we involve them early?
6. What are your expectations for the process — how involved do you want to be?

## Green Flags

- They've done research before the call (they know our work)
- They have a clear goal, not just a vague desire to "improve" the website
- They respond promptly and professionally to initial outreach
- They're willing to share what hasn't worked before
- They ask about our process, not just our price
- They mention wanting an ongoing relationship
