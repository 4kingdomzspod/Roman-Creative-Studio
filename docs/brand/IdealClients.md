# Ideal Clients

## The Ideal Roman Creative Studio Client

Our best clients share a common profile regardless of industry. Understanding this profile helps us qualify leads faster and design a better experience for everyone.

---

## Profile: The Serious Owner

**They understand that a website is an investment, not an expense.**  
They're not looking for the cheapest option. They've seen what poor digital presence costs them in lost customers and damaged credibility.

**They have a clear goal.**  
They know what they want the website to accomplish — more appointments booked, more donations, more leads, more credibility. They may not know how to achieve it, but they know what success looks like.

**They're decisive and organized.**  
They can get us what we need — logo files, photos, content direction — on a reasonable timeline. They respect the process and make decisions without weeks of delay.

**They trust professional judgment.**  
They hire us because we know what we're doing. They give feedback but don't micromanage design decisions. They understand the difference between personal preference and strategic effectiveness.

**They value the relationship.**  
They're not looking for a one-time vendor. They want a partner who understands their business and can continue to help them grow.

## Industry Ideal Clients

### Dental Practices
- Solo or group practice (1–5 dentists)
- Already generating revenue, looking to grow new patient volume
- May be acquiring a practice or rebranding after a name change
- Wants to attract fee-for-service patients, not just insurance-dependent volume

### Churches
- Growing congregation (50–500 members)
- Leadership that understands digital outreach matters
- Has a desire to reach new people in their community
- May have outgrown a template site or a site built by a volunteer

### Local Service Businesses
- Contractor, plumber, landscaper, HVAC — generating $500K–$5M annually
- Relying primarily on word of mouth, ready to scale with digital presence
- Competes with franchise operations and wants to look equally credible

### Startups / Entrepreneurs
- Have a validated offer (not just an idea)
- Ready to invest in brand and online presence
- Understand that their digital credibility affects their ability to close deals

## Red Flags (Clients to Decline)

- **Price-only decision:** "Who's the cheapest?" is their primary question
- **No clear goal:** Can't articulate what success looks like
- **Control issues:** Wants to approve every font choice and pixel position
- **History of vendor-hopping:** Has worked with 3+ agencies in 2 years
- **Unrealistic timeline:** Needs it in a week with no flexibility
- **Scope creep tendency:** Keeps adding requirements after project has started

---

## Qualification Questions

Use these in discovery calls to identify fit:

1. What's the primary goal for this project? What does success look like in 6 months?
2. What's your timeline, and what's driving it?
3. Have you worked with a designer or agency before? What was that experience like?
4. What's your budget range for this project?
5. Who else is involved in making this decision?
