## Purpose

Define how Roman Creative Studio sets, cascades, and measures Objectives and Key Results at every level of the organization.

**Business Value:** OKRs create alignment without bureaucracy. When every person understands how their work connects to company goals, effort is concentrated on what matters. OKRs replace status meetings with outcome tracking.

---

## OKR Fundamentals

### What Is an Objective?
An inspiring, qualitative statement of what you want to achieve. Objectives should:
- Be ambitious but achievable
- Be meaningful and motivating (not just a number)
- Be clear enough that anyone reading it understands the goal
- Not be a task ("Launch X") but an outcome ("Become the go-to agency for dental practices")

### What Is a Key Result?
A specific, measurable outcome that proves you achieved the objective. Key Results should:
- Be quantifiable (a number, a percentage, a binary yes/no)
- Be clearly measurable (no ambiguity about whether it's achieved)
- Be 2–4 per objective
- Be outcomes, not outputs ("Client NPS > 8.0" not "Send NPS surveys")

### What OKRs Are NOT
- A task list
- A job description
- A guarantee (70% achievement is healthy — 100% means targets were too easy)
- A performance review tool (OKRs measure team/company performance, not individuals)

## OKR Levels

```
COMPANY OKRs
(Set annually, reviewed quarterly)
       ↓
DEPARTMENT OKRs
(Set quarterly, aligned to company OKRs)
       ↓
TEAM / PROJECT OKRs
(Set quarterly, aligned to department OKRs)
       ↓
INDIVIDUAL OKRs
(Set quarterly, aligned to team + personal development)
```

**Cascade Rule:** Every OKR at lower levels must support at least one OKR at the level above it.

## Setting Good OKRs: The RCS Standard

### The Objective Test
A good objective answers YES to all of these:
- [ ] Is it inspiring? Would a team member feel energized by it?
- [ ] Is it directional? Does it point somewhere specific?
- [ ] Is it achievable in the timeframe?
- [ ] Is it outcome-focused (not activity-focused)?

### The Key Result Test
A good KR answers YES to all of these:
- [ ] Is it measurable? Is there a number?
- [ ] Is there no ambiguity about whether it's achieved?
- [ ] Is it achievable but ambitious? (60–70% confidence you'll hit it)
- [ ] Is it an outcome, not a task?
- [ ] Can it be tracked monthly or more frequently?

### Common OKR Mistakes

| Mistake | Wrong | Right |
|---------|-------|-------|
| Output not outcome | "Publish 12 blog posts" | "Organic traffic grows 40%" |
| Activity not result | "Run 20 client calls" | "Close 6 new projects" |
| Vague target | "Improve client satisfaction" | "Achieve NPS ≥ 8.0" |
| Too easy | "Keep MRR above $0" | "Grow MRR from $500 to $2,000" |
| Too many | 10 OKRs per department | 3 OKRs max per level |

## OKR Scoring

### Scoring Scale
| Score | Meaning |
|-------|----------|
| 1.0 | Fully achieved — exceptional |
| 0.7–0.9 | Substantially achieved — great work |
| 0.5–0.6 | Partially achieved — made progress |
| 0.3–0.4 | Below target — needs review |
| 0.0–0.2 | Not achieved — investigate root cause |

### Target Distribution
- Average company OKR score: 0.65–0.75 is healthy
- Consistently 1.0 = targets are too easy; raise ambition
- Consistently <0.5 = targets are unrealistic or execution is broken

### Scoring Honesty
- OKR scores are honest, not aspirational
- No partial credit for effort — KRs are pass/fail on the number
- Explanations for misses are mandatory; blame is not

## OKR Review Process

### Weekly (Individual Level)
- Each person reviews their KRs at start of week
- Updates progress in Notion OKR tracker
- Flags any KRs turning Red (at risk)

### Monthly (Department Level)
- Department head reviews all team KRs
- Identifies Red KRs and escalates if needed
- Adjusts plans if blockers are identified

### Quarterly (Company Level)
- Full OKR grading session (see QuarterlyPlanning.md)
- CEO presents company OKR scores to full team
- Celebration of wins; honest post-mortem on misses

## Dependencies Tracking

When one OKR depends on another team's output:

**Format:**
```
KR1.2 depends on: Engineering completing TOOL-03 by Month 5
Risk if dependency misses: KR1.2 delayed by 1 quarter
Mitigation: Interim manual process documented
```

Dependencies are reviewed at mid-quarter check-in.
