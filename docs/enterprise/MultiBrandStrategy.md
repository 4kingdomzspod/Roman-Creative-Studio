## Purpose

Define the architecture and governance for Roman Creative Studio's brand portfolio — establishing how the master brand relates to sub-brands, product brands, and future independent brands as the company evolves from a single-brand agency to a multi-brand creative platform.

**Business Value:** Unplanned brand proliferation creates confusion, dilutes the master brand, and creates operational complexity. A deliberate multi-brand strategy ensures every new brand serves a strategic purpose, maintains quality standards, and either strengthens the RCS ecosystem or stands independently when appropriate.

---

## Brand Architecture Philosophy

### The Branded House vs. House of Brands

| Model | What It Is | Example | When to Use |
|-------|-----------|---------|-------------|
| **Branded House** | One master brand; all products are sub-brands | Google (Google Maps, Google Drive) | When master brand is the value |
| **House of Brands** | Portfolio of independent brands | P&G (Tide, Pampers, Gillette) | When segments require different positioning |
| **Hybrid** | Master brand + some independent brands | Apple (Apple + Beats) | When both apply at different stages |

**RCS Strategy:** Start as a Branded House. Evolve toward Hybrid as product and education brands develop distinct audiences.

**Positioning:** The premier creative technology agency for service businesses

## Shared Brand Standards (All Brands)

Regardless of how independent each brand becomes, these standards apply to all:

1. **Quality floor:** Every brand must maintain the same production quality as RCS agency work
2. **Accessibility:** All brand assets and digital experiences meet WCAG 2.1 AA
3. **Privacy:** All brands follow the RCS privacy framework
4. **Values alignment:** No brand under the RCS umbrella can contradict our core values
5. **Financial accountability:** Each brand has its own P&L; no cross-subsidization without CEO approval

## Brand Launch Criteria

Before launching any new brand or sub-brand:

- [ ] Clear audience defined (different from existing brands)
- [ ] Clear positioning defined (not competing with existing brands)
- [ ] Naming and visual identity approved by Creative Director
- [ ] Domain registered
- [ ] Brand standards documented
- [ ] Revenue model defined
- [ ] Launch trigger metric met
- [ ] CEO approval
