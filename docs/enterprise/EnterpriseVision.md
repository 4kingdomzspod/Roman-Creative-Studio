## Purpose

This document defines what Roman Creative Studio is becoming — not just what it is today. It is the north star that guides every strategic decision, every hire, every product, and every client relationship across decades.

**Business Value:** Without a long-range enterprise vision, organizations optimize for the short term and lose their identity as they grow. This document ensures every stage of growth is intentional, values-aligned, and architecturally sound.

---

## The Enterprise Definition

Roman Creative Studio is not a freelance shop, not a boutique agency, not a SaaS startup. It is a **creative technology company** — one that delivers premium services, builds scalable products, educates the next generation of designers, and advocates for the web being accessible, beautiful, and effective for every business.

**What we are building:**
- A world-class agency with a national reputation
- A digital product ecosystem trusted by designers and businesses
- An education platform that shapes how the next generation builds the web
- A community that is the home base for creative professionals building service businesses
- Software that solves real problems for agencies and small businesses
- A company that operates without founder dependency within 10 years

## Enterprise Mission

> Roman Creative Studio exists to make small and mid-size service businesses impossible to ignore online — through design that earns attention, systems that build trust, and technology that creates lasting results.

**Why this matters beyond revenue:** Thousands of local businesses — dental practices, construction companies, churches, law firms, healthcare providers — are losing clients to competitors with better websites. We level that playing field. Every site we build changes someone's livelihood.

## Core Values (Enterprise Level)

These values govern not just how we work today, but how every future leader, product, and system must behave:

### 1. Craftsmanship Over Volume
We build fewer things, better. We will never sacrifice quality for growth. Every client, every product, every document, every line of code reflects our standard.

### 2. Long-Term Thinking
We make decisions for decades, not quarters. We plant trees whose shade we may not sit under. We build systems that outlast the people who built them.

### 3. Radical Clarity
We communicate with precision. Our documentation is so clear that anyone can pick it up and execute. Our client communication is so honest that clients never wonder what's happening.

### 4. Inclusive Excellence
We build for everyone. Accessibility is not a checklist — it is a commitment that the web we build is usable by every human who encounters it.

### 5. People Before Profit
Clients, team members, and community come before quarterly numbers. Sustainable profitability is the result of serving people exceptionally well.

### 6. Ownership Culture
Every person at RCS owns their domain as if it were their company. We hire people who take initiative, document their work, and hold themselves accountable without being managed.

### 7. Innovation Without Chaos
We experiment deliberately. We adopt technology when it serves our mission, not because it's new. We innovate in structured lanes so the core business never suffers.

### 8. Legacy Thinking
We build things meant to last. Our clients' websites should still work in 10 years. Our documentation should guide a hire we haven't met yet. Our culture should survive leadership transitions.

## Enterprise Stages

### Stage 1: Agency (Current — Year 1–2)
- Revenue driver: Services
- Team: Founder + contractors
- Focus: First clients, first MRR, first products
- Success signal: $10k/mo consistent revenue

### Stage 2: Studio (Year 2–3)
- Revenue driver: Services + Products + Community
- Team: 2–4 people
- Focus: Systematizing delivery, launching education, MRR architecture
- Success signal: $30k/mo, >30% from non-service revenue

### Stage 3: Platform (Year 3–5)
- Revenue driver: Diversified (services, products, courses, SaaS)
- Team: 5–10 people
- Focus: SaaS launch, community growth, national presence
- Success signal: $100k/mo, >50% recurring revenue

### Stage 4: Company (Year 5–8)
- Revenue driver: Platform + Enterprise Agency
- Team: 10–25 people
- Focus: Management layer, systems autonomy, geographic expansion
- Success signal: $500k/mo, founder works on company not in it

### Stage 5: Institution (Year 8–15)
- Revenue driver: Full ecosystem (all 5 pillars)
- Team: 25–75 people
- Focus: Legacy, culture, possible acquisition or IPO positioning
- Success signal: $1M+/mo, operates without founder involvement

## Non-Negotiables

Regardless of scale, revenue, or external pressure, these cannot change:

1. **WCAG 2.1 AA minimum** on every site we build — always
2. **Domain:** romancreativestudio.co — the .co is intentional and permanent
3. **Contact:** Alexander@romancreativestudio.co — founder email stays as long as founder is active
4. **Service quality floor:** BUILD tier minimum $3,500, GROW $6,500, SCALE $12,000+
5. **Care plan quality:** Every care plan client gets a real human point of contact
6. **Documentation standard:** If it's not documented, it doesn't exist as a process
7. **Client honesty:** We never oversell, never hide problems, never blame clients
8. **Culture:** We do not hire people who don't share our values, regardless of skill
