# Brand Governance System — Overview
**Roman Creative Studio — Brand Control & Consistency Engine**
_Last Updated: 2025 | Version 1.0_

---

## Purpose

This is the enforcement layer of Roman Creative Studio. The Brand Governance System exists for one purpose: to ensure that every output — digital or print, internal or client-facing, designed or written — reflects the same brand, at the same quality standard, every time.

Phase 2A established the brand strategy.
Phase 2B established the visual identity system.
**Phase 2C enforces both.**

---

## Core Governance Principle

> **Consistency is the product. Every inconsistency is a quality defect.**

Brand governance is not bureaucracy. It is craftsmanship infrastructure. The rules in this system exist because:

1. Inconsistency erodes trust.
2. Trust is the product Roman Creative Studio sells.
3. Therefore, inconsistency is a direct threat to the business.

---

## Mandatory vs. Advisory

Every rule in this system is **mandatory** unless explicitly labeled `[ADVISORY]`.

There are no optional brand guidelines. Guidelines that are optional are not guidelines — they are suggestions. Roman Creative Studio operates to a higher standard.
