# Business Asset Guidelines
**Roman Creative Studio — Brand Governance System**
_Last Updated: 2025 | Version 1.0_

---

## Purpose

Define how the RCS brand appears in all business documents — proposals, contracts, invoices, presentations, case studies, and email signatures. Business documents are client-facing. A poorly branded proposal undermines the value proposition of an agency that sells premium design. Every document must look as polished as the work it represents.

> **A proposal IS a design deliverable. Treat it as such.**

---

## 1. Universal Document Rules

These apply to every business document RCS produces:

1. **Dark brand theme only.** All RCS business documents use the dark brand palette. No light-background alternatives.
2. **Logo on every page.** Either the Full Lockup (cover/header) or the Wordmark/Icon (footer/corner).
3. **Brand fonts only.** Cormorant Garamond and Inter. No system fonts in client-facing documents.
4. **Brand colors only.** The `--color-*` token palette. No document-specific color overrides.
5. **Professional tone always.** Even informal check-in documents reflect brand voice.
6. **Version-controlled.** All documents have a version number and date.
7. **PDF delivery only.** Final client-facing documents are always delivered as PDF. Never `.docx` or `.pptx` directly.

## 2. Proposals

### Structure

Every proposal must include these sections in this order:
1. Cover page
2. The problem we're solving (client context)
3. Our approach / process overview
4. Scope of work (detailed)
5. Investment (pricing)
6. Timeline
7. About Roman Creative Studio
8. Next steps / CTA
9. Terms and conditions (abbreviated; full in contract)

### Design Rules

**Cover Page:**
- Background: `#0C0E11`
- Logo: Full Lockup, centered, large (minimum 240px wide)
- Client name or project title: Cormorant Garamond, `--text-4xl` or larger, gold or white
- Tagline or project description: Inter, `--text-lg`, `--color-text-muted`
- Date: Inter, `--text-sm`, `--color-text-subtle`, bottom center

**Interior Pages:**
- Background: `#0C0E11` or `#1B1E23` for alternating section effect
- Section headings: Cormorant Garamond, `--text-3xl`, white or gold
- Body copy: Inter, `--text-base`, `--color-text`
- Callout boxes: `--color-surface-elevated` background, `--color-border-brand` border, gold icon
- Pricing table: Clean grid, gold header row, alternating surface/muted rows
- Page numbers: Inter, `--text-xs`, bottom center, `--color-text-subtle`

**Header (interior pages):**
- Left: Wordmark or Horizontal Lockup, small
- Right: Document title ("Proposal for [Client Name]")
- Separator: `--color-border` horizontal rule below header

**Footer (interior pages):**
- Left: `Alexander@romancreativestudio.co` | `romancreativestudio.co`
- Right: Page number and total ("Page 3 of 12")
- Separator: `--color-border` horizontal rule above footer

### Typography in Proposals

| Element | Font | Size Token | Weight | Color |
|---------|------|-----------|--------|-------|
| Cover headline | Cormorant Garamond | `--text-5xl` | 600 | White or gold |
| Section H2 | Cormorant Garamond | `--text-3xl` | 600 | White |
| Section H3 | Inter | `--text-xl` | 600 | White |
| Body copy | Inter | `--text-base` | 400 | `--color-text` |
| Captions / labels | Inter | `--text-sm` | 500 | `--color-text-muted` |
| Price figures | Inter | `--text-2xl` | 700 | `--color-brand-gold` |
| Fine print | Inter | `--text-xs` | 400 | `--color-text-subtle` |

## 3. Contracts

### Design Rules

Contracts prioritize legibility and legal clarity over design expression. The brand is present but subdued.

- **Background:** `#0C0E11` or clean white (contracts may use light background for legal readability)
- **Logo:** Horizontal Lockup, top-left, small
- **Body copy:** Inter, `--text-base`, 1.7 line-height for dense legal text
- **Section headings:** Inter, `--text-lg`, weight 600 — not Cormorant (legal precision over elegance)
- **Signature blocks:** Clearly demarcated with `--color-border` lines, ample space
- **No decorative graphics in body.** Clean, professional, uncluttered.

### Tone

- Clear and professional. No jargon.
- Direct about terms — do not obscure important clauses in complex language.
- Review contract copy against `docs/brand/BrandVoice.md` — Contractual/Professional tone mode.

## 4. Invoices

### Structure

1. Header: Logo + "Invoice" label + Invoice number + Date
2. Billing: From (RCS) and To (client) addresses
3. Line items: Service descriptions + quantities + rates + subtotals
4. Summary: Subtotal + any applicable taxes + total due
5. Payment terms and due date
6. Payment instructions
7. Footer: Contact info + thank-you message

### Design Rules

- **Clean and scannable** — invoices must be easy to process at a glance
- **Logo:** Horizontal Lockup, top-left
- **"Invoice" label:** Cormorant Garamond or Inter Bold, large, right-aligned in header
- **Total due:** Prominent, gold (`--color-brand-gold`), large (`--text-3xl`)
- **Line items table:** `--color-surface` rows, `--color-border` borders, Inter `--text-sm`
- **Due date:** Prominently displayed, Inter Bold

## 5. Presentations

### Slide Types

| Slide Type | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Title slide** | Cover: Full Lockup + presentation title + presenter name + date |
| **Section divider** | Dark full-bleed with section title in Cormorant Garamond, gold accent |
| **Content slide** | Heading + body copy/bullets or visual |
| **Data/chart slide** | Heading + graph/chart with brand-colored data visualization |
| **Quote slide** | Large Cormorant Garamond pull quote, gold accent mark |
| **Full-bleed visual** | Photography or screenshot, full-bleed with minimal text overlay |
| **Closing slide** | Full Lockup + CTA + contact info |

### Design Rules

- **Slide dimensions:** 16:9 (1920 × 1080px) standard
- **Background:** `#0C0E11` primary, `#1B1E23` for alternating content slides
- **One idea per slide.** Do not pack slides with text.
- **Bullet points:** Maximum 5 bullets per slide. Maximum 10 words per bullet.
- **Logo:** Small Horizontal Lockup or Icon Mark, bottom-right, every slide except full-bleed
- **No built-in presentation themes.** Never use PowerPoint or Google Slides default themes.
- **Animations:** Slide transitions maximum: fade (250ms). No fly-ins, bounces, or spin effects.

### Typography in Presentations

| Element | Font | Size | Weight |
|---------|------|------|--------|
| Title slide headline | Cormorant Garamond | 48–72px | 600 |
| Section divider title | Cormorant Garamond | 40–56px | 600 |
| Slide heading | Inter or Cormorant | 28–36px | 600 |
| Body / bullets | Inter | 18–24px | 400 |
| Caption / source | Inter | 12–14px | 400 |
| Data callout | Inter | 32–48px | 700 |

## 6. Case Studies

### Structure

1. Cover: Client name / project title + cover image
2. Challenge: What the client needed to solve
3. Our approach: What we did and why
4. Solution: What we built / designed
5. Results: Measurable outcomes (with permission)
6. Testimonial (if available)
7. Project details: Industry, services, timeline
8. CTA: View live site / Book a call

### Design Rules

- Use Category B social template aesthetic (work-forward, design-focused)
- Client work screenshots must be full-quality, retina-ready
- Results are stated in specifics: "42% increase in leads" not "significant improvement"
- Client testimonials use large Cormorant Garamond pull-quote treatment with attribution

## 7. Email Signatures

### Required Elements

```
[Name]
[Title] | Roman Creative Studio
[Phone] | Alexander@romancreativestudio.co
romancreativestudio.co
[Optional: LinkedIn URL]
```

### Design Rules

- **Plain text version:** Always available as fallback
- **HTML version:** Dark background (`#0C0E11`), Inter font (with system font fallback), white text, gold name or title
- **Logo:** Small Horizontal Lockup above or beside contact info
- **No animated GIFs in email signatures.**
- **No marketing banners in every email signature.** Promotional banners are acceptable on campaign emails only, not routine correspondence.
- **Maximum width:** 600px
- **Tone:** Professional. The signature does not include motivational quotes or personal taglines.

### Signature HTML Pattern

```html
<table style="font-family: Inter, -apple-system, sans-serif; background: #0C0E11; padding: 16px; border-radius: 8px; border: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08);">
  <tr>
    <td style="padding-right: 16px; border-right: 1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08);">
      <img src="[logo-url]" alt="Roman Creative Studio" width="120" height="auto" />
    </td>
    <td style="padding-left: 16px;">
      <p style="margin: 0; color: #D4AF37; font-weight: 600; font-size: 15px;">[Name]</p>
      <p style="margin: 4px 0; color: rgba(240,239,233,0.65); font-size: 13px;">[Title] | Roman Creative Studio</p>
      <p style="margin: 8px 0 0; color: rgba(240,239,233,0.65); font-size: 13px;">Alexander@romancreativestudio.co | romancreativestudio.co</p>
    </td>
  </tr>
</table>
```

## 8. PDFs and Exported Documents

- All client-facing documents are exported as PDF — never delivered as editable source files unless explicitly contracted
- PDF metadata must be set: Title, Author (Roman Creative Studio), Creator
- Fonts must be embedded in PDF
- No password protection on standard delivery documents (use secure file transfer instead)
- File naming follows `FileAssetManagement.md` conventions
