# Social Media Branding Rules
**Roman Creative Studio — Brand Governance System**
_Last Updated: 2025 | Version 1.0_

---

## Purpose

Define how Roman Creative Studio presents itself across all social media platforms. Social media is often the first brand touchpoint for a potential client. Every post, profile, and piece of content must reflect the same premium, consistent brand identity — regardless of platform trends, "viral" formats, or algorithm pressure.

> **Social content must always look like it came from the same brand. If it doesn't, it doesn't go out.**

---

## 1. Non-Negotiable Social Brand Rules

Before any platform-specific rules: these apply everywhere, always.

1. **The dark palette is non-negotiable.** Social graphics use the RCS dark background system. No white or light backgrounds on RCS brand content.
2. **Gold is the only accent color.** No substitute colors. No trend-driven palette experiments.
3. **Inter for all UI text in graphics.** Cormorant Garamond for display headlines at large size only.
4. **The logo appears on every piece of content.** Bottom-right corner minimum. Never omitted.
5. **No clipart, no generic stock illustrations, no cartoon icons.** Heroicons only for iconography. Photography follows the Photography System.
6. **No fonts outside the brand system.** No "cool trending fonts" for social graphics.
7. **Captions must match brand voice.** The BrandVoice and ToneOfVoice documents govern all written content.

## 2. Profile Image Rules

**Approved profile image:** The Social Profile Version of the RCS logo (square 1:1 ratio).

| Platform | Recommended Export Size | Display Size | Notes |
|----------|------------------------|-------------|-------|
| Instagram | 800 × 800px | 110px circle | Will be cropped to circle — ensure logo has adequate margin |
| LinkedIn | 800 × 800px | 300px circle | Same circular crop — leave 15% safe zone |
| YouTube | 800 × 800px | 88px–250px | Circular on most surfaces |
| TikTok | 800 × 800px | 200px circle | Circular crop |
| Twitter/X | 800 × 800px | 400px circle | Circular |
| Facebook | 800 × 800px | 170px circle | Circular |

### Profile Image Rules
- ✅ Icon mark on dark brand background (`#0C0E11`)
- ✅ Gold border or glow treatment to distinguish from platform chrome
- ❌ No wordmark in profile image (too small to read at display size)
- ❌ No seasonal modifications to the profile image
- ❌ No frame overlays (causes profile image to look unofficial)
- ❌ Never use a photo as the RCS profile image on any platform

### Platform Specifications

| YouTube | 2560 × 1440px | Center 1546 × 423px | TV/desktop/mobile areas differ |
| Twitter/X | 1500 × 500px | Center 1200 × 400px | Profile overlaps bottom-left |
| Facebook Page | 851 × 315px | Center 640 × 315px | |
| TikTok | No banner | — | Profile bio only |

### Banner Design Rules

- **Background:** `--color-bg` (#0C0E11) or dark photography with overlay
- **Logo:** Full Lockup or Horizontal Lockup, positioned in safe zone
- **Typography:** Optional tagline in Inter, `--text-lg` or larger
- **Gold accent:** One gold element — either a line, glow, or highlighted text
- **Updated:** Banners updated maximum 4× per year. No reactive banner changes for trends.
- **Prohibited:** Stock photo montages, text-heavy banners, multicolor backgrounds

## 4. Post Template Consistency Rules

### Three Approved Post Template Categories

#### Category A: Content / Value Posts
Sharing insights, tips, or educational content.

```
Layout: Dark background (#0C0E11 or #1B1E23)
Headline: Cormorant Garamond, large (30px+), white or gold
Body copy (if any): Inter, 16px+, --color-text
Gold accent: One line, divider, or highlighted phrase
Logo: Bottom-right, small (Horizontal or Icon mark)
Tag/eyebrow: ALL CAPS Inter, --text-xs, --color-brand-gold, --tracking-widest
```

#### Category B: Showcase / Portfolio Posts
Displaying client work or RCS projects.

```
Layout: Full or near-full bleed client work screenshot/photo
Overlay: Subtle dark gradient at bottom (rgba(12,14,17,0.65))
Text: Client name or project type in white Inter
Logo: Bottom-right with 16px clear space
Subtle gold border or accent: Optional
No busy text blocks — let the work speak
```

#### Category C: Promotional / CTA Posts
Service offerings, promotions, booking prompts.

```
Layout: Dark background, structured grid
Headline: Cormorant Garamond, large, white or gold
CTA text: Bold Inter, gold or white
Button visual (if shown): Primary gold button style
Logo: Prominent, top or center
Gold: Can be more prominent on this template type only
```

### What Is Not a Template

- **Trend-reactive content** (e.g., jumping on a meme format) is only permitted if it can be executed within the brand system. If the format requires breaking brand rules, the format is declined.
- **Reels/TikTok graphics** follow the same rules even when designed for vertical format
- **Story slides** follow the same rules at 1080 × 1920px

## 5. Typography Rules for Social Graphics

| Element | Font | Weight | Size Range | Color |
|---------|------|--------|-----------|-------|
| Post headline | Cormorant Garamond | 600–700 | 32–64px | White or Gold |
| Subheadline | Inter | 500–600 | 18–24px | White |
| Body copy in graphic | Inter | 400 | 14–18px | --color-text or --color-text-muted |
| Label / eyebrow | Inter | 600 | 10–12px (ALL CAPS) | --color-brand-gold |
| CTA text | Inter | 700 | 14–18px | --color-brand-gold or white |
| Caption/credit | Inter | 400 | 10–12px | --color-text-subtle |

**Rules:**
- No more than three type sizes in a single graphic
- No more than two font families in a single graphic (Cormorant + Inter is the only permitted combination)
- Never use ALL CAPS for Cormorant Garamond — it was designed as a mixed-case typeface
- Never use Inter at display sizes without explicit design review

## 6. Color Usage Rules for Social Content

### Approved Palette for Social

| Color | Hex | Approved Use |
|-------|-----|-------------|
| Brand gold | `#D4AF37` | Headlines, accents, CTAs, dividers |
| Warm white | `#F0EFE9` | Body text, headings on dark bg |
| Page background | `#0C0E11` | Primary background |
| Surface | `#1B1E23` | Secondary background, card areas |
| Gold subtle | `rgba(212,175,55,0.12)` | Subtle tint areas |

### Prohibited on Social

- ❌ White or light backgrounds (unless explicitly a Light Mode branded post, clearly labeled)
- ❌ Any color not in the brand token system
- ❌ Multiple accent colors in the same graphic
- ❌ Using error red, success green, or warning amber as design accents
- ❌ Neon colors, gradient rainbow fills, oversaturated palettes

## 7. Platform-Specific Rules

### Instagram
- **Grid aesthetic:** The 9-grid should be visually cohesive when viewed as a whole. Dark backgrounds ensure this.
- **Reels thumbnails:** Must follow Category B or C template rules
- **Stories:** 1080 × 1920px. Same brand rules apply. Story content expires in 24h — still must meet brand standards.
- **Bio:** Professional, concise. Must include `romancreativestudio.co` link.

### LinkedIn
- **Post format:** Professional tone (see ToneOfVoice.md — Professional/Educational modes)
- **Article headers:** Full brand graphic following Category A template
- **No memes.** LinkedIn is a professional network. Humor is acceptable; meme formats are not.
- **Company page updates:** Must be reviewed before publishing. Not posted reactively.

### YouTube
- **Thumbnails:** Dark background, large clear Inter or Cormorant headline, logo bottom-right
- **End cards:** Brand-consistent. Dark background, channel name in brand system type.
- **Video lower-thirds:** Inter font, dark semi-transparent background, white text
- **No yellow thumbnail outlines** or other YouTube-trend elements that break the brand aesthetic

### TikTok
- **Lower-thirds / text overlays:** Inter only. Native TikTok fonts are prohibited on branded content.
- **Branded content:** Starts with RCS logo reveal or consistent intro motion
- **Trending audio:** Acceptable to use. The visual identity still governs — never compromise visuals for trend participation.
- **Personal vs. brand:** The RCS TikTok represents the brand. No unbranded personal content on the brand account.

## 8. Website Preview / OG Image Rules

Open Graph images appear when RCS links are shared on social media. They must be on-brand.

**OG Image Specification:**
- Size: 1200 × 630px
- Background: `#0C0E11`
- Logo: Full Lockup, centered or left-aligned
- Tagline or page headline: Cormorant Garamond or Inter, white
- Gold accent element: Optional
- No third-party logos or imagery without permission

**Per-page OG images** are preferred over a single global OG image. Each service page, blog post, and landing page should have a unique OG image following the above spec.
