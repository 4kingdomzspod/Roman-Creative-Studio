## Purpose

Define when, how, and why Roman Creative Studio holds meetings — ensuring every meeting has clear purpose, runs efficiently, and results in documented outcomes.

**Business Value:** Meetings are expensive. A 1-hour meeting with 4 people costs 4 hours of productivity. This document eliminates unnecessary meetings and maximizes the value of necessary ones.

**Owner:** CEO / Operations Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** CommunicationStandards.md, KnowledgeBase.md, CompanyCulture.md

---

## Meeting Principles

1. **No agenda = no meeting.** Every meeting requires a written agenda sent at least 24 hours in advance.
2. **Async first.** If a decision or update can be communicated via Slack or Notion, it should be. Don't schedule a meeting to share information that belongs in a document.
3. **Start on time. End on time.** Respecting time is respecting people.
4. **Notes are mandatory.** Every meeting ends with written notes and action items. If no one can take notes, the meeting doesn't happen.
5. **Standing meetings need standing value.** Recurring meetings are audited quarterly. Remove any that no longer provide value.
6. **Minimum viable invite list.** Only invite people who need to be there to make a decision or contribute meaningfully. Observers are async.

## Meeting Types

### 1. Daily Standup

**When:** Async (Slack) or live at 9:00 AM (Stage 3+ with active team)  
**Duration:** 15 minutes max (live) or 5-minute Slack post (async)  
**Who:** All active project team members  
**Cadence:** Monday–Friday during active projects

**Format (Slack async):**
```
**Yesterday:** What I worked on and completed
**Today:** What I'm working on today
**Blockers:** Anything blocking my progress (or "None")
```

**Live standup format:**
- Each person: 2 minutes max (same 3 questions)
- Blockers not solved in standup go to a follow-up conversation outside the standup
- No problem-solving in standup

---

### 2. Weekly Team Planning

**When:** Monday 10:00 AM  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Who:** All team members (Stage 3+) or CEO solo (Stage 1–2)  
**Cadence:** Weekly

**Agenda (rotating owner):**
1. Last week: wins and misses (10 min)
2. This week: priorities and projects (15 min)
3. Active pipeline: new leads or proposals (5 min)
4. Blockers: anything the team needs resolved (10 min)
5. Announcements: any updates from CEO/Director (5 min)

**Output:** Notes posted in Slack #general within 30 minutes of meeting end.

---

### 3. Project Kickoff

**When:** Day 1 of every new project  
**Duration:** 60 minutes (client) + 30 minutes (internal)  
**Who (internal):** CEO + Designer + Developer + PM (if applicable)  
**Who (client call):** CEO + Designer + Client

**Internal kickoff agenda:**
1. Project brief review (10 min)
2. Role assignments and ownership (10 min)
3. Timeline review and milestone setting (10 min)

**Client kickoff agenda:**
1. Introductions and team overview (5 min)
2. Project goals and success metrics (15 min)
3. Timeline and milestone review (10 min)
4. Communication preferences and tools (5 min)
5. Q&A (15 min)
6. Next steps and first deliverable (10 min)

**Output:** Meeting notes + project timeline document created in Notion before EOD.

---

### 4. Sprint Review / Project Status

**When:** Every 2 weeks (bi-weekly) on active projects  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Who:** Project team (internal only)  
**Cadence:** Bi-weekly per active project

**Agenda:**
1. What we completed this sprint (10 min)
2. What's next (10 min)
3. Blockers and risks (5 min)
4. Timeline check: on track? (5 min)

**Output:** Project status updated in Notion/Linear. Any client-facing updates drafted.

---

### 5. Retrospective

**When:** Within 5 business days of project completion  
**Duration:** 45 minutes  
**Who:** All project team members  
**Cadence:** After every project

**Agenda:**
1. What went well? (15 min)
2. What didn't go well? (15 min)
3. What do we change next time? (10 min)
4. Action items with owners (5 min)

**Format:** Miro board or Notion template with 3 columns (Well / Didn't / Change).

**Rules:**
- Focus on process, not people
- No blame; assume positive intent
- Every insight becomes an action item or documentation update
- Retros are private to the internal team (not shared with clients)

---

### 6. 1:1 (One-on-One)

**When:** Weekly (flexible scheduling)  
**Duration:** 30 minutes  
**Who:** Manager + direct report  
**Cadence:** Weekly (Stage 3+) / Bi-weekly (Stage 2)

**Format:** Employee-driven agenda. Manager listens and coaches.

**Shared 1:1 Notion doc (updated before each meeting by employee):**
- What I worked on this week
- What I'm proud of
- What's been difficult
- What I need from you (my manager)
- Upcoming priorities

**Manager prepares:**
- Feedback on recent work (specific, documented)
- Coaching questions
- Any business updates relevant to the role

**Not for:** Task assignment, project management updates (use async for those).

---

### 7. Leadership Meeting

**When:** Monday 2:00 PM (Stage 3+)  
**Duration:** 60 minutes  
**Who:** CEO + Department Directors  
**Cadence:** Weekly (Stage 3+)

**Agenda:**
1. KPI dashboard review: revenue, pipeline, MRR, project health (20 min)
2. Department updates (5 min each department) (20 min)
3. Decisions needed (10 min)
4. Strategic agenda item (rotate ownership) (10 min)

**Output:** Decision log updated. Action items with owners and due dates.

---

### 8. Quarterly Planning

**When:** First week of each quarter (January, April, July, October)  
**Duration:** 3–4 hours (half-day session)  
**Who:** CEO + Directors (Stage 3+) or CEO solo (Stage 1–2)  
**Cadence:** Quarterly

**Agenda:**
1. Prior quarter review: goals hit vs. missed (45 min)
2. Financial review: revenue, expenses, MRR (30 min)
3. New quarter OKRs (Objectives + Key Results) (60 min)
4. Hiring and capacity plan (30 min)
5. Key projects and initiatives (30 min)
6. Marketing and pipeline plan (30 min)

**Output:** Quarterly OKR document published to Notion. Budget adjustments filed. Project backlog updated.

---

### 9. Annual Planning

**When:** December (2 days offline)  
**Duration:** 2 full days  
**Who:** CEO + Directors  
**Cadence:** Annual

**Day 1: Retrospective**
- Year review: revenue, clients, team, wins, losses
- Culture health check (anonymous team survey reviewed)
- Competitive landscape review
- What worked, what didn't, what we stop doing

**Day 2: Forward Planning**
- New year revenue and MRR targets
- Hiring plan
- Product and service roadmap
- Marketing and growth strategy
- Budget and financial plan
- OKRs for Q1

**Output:** Annual plan document in Notion. Q1 OKRs finalized. Budget approved. Shared with team in January all-hands.

---

### 10. Client Update Calls

**When:** As needed per project; monthly for care plan clients (Growth Partner only)  
**Duration:** 30 minutes (project) / 30 minutes (care plan monthly call)  
**Who:** CEO or Account Manager + Client

**Project call agenda:**
1. Status update: what's complete, what's next (10 min)
2. Open questions requiring client input (10 min)
3. Next steps and deadlines (5 min)
4. Q&A (5 min)

**Output:** Written call summary sent to client within 24 hours of call.

## Meeting Hygiene Rules

1. **Late arrivals:** If you're >5 minutes late with no notice, the meeting starts without you.
2. **No multitasking.** Camera on for all client calls. Full presence for all internal meetings.
3. **Phone off.** No phone checking during meetings unless explicitly on-call.
4. **Notes published within 2 hours** of meeting end (not days later).
5. **Action items format:** `[Owner] [Task] by [Date]` — always three elements.
6. **Decision log:** Every decision made in a meeting is documented. No verbal-only decisions.
