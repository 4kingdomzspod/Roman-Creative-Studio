## Purpose

Define how Roman Creative Studio recognizes, rewards, and celebrates team members — from small daily wins to major career milestones. Recognition is not a perk; it is a management responsibility.

**Business Value:** Recognized employees are retained employees. Studies consistently show recognition is more effective than compensation alone in driving discretionary effort and loyalty. At RCS, recognition is built into the operating rhythm, not left to chance.

**Owner:** CEO / Department Directors  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** PerformanceReviews.md, PromotionFramework.md, CareerPaths.md, CompanyCulture.md

## Recognition Philosophy

1. **Recognize behavior, not just outcomes.** A team member who went above and beyond on a project that ultimately lost a client still deserves recognition for the effort.
2. **Specific > generic.** "Great job" is noise. "The accessibility audit you ran on the Martinez project caught 14 violations that would have caused real legal exposure for the client" is meaningful.
3. **Public and private.** Public recognition in #wins builds culture. Private recognition in 1:1 shows genuine attention.
4. **Timely.** Recognition delayed is recognition diluted. Recognize within 48 hours of the behavior.
5. **Peer recognition matters as much as manager recognition.** Build mechanisms for teammates to recognize each other.

## Recognition Tiers

### Tier 1: Daily Recognition (No budget required)

**#wins Slack channel**
- Any team member can post in #wins to celebrate themselves or others
- CEO responds to every #wins post with a specific reply (not just emoji)
- Examples worth posting:
  - Client said something positive about your work
  - Solved a difficult technical problem
  - Delivered under deadline
  - Helped a teammate get unstuck
  - Found and fixed a bug before launch
  - Documentation contribution that helped others

**Shoutouts in meetings**
- Every weekly team meeting begins with a shoutout moment (2 min)
- Manager specifically names a behavior and its impact
- Rotate recognition across the team — don't always recognize the same person

**DM recognition**
- Managers send a personal Slack DM when they notice something excellent
- No formula needed: just be specific and genuine

---

### Tier 2: Monthly Recognition

**Team Member of the Month (Stage 3+)**
- Nominated by peers and managers
- Voted on in last team meeting of each month (or async Slack poll)
- Winner announced in #general with specific reasons why
- Recognition: $50 Amazon gift card + featured in monthly newsletter (internal)
- No team member can win consecutive months

**Client Compliment Pass-Through**
- Any client compliment about a specific team member is:
  1. Forwarded directly to that person via Slack DM
  2. Shared in #wins with client name and context
  3. Noted in their performance file for annual review

---

### Tier 3: Quarterly Recognition

**Performance Bonus (Stage 3+)**

| Performance Level | Quarterly Bonus |
|------------------|----------------|
| Exceeded all KPIs | 5–10% of quarterly salary |
| Met all KPIs | 2–5% of quarterly salary |
| Met most KPIs (>80%) | 1–3% of quarterly salary |
| Below targets | No bonus |

**Criteria for bonus eligibility:**
- 90+ days of employment (no bonus in first quarter)
- No active PIP
- KPIs documented and agreed upon at start of quarter
- Bonus calculated on base salary, not total compensation

**Quarterly Team Celebration (Stage 3+)**
- Virtual team event or in-person (if team is co-located)
- Budget: $50/person per quarter
- Format: team dinner, experience, or activity (voted on by team)

---

### Tier 4: Annual Recognition

**Annual Performance Bonus**

| Annual Review Score | Annual Bonus |
|--------------------|-------------|
| 4.5–5.0 (Exceptional) | 10–15% of base salary |
| 4.0–4.4 (Strong) | 6–10% of base salary |
| 3.5–3.9 (Solid) | 3–6% of base salary |
| 3.0–3.4 (Meets expectations) | 0–3% |
| <3.0 | No bonus |

**"Founder's Award" (Annual, 1 recipient)**
- Selected by CEO
- Criteria: Most significant contribution to RCS culture, systems, or growth
- Recognition: $500 bonus + formal announcement + permanent mention in RCS company history doc
- First award eligible when team size ≥3

**Profit Sharing (Stage 4+, Year 3+)**
- When annual net profit exceeds targets, distribute 10–15% of excess profit to team
- Distribution formula: weighted by role level and tenure
- Requires CEO approval and minimum 6-month employment to participate

## Mentorship Program (Stage 3+)

### Internal Mentorship
- Every senior team member mentors 1 junior team member
- Formal 30-min bi-weekly 1:1 (separate from manager 1:1)
- Mentor is NOT the mentee's manager
- Duration: 6-month cycles, then reassigned
- Mentors recognized in quarterly review with explicit credit

### External Mentorship Support
- RCS supports employees connecting with external mentors
- CEO can facilitate introductions to agency owners, designers, developers in network
- SCORE, ADPList, or industry communities encouraged
- Mentorship time (up to 2 hours/month) is approved company time

## Recognition Anti-Patterns to Avoid

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails |
|-------------|-------------|
| Recognizing only revenue outcomes | Demotivates support and operations roles |
| Always recognizing the same person | Makes team feel invisible; breeds resentment |
| Generic recognition ("great job!") | Feels performative; not memorable |
| Late recognition (weeks after the fact) | Loses impact; feels like an afterthought |
| Recognition without specificity | Team doesn't know what behavior to repeat |
| Making recognition competitive | Creates zero-sum culture; undermines teamwork |
