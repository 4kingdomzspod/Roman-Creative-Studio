## Purpose

Define the complete offboarding process for employees and contractors leaving Roman Creative Studio — ensuring clean transitions, protected business assets, continued client service, and positive lasting relationships.

**Business Value:** Poor offboarding creates security vulnerabilities, knowledge loss, and damaged relationships. A structured offboarding process protects the business and leaves every departing team member as a potential future ambassador, referral source, or re-hire.

**Owner:** CEO / Operations Manager  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** SuccessionPlanning.md, KnowledgeBase.md, EmployeeHandbook.md, ContractorHandbook.md

---

## Offboarding Principles

1. **Professional until the last day.** The quality of the exit defines the long-term relationship. Departing team members should feel respected and valued regardless of the circumstances.
2. **Security first.** Access revocation is the #1 priority. No former employee or contractor should have active access to RCS systems after their last day.
3. **Knowledge transfer is mandatory.** No team member leaves without documenting their processes and transferring ownership of their work.
4. **Exit is an opportunity.** Exit interviews reveal what's not working. That feedback is valuable even when it's uncomfortable.
5. **Leave the door open.** Boomerang hires are real. Contractors rebook. Maintain the relationship.

---

## Offboarding Types

| Type | Notice Period | Process Intensity |
|------|-------------|------------------|
| Voluntary resignation (employee) | 2 weeks minimum | Full offboarding |
| Voluntary resignation (contractor) | 1 week (or per agreement) | Abbreviated offboarding |
| End of contract (contractor) | As per contract | Abbreviated offboarding |
| Involuntary termination (employee) | Immediate (w/ severance if applicable) | Full offboarding |
| Mutual separation | Agreed timeline | Full offboarding |

## Offboarding Checklist — Employee

### Day 1 of Notice (Immediate)

**Logistics:**
- [ ] CEO acknowledges resignation in writing
- [ ] Confirm last working day
- [ ] Determine what to communicate to team and when
- [ ] Determine what to communicate to clients and when
- [ ] Begin succession planning for role coverage

**Involuntary termination:**
- [ ] HR conversation with documentation (PIP outcome or policy violation)
- [ ] CEO present for all involuntary termination conversations
- [ ] Final paycheck prepared per applicable law
- [ ] Access revocation begins immediately

---

### Week 1–2: Knowledge Transfer

**Documentation review:**
- [ ] Departing employee lists all processes they own
- [ ] For each process: is it documented? If not, document it this week.
- [ ] New owner assigned to each process and documentation page
- [ ] New owner confirms they understand each transferred responsibility
- [ ] Active project status documented for each project they touch

**Client transition:**
- [ ] CEO reviews all active client relationships owned by departing team member
- [ ] Transition plan created: who owns each client going forward?
- [ ] Client notification drafted (if client relationship warrants direct communication)
- [ ] Warm handoff scheduled where possible (departing employee introduces new contact)

**Credentials audit:**
- [ ] Complete list of all tools the employee has access to
- [ ] Which credentials are shared (must be changed)?
- [ ] Which credentials are personal (will be revoked)?
- [ ] Who will be the new owner for each tool?

---

### Last Working Day

**Access revocation (complete before EOD):**
- [ ] Google Workspace account deactivated (not deleted — archive emails first)
- [ ] Slack workspace removed
- [ ] GitHub org access revoked
- [ ] Figma organization removed
- [ ] Notion access revoked
- [ ] HubSpot user deactivated
- [ ] Stripe user removed (if applicable)
- [ ] QuickBooks user removed (if applicable)
- [ ] MailerLite user removed (if applicable)
- [ ] Any shared passwords changed (LastPass/1Password)
- [ ] Physical equipment return confirmed (if applicable)
- [ ] Calendly integration removed
- [ ] Any admin access to client sites revoked

**Final deliverables:**
- [ ] All work-in-progress handed off and confirmed received
- [ ] Figma files organized and named correctly
- [ ] GitHub repos: all WIP branches merged or archived
- [ ] Client files transferred to team Google Drive
- [ ] Notion pages: ownership transferred, not orphaned
- [ ] Email auto-responder set to forward to CEO or new contact

**Final compensation:**
- [ ] Last paycheck confirmed (including any PTO payout per applicable law)
- [ ] Final expenses reimbursed
- [ ] Bonus pro-ration confirmed (if applicable per offer letter)

---

### Exit Interview

**Conducted by:** CEO or Operations Manager  
**Timing:** Last week or last day  
**Format:** Video call (30–45 minutes) or written survey  
**Tone:** Curious, not defensive. The goal is honest feedback.

**Exit interview questions:**
1. What prompted your decision to leave?
2. What did you enjoy most about working at Roman Creative Studio?
3. What was your biggest frustration?
4. Was there anything we could have done differently to retain you?
5. How would you describe the culture here?
6. What would you change about the way we work?
7. Would you recommend RCS to a friend as a place to work? Why or why not?
8. Is there anything you wish you had said during your time here?
9. Do you want to stay connected? (referrals, freelance, future roles)

**Output:** Exit interview summary documented in CEO-only file. Themes tracked annually. Actions taken where patterns emerge.

## Offboarding Checklist — Contractor

### End of Contract

- [ ] Final deliverables received and confirmed
- [ ] Final invoice submitted and paid within 14 days
- [ ] All project files transferred to RCS Google Drive or GitHub
- [ ] Access revoked from all RCS tools
- [ ] Portfolio permission status communicated (approved/restricted)
- [ ] NDA reminder sent (confidentiality survives contract end)
- [ ] 1099 information confirmed (if >$600 in year)
- [ ] Brief feedback survey sent (3 questions, optional)
- [ ] Contractor rated and filed in vetted pool (good/neutral/do not rehire)
- [ ] Warm close message sent: "Thank you for the partnership."

## Post-Offboarding

### 30 Days After Departure
- [ ] Confirm all access is fully removed (security audit)
- [ ] Confirm client transitions are stable
- [ ] Knowledge transfer completeness check: are there gaps?
- [ ] Update org chart and role documentation
- [ ] Begin or continue recruiting for replacement

### Maintaining the Relationship

**Alumni network (Stage 3+):**
- Former team members who left on good terms are invited to a lightweight alumni Slack channel
- CEO sends a personal update 1–2x/year to former team members
- Former team members are considered first for re-hires and referrals
- Positive referrals from alumni are tracked and recognized

**Reference policy:**
- RCS provides employment verification for all former employees
- Professional references provided for good-standing departures
- CEO provides personal reference for top performers
- For involuntary terminations: employment dates and title only (no performance commentary)
