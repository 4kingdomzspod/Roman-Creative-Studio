## Purpose

Define clear career growth trajectories for every discipline at Roman Creative Studio. Team members should always know what level they're at, what's required to advance, and what growth looks like at this company.

**Business Value:** Reduces turnover. Attracts ambitious talent. Creates a culture of growth. Enables merit-based promotion decisions.

**Owner:** CEO / Department Directors  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Related Documents:** RoleDescriptions.md, PromotionFramework.md, PerformanceReviews.md, Training.md

## Career Level Definitions

### IC (Individual Contributor) Track

```
Junior → Mid-Level → Senior → Lead
```

### Management Track

```
Lead → Manager → Director → VP / Executive
```

## Track 1: Design

### Junior UI/UX Designer
**Experience:** 0–1 year  
**Compensation range:** $40,000–$50,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Works from detailed briefs with close supervision
- Needs feedback on most design decisions
- Proficient in Figma basics; learning design system
- Pixel-accurate at execution; developing judgment
- Takes <3 revisions on basic tasks with guidance

**To advance to Mid-Level:**
- [ ] Complete all training modules (02, 03)
- [ ] 100% WCAG compliance on 3 consecutive projects
- [ ] >55% first-round client approval rate over 6 months
- [ ] Positive peer review from at least 1 senior team member
- [ ] 12+ months at Junior level

---

### Mid-Level Designer
**Experience:** 1–3 years  
**Compensation range:** $50,000–$65,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Works from project briefs with minimal supervision
- Makes most design decisions independently
- Expert in Figma and design system
- Handles client feedback sessions independently
- Contributes to design system improvements
- Mentors junior designers informally

**To advance to Senior:**
- [ ] Lead 3+ projects end-to-end with positive outcome
- [ ] >60% first-round client approval rate over 6 months
- [ ] Contribute 1 design system improvement (documented)
- [ ] Demonstrate accessibility expertise (no violations in 6 months)
- [ ] 18+ months at Mid-Level

---

### Senior Designer
**Experience:** 3–6 years  
**Compensation range:** $65,000–$80,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Owns projects end-to-end with full autonomy
- Sets design direction and makes strategic decisions
- Mentors junior and mid-level designers
- Contributes to new service development
- Presents work confidently to clients
- Recognized by clients as expert and advisor

**To advance to Design Lead / Creative Director:**
- [ ] Mentor 2+ junior/mid designers with documented growth
- [ ] Lead design system major version update
- [ ] Present work at team level (internal crit session or case study)
- [ ] Demonstrate business acumen (understand how design decisions affect revenue)
- [ ] 24+ months at Senior level

---

### Design Lead / Creative Director
**Experience:** 6+ years  
**Compensation range:** $80,000–$110,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Sets creative vision and quality standard for entire agency
- Hires, manages, and develops the design team
- Interfaces with CEO on brand direction
- Contributes to business development (portfolio, proposals)
- Drives design innovation and industry presence

## Track 2: Engineering (Development)

### Junior Frontend Developer
**Experience:** 0–1 year  
**Compensation range:** $45,000–$58,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Implements from detailed design specs with supervision
- Requires code review on all PRs
- Proficient in HTML/CSS; developing JS proficiency
- Needs help with complex layout and responsive design
- PageSpeed >85 with guidance

**To advance to Mid-Level:**
- [ ] Complete training modules (04, 05, 06, 12)
- [ ] Submit 10+ PRs that pass review without major changes
- [ ] Achieve PageSpeed >90 on 3 independent projects
- [ ] Zero accessibility violations on 3 projects
- [ ] 12+ months at Junior level

---

### Mid-Level Developer
**Experience:** 1–3 years  
**Compensation range:** $60,000–$78,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Implements projects with minimal supervision
- Reviews PRs from junior developers
- Expert in HTML/CSS/JS; competent in React/Next.js
- Handles integrations independently (Stripe, MailerLite, etc.)
- Consistently achieves PageSpeed >90

**To advance to Senior:**
- [ ] Lead implementation of 3+ complete projects
- [ ] Complete advanced TypeScript or backend training
- [ ] Achieve 0 post-launch bugs for 2 consecutive projects
- [ ] Mentor 1 junior developer
- [ ] 18+ months at Mid-Level

---

### Senior Developer
**Experience:** 3–6 years  
**Compensation range:** $80,000–$105,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Full project ownership (architecture, implementation, deployment)
- Handles complex integrations and custom systems
- Sets technical standards for the team
- Proficient across frontend and backend
- Makes technology decisions and recommendations to leadership

**To advance to Engineering Lead / Technical Director:**
- [ ] Architect 1+ novel technical solution adopted by team
- [ ] Conduct formal technical training session
- [ ] Hire or mentor mid/junior developer to next level
- [ ] Document all architectural decisions for 12+ months
- [ ] 24+ months at Senior level

---

### Engineering Lead / Technical Director
**Experience:** 6+ years  
**Compensation range:** $105,000–$140,000  
**Characteristics:**
- Sets technical vision and architecture standards
- Hires and manages engineering team
- Interfaces with CEO and Creative Director on product direction
- Drives technology stack evolution
- Accountable for all technical quality

## IC vs. Management Track Decision

Not everyone wants to manage. Not everyone should. Roman Creative Studio values and compensates both tracks equally at senior levels.

| Level | IC Track | Management Track | Comp Range |
|-------|----------|-----------------|------------|
| Junior | Junior Specialist | N/A | $35–58k |
| Mid | Specialist | N/A | $50–78k |
| Senior | Senior Specialist | Team Lead | $65–105k |
| Principal | Principal Specialist | Manager | $90–130k |
| Distinguished | Distinguished Specialist | Director | $110–150k |
| Fellow | Fellow | VP / Executive | $130k+ |

**IC vs. Management choice made at Senior level.** No one is required to move into management to earn top compensation.
