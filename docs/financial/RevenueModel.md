# Revenue Model

**Owner:** Alexander Roman / CFO / CEO  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Last Updated:** 2026-07-01  
**Status:** Model Defined — Revenue Activation Pending

---

## Purpose

Document every revenue stream available to Roman Creative Studio — current, planned, and potential. Define the purpose, ideal client, project size, delivery time, profitability, scalability, and automation potential for each stream. Build the architecture for a diversified, sustainable revenue engine.

---

## Business Value

A single revenue stream is a single point of failure. This model creates a roadmap from project-dependent income to a diversified portfolio of active, recurring, and passive revenue — each reinforcing the others through the same client relationship.

---

## Revenue Stream Classification

| Type | Definition | Examples |
|------|------------|----------|
| Active | Requires direct work per engagement | Custom websites, consulting |
| Recurring | Ongoing subscription revenue | Care Plans, SEO retainers |
| Passive | Revenue generated without active work | Templates, courses, affiliate |
| Partnership | Revenue from referral relationships | Hosting affiliate, partner referrals |

## TIER 1 — CORE ACTIVE REVENUE (Current)

---

### REV-01 — Custom Website Design & Development (BUILD)

**Purpose:** Design and develop a complete, conversion-optimized website for businesses that need a professional online presence.

**Ideal Client:** Local service businesses (dental, medical, construction, retail) with 1–20 employees, no existing website or outdated one, clear budget intent.

**Average Project Size:** $3,500  
**Delivery Time:** 3–5 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 65–75% (solo delivery)  
**Risk Level:** Low (well-defined scope, clear deliverables)

**Profitability:**
- Estimated delivery hours: 35–45 hours
- Effective hourly rate: $78–$100/hour at $3,500
- Contractor-delivered: margin compresses to 40–55%

**Scalability:** Medium — bounded by Alexander's time at current stage

**Automation Potential:** Medium — AI can accelerate copywriting, SEO meta, and brief creation (saves 5–8 hours per project)

**Recurring Revenue Potential:** High — every BUILD client is a Care Plan candidate ($197/mo)

**Dependencies:** Website platform, design tools, domain/hosting setup

**Future Improvements:**
- Pre-built component library reduces BUILD delivery time from 40 to 25 hours
- AI-generated first-draft copy reduces content time by 60%
- Standardized onboarding questionnaire reduces brief clarification cycles

---

### REV-02 — Advanced Website (GROW)

**Purpose:** Deliver a sophisticated, multi-page website with advanced functionality, conversion optimization, and industry-specific features for growing businesses.

**Ideal Client:** Established businesses with existing marketing strategy, dedicated marketing budget, and growth goals (dental groups, healthcare practices, mid-size local businesses).

**Average Project Size:** $6,500  
**Delivery Time:** 5–7 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 65–72%

**Profitability:**
- Estimated delivery hours: 60–75 hours
- Effective hourly rate: $87–$108/hour at $6,500

**Scalability:** Medium  
**Automation Potential:** Medium  
**Recurring Revenue Potential:** High — prime candidate for SEO Retainer ($497/mo)

**Dependencies:** BUILD capability fully systematized

**Future Improvements:** Sub-contractor for development frees Alexander to handle 2 GROW projects simultaneously

---

### REV-03 — Custom Enterprise Website (SCALE)

**Purpose:** Deliver a complex, custom-engineered web presence for businesses that require advanced integrations, multiple stakeholders, and premium positioning.

**Ideal Client:** Healthcare networks, multi-location businesses, nonprofits with significant digital footprint, startups requiring investor-grade web presence.

**Average Project Size:** $12,000–$25,000+  
**Delivery Time:** 8–16 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 55–65% (higher contractor involvement)

**Scalability:** Low at current stage (too complex for solo delivery at high volume)
**Automation Potential:** Low  
**Recurring Revenue Potential:** Very High — prime Growth Partner candidate ($997/mo)

**Dependencies:** GROW tier fully systematized; contractor relationships established

**Future Improvements:** SCALE projects eventually transition to team delivery model

---

### REV-04 — Website Redesign

**Purpose:** Transform an existing, underperforming website into a modern, conversion-optimized presence without starting from scratch.

**Ideal Client:** Businesses with existing websites that are 3–7 years old, slow, not mobile-friendly, or generating no leads.

**Average Project Size:** $2,500–$5,500  
**Delivery Time:** 3–5 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 60–70%

**Notes:** Redesigns often have hidden complexity (content migration, legacy code) — scope carefully.

**Recurring Revenue Potential:** High — redesign clients often move to Care Plans

---

### REV-05 — Landing Page Design

**Purpose:** Build a single, high-conversion page for a specific campaign, product, or service offer.

**Ideal Client:** Businesses running paid ads, launching a new service, or running a promotion.

**Average Project Size:** $1,200–$2,500  
**Delivery Time:** 1–2 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 70–80% (lower complexity, faster delivery)

**Notes:** Good entry-point project; often leads to full website engagement.

**Automation Potential:** High — landing page templates + AI copy = rapid delivery

---

### REV-06 — Brand Identity Package

**Purpose:** Create foundational brand assets: logo, color palette, typography, brand guidelines.

**Ideal Client:** New businesses or rebranding businesses without visual identity.

**Average Project Size:** $1,500–$3,500  
**Delivery Time:** 2–3 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 65–75%

**Notes:** Often bundled with website projects. Standalone brand work can lead to website engagement.

**Recurring Revenue Potential:** Low (brand is typically one-time)

---

### REV-07 — Accessibility Audit

**Purpose:** Audit an existing website against WCAG 2.1 AA standards and deliver a prioritized remediation report.

**Ideal Client:** Businesses concerned about ADA liability; nonprofits with grant requirements; healthcare and government-adjacent organizations.

**Average Project Size:** $750–$1,500  
**Delivery Time:** 3–5 business days  
**Gross Margin:** 75–85% (low labor, high value)

**Automation Potential:** Very High — axe-core + Lighthouse + Claude API can automate 70% of audit generation

**Recurring Revenue Potential:** Medium — monthly accessibility monitoring as a Care Plan add-on

---

### REV-08 — Performance Optimization

**Purpose:** Improve an existing website's Core Web Vitals scores, load time, and Lighthouse performance scores.

**Ideal Client:** Businesses with existing websites that rank poorly or load slowly.

**Average Project Size:** $800–$2,000  
**Delivery Time:** 1–2 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 70–80%

**Automation Potential:** Medium — diagnostic is automatable; fix implementation is manual

---

## TIER 2 — RECURRING REVENUE (Priority Build)

---

### REV-09 — Website Care Plan ($197/mo)

**Purpose:** Provide ongoing website maintenance, security monitoring, performance checks, and minor content updates.

**Ideal Client:** Every completed project client. Non-technical business owners who can't maintain their own website.

**Monthly Rate:** $197  
**Delivery Time:** 3–4 hours/month  
**Gross Margin:** 55–70% (improves dramatically with automation)

**Scalability:** High — 20 Care Plan clients = $3,940/month with manageable time investment

**Automation Potential:** Very High — monitoring, reporting, and minor checks are automatable

**Retention Strategy:** Monthly reports showing value delivered; proactive communication

**Dependencies:** Stripe subscriptions active; monthly report template ready

---

### REV-10 — SEO Retainer ($497/mo)

**Purpose:** Deliver ongoing SEO services: keyword tracking, content recommendations, on-page optimization, monthly reporting.

**Ideal Client:** Businesses that completed a GROW or SCALE project and want to grow organic traffic.

**Monthly Rate:** $497  
**Delivery Time:** 6–8 hours/month  
**Gross Margin:** 55–65%

**Scalability:** Medium — requires consistent monthly output; can be partially automated

**Automation Potential:** High — rank tracking, reporting, and brief generation are automatable

**Recurring Revenue Potential:** Is recurring revenue. Primary upsell from Care Plan.

---

### REV-11 — Growth Partner ($997/mo)

**Purpose:** Serve as the client's outsourced digital growth team — strategy, SEO, content, performance, analytics, and monthly advisory calls.

**Ideal Client:** Ambitious businesses with a digital growth mindset and budget; typically SCALE tier project clients.

**Monthly Rate:** $997  
**Delivery Time:** 10–15 hours/month  
**Gross Margin:** 55–65%

**Scalability:** Low at solo stage (can't manage many at this depth) — cap at 5 Growth Partner clients

**Automation Potential:** Medium — reporting automated; strategy and calls are not

---

### REV-12 — Hosting & Domain Management

**Purpose:** Manage client website hosting, domain registration, and DNS configuration.

**Pricing Model:** Pass-through + management fee ($30–75/month depending on complexity)

**Gross Margin:** 40–60% (low-effort if managed properly)

**Notes:** GitHub Pages hosting for static sites is free — RCS's hosting cost for static HTML clients is $0. Charge for management of the hosting relationship, not the hosting itself.

**Scalability:** Very High — low time per client; scales linearly

---

### REV-13 — AI Automation Services

**Purpose:** Implement AI-powered automations for clients: lead qualification, email automation, chatbots, content generation workflows.

**Average Project Size:** $2,000–$5,000 setup + $200–$500/month maintenance  
**Delivery Time:** 2–4 weeks setup  
**Gross Margin:** 65–75%

**Ideal Client:** GROW/SCALE clients who are ready for AI-enhanced operations.

**Scalability:** Medium now; High as AI tooling matures

**Automation Potential:** High (meta: AI automates AI delivery)

---

### REV-14 — CRM Integration & Setup

**Purpose:** Configure and connect a client's CRM (HubSpot, Salesforce, or similar) to their website and email systems.

**Average Project Size:** $1,500–$3,500  
**Delivery Time:** 1–2 weeks  
**Gross Margin:** 60–70%

**Recurring Revenue Potential:** CRM maintenance retainer ($197–$497/month)

## TIER 3 — CONSULTING & KNOWLEDGE REVENUE

---

### REV-15 — Strategy Consulting

**Purpose:** Provide executive-level digital strategy guidance to business owners without a full web or marketing team.

**Pricing Model:** $200–$350/hour or $1,500–$3,000 per engagement  
**Delivery Time:** Ongoing  
**Gross Margin:** 85–95% (pure knowledge transfer, no delivery overhead)

**Ideal Client:** Business owners who need thinking, not building.

**Notes:** Consulting at this level requires reputation and proven results. Not a Day 1 offering — position for Year 2+.

---

### REV-16 — Team Training / Workshops

**Purpose:** Train client marketing teams on web strategy, SEO, content, and working effectively with a web agency.

**Pricing Model:** $500–$2,000 per workshop session  
**Delivery Time:** 2–4 hours per session  
**Gross Margin:** 85–95%

**Scalability:** Low (time-bound) until recorded

## TIER 4 — DIGITAL PRODUCTS (Passive Revenue)

---

### REV-17 — Website Templates

**Purpose:** Sell premium, pre-built website templates to DIY business owners and developers.

**Pricing Model:** $97–$497 per template (one-time) or $29/mo subscription access  
**Delivery Time:** 0 (passive after creation)  
**Gross Margin:** 90–95% (zero marginal cost per sale)

**Scalability:** Very High — unlimited sales with no additional work

**Stage Readiness:** Stage 3–4 (needs portfolio and audience)

---

### REV-18 — Online Courses

**Purpose:** Teach business owners how to maximize their website investment: SEO, content, conversion optimization.

**Pricing Model:** $97–$497 per course  
**Gross Margin:** 85–95%

**Stage Readiness:** Stage 4–5 (needs 5,000+ email subscribers and proven authority)

---

### REV-19 — Lead Magnet / Resource Downloads

**Purpose:** Free downloadable resources that capture email addresses and warm leads for agency services.

**Revenue Model:** Indirect — email list growth → nurture → service sales

**Direct Revenue:** $0 (free to download)

**Indirect Value:** Every 100 downloads at 5% conversion = 5 leads; at 20% close rate = 1 client = $3,500–$6,500 revenue

---

### REV-20 — Community Membership

**Purpose:** Monthly membership community for business owners focused on digital marketing and web strategy.

**Pricing Model:** $29–$49/month  
**Gross Margin:** 80–90%

**Stage Readiness:** Stage 4–5 (needs established audience)

---

## TIER 5 — PARTNERSHIP & AFFILIATE REVENUE

---

### REV-21 — Hosting Affiliate Partnerships

**Purpose:** Earn commission recommending hosting, domain, and tool providers to clients.

**Revenue:** $50–$200 per referred customer  
**Gross Margin:** 100% (referral, no delivery)

**Examples:** WP Engine ($200/referral), Cloudflare (partner program), Google Workspace reseller

**Stage Readiness:** Stage 1 — can activate immediately (low friction)

---

### REV-22 — White-Label Services (Agency Partners)

**Purpose:** Deliver web design and development for other agencies under their brand.

**Pricing Model:** $2,500–$5,000 per project (wholesale pricing to partner agency)

**Gross Margin:** 55–65% (lower margin than direct but volume is predictable)

**Benefits:** Stable, predictable project pipeline; no sales effort per project

**Stage Readiness:** Stage 2–3

---

### REV-23 — Referral Partner Program

**Purpose:** Incentivize other professionals (accountants, lawyers, real estate agents, business coaches) to refer clients.

**Referral Fee:** 10% of first project value ($350–$650 per referred project)

**Stage Readiness:** Stage 1–2 (can structure now, activate with first partners)
