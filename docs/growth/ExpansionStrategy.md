# Expansion Strategy

## Principles

Growth at Roman Creative Studio follows one rule: **never expand faster than quality can be maintained**.

This means:
- Systematize before adding capacity
- Document before delegating
- Test before scaling
- Maintain standards at every stage of growth

---

## Phase 1: Solo Optimization (Now)

Maximize output and quality as a solo operator:

- Document every process (this AOS is that work)
- Build templated starting points for common project types
- Establish recurring revenue base
- Build referral engine
- Create content that generates inbound leads
- Set the quality standard that everything else will be measured against

**Capacity:** 3–4 active projects at a time + ongoing care clients

---

## Phase 2: First Collaborators

Bring in specialized help for specific capabilities:

- **Copywriter** (contract) — for clients who need website copy written from scratch
- **Photographer** (contract) — for clients who need professional photos
- **SEO Specialist** (contract) — for clients on full SEO retainers beyond the basics

These are contractors, not employees. Clear scope, clear deliverables, clear communication expectations.

**Capacity:** 6–8 active projects with collaborator support

---

## Phase 3: Core Team

When volume and recurring revenue justify it, bring in permanent help:

- **Design / Brand Specialist** — takes on design-heavy projects
- **Front-End Developer** — takes on build-heavy projects
- **Client Success** — manages ongoing client relationships and care plans

**Capacity:** 15–20 active projects + full recurring base

---

## Geographic Expansion

Roman Creative Studio operates nationally from day one (remote). Geographic expansion means:
- Targeting city-specific keywords ("web design agency in [city]")
- Building location-based landing pages
- Partnering with local business networks in target markets

This is a Phase 2 content strategy, not a physical expansion.

---

## Vertical Expansion

As expertise deepens in specific industries, expansion means going deeper, not just wider:

- **Dental:** From websites → full digital marketing → practice growth consulting
- **Church:** From websites → digital ministry strategy → community platform implementation
- **Local Business:** From websites → full local SEO → reputation management

Deep vertical expertise commands higher prices and stronger referral networks than being a generalist.
