# Recurring Revenue

## Why Recurring Revenue Matters

Project-based revenue is unpredictable. Every month starts at zero. Recurring revenue creates:
- **Predictability** — you know what's coming in before the month starts
- **Stability** — a slow new-business month doesn't create a cash crisis
- **Deeper client relationships** — ongoing clients are better clients
- **Compounding value** — each new recurring client adds to a growing base

**Target:** Recurring revenue covers basic operating costs within 12 months.

---

## Revenue Streams

### 1. Website Care Plans
**Current Priority: 🔴 High**

Every client who launches a site should be offered a care plan.

| Plan | Price | Includes |
|------|-------|----------|
| Basic Care | $150–$250/mo | Hosting, security monitoring, backups, software updates |
| Growth Care | $400–$700/mo | Basic + monthly analytics report, up to 2 hours content updates |
| Partner Care | $800–$1,500/mo | Growth + SEO reporting, strategy call, priority support |

**Pitch at launch:** "Your site is an asset. A Care Plan keeps it secure, fast, and performing. It's like insurance for your digital presence."

---

### 2. SEO Retainers
**Current Priority: 🟠 Medium**

For clients who want to grow their organic search presence over time.

| Retainer | Price | Includes |
|----------|-------|----------|
| Local SEO Starter | $500–$800/mo | GBP optimization, local citations, monthly reporting |
| Content + SEO | $800–$1,500/mo | Starter + 2 blog posts/mo, keyword tracking |
| Full SEO Partner | $1,500–$3,000/mo | Content + competitive analysis, link building |

---

### 3. Hosting
**Current Priority: 🟠 Medium**

If managing hosting for clients (vs. GitHub Pages for own site), white-label or reseller hosting can add $20–$50/mo per client with minimal effort.

Note: For static sites, GitHub Pages is free — this applies to clients on WordPress or other CMS platforms.

---

### 4. AI Automation Retainers
**Current Priority: 🟡 Medium (build capability first)**

As AI tools mature, offering automation implementation and maintenance as a retainer:
- Chatbot setup and maintenance
- Email automation via CRM
- Lead routing and notification systems
- AI content workflows
