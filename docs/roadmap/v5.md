# Roadmap: Version 5.0

**Theme:** Authority — Establish Roman Creative Studio as the recognized expert in target industries, launch digital products, and build toward a team.

**Timeline:** Q2 – Q4 2027

---

## 🔴 Critical

### Brand Authority
- [ ] **Recognized in at least 2 target industries** — Active in dental and/or church communities online
- [ ] **20+ client projects completed** — Portfolio demonstrates clear expertise
- [ ] **5+ published case studies** with specific, measurable outcomes
- [ ] **Speaking or feature in industry publication** (dental association, church network, local business group)

### Revenue
- [ ] **$5,000+ MRR** from recurring clients (care plans + retainers)
- [ ] **First digital product launched** (template or course)
- [ ] **10+ active care plan clients**

---

## 🟠 High

### Team
- [ ] First contractor or part-time collaborator integrated into workflow
- [ ] Documented SOPs for: design handoff, development, QA, client communication
- [ ] All client-facing work meets the same quality bar regardless of who executes

### Content & SEO
- [ ] 20+ blog posts published
- [ ] Email list of 500+ subscribers
- [ ] 3+ organic leads per week from SEO
- [ ] 2–3 industry-specific resource downloads available

### Digital Products
- [ ] **Premium website template** for dental practices — sold on Gumroad or direct
- [ ] **Local SEO course** outline complete, at least Module 1 recorded

---

## 🟡 Medium

- [ ] 10+ total industry service pages
- [ ] Roman Creative Studio mentioned/referenced in online communities for target industries
- [ ] Podcast interview series with industry thought leaders (6+ episodes)
- [ ] Agency Playbook product outline (documentation for other solo agency owners)

---

## Architecture: Phase 3 Site

By v5.0, the site should be migrated to a static site generator to support:
- 20+ pages without HTML duplication
- Blog with proper pagination and categories
- Case study template
- Resource library
- Dynamic testimonials

**Recommended:** Eleventy or Astro (both are lightweight, HTML-first, no framework lock-in)

---

## The 2030 Vision Check

At v5.0, pause and check against the 10-year vision from [FutureVision.md](../brand/FutureVision.md):

- Are we on track for a team of 3–5 by 2030?
- Is Roman Creative Studio a recognized name in at least 2–3 industries?
- Is recurring revenue 40%+ of total revenue?
- Do clients refer us without being asked?

If yes — stay the course. If not — identify the gap and build the next roadmap around closing it.
