## Service Page Template Structure

Every industry page should follow this structure:

1. **Hero** — Industry-specific headline + 1 primary CTA
2. **Problem section** — "What bad [industry] websites cost you"
3. **Solution section** — What we build for [industry]
4. **Feature highlights** — 3–6 specific features relevant to this industry
5. **Process preview** — Brief overview of our process
6. **Testimonials** — Ideally from the specific industry
7. **Pricing preview** — Starting prices to qualify leads
8. **FAQ** — Industry-specific objections and questions
9. **CTA section** — Final conversion push
10. **Footer**

---

## SEO Requirements for Each Industry Page

- Title tag: `[Industry] Website Design | Roman Creative Studio`
- Meta description: industry-specific, 150–160 characters
- H1: Contains primary keyword naturally
- H2s: Supporting keywords ("dental website features", "church website design process", etc.)
- Internal links: Link to homepage, pricing, contact, and process pages
- External: No mandatory external links; add if citing industry stats
