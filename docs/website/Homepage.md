## Purpose

The homepage is the primary landing page for all traffic channels — direct, organic search, social, and referral. It serves as the brand's first impression and the navigation hub to all other pages.

---

## Business Goal

Convert visitors into qualified leads who either:
1. Book a discovery call via `/book`
2. Reach out via `/contact`
3. Explore the portfolio to build confidence, then convert

---

## Target Audience

Business owners who are research-ready: they've identified they need a website or digital presence upgrade and are evaluating agencies.

---

## SEO Goal

- **Primary keyword:** "digital agency for small businesses" / "web design agency"
- **Secondary:** "custom website design" / "small business website design"
- **Local:** Not yet targeting a specific city — opportunity to add location targeting
