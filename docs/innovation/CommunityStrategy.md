## Overview

The RCS Community is a paid membership for web designers, agency owners, and creative entrepreneurs who want to grow their craft and business alongside peers. It is the ecosystem that connects all RCS products — courses, templates, tools, and services — creating network effects and long-term retention.

**Why Community:** Communities compound. A course is consumed once. A community is returned to weekly. Members who stay 12+ months become advocates, referral sources, and our most loyal customers.

**Strategic Position:** Not a generic "design community." Specifically built for creative professionals running or building service businesses. Every feature, every discussion, every resource is filtered through that lens.

## Platform Selection

### Evaluation Criteria

| Platform | Monthly Cost | Strengths | Weaknesses |
|----------|-------------|-----------|------------|
| Circle.so | $89–$399 | Best community UX, built for creators | Cost scales with members |
| Skool | $99 flat | Simple, gamification, courses built-in | Less customizable |
| Discord | Free | Familiar to many, free | Poor monetization, noisy UX |
| Slack | Free–$15/user | Familiar to professionals | Not community-native, expensive at scale |
| Mighty Networks | $41–$99 | Native courses + community | UI not as clean |

**Year 1 Recommendation:** Start with a private Slack workspace (free) for the first 20–50 founding members.  
**Year 2 Recommendation:** Migrate to Circle.so Professional ($399/mo) when MRR justifies it.

**Migration Trigger:** When community MRR exceeds $2,000/mo — migrate to Circle.so.

## Member Acquisition Strategy

### Channel 1: Email List (Primary)
- All RCS email subscribers offered founding member discount
- Lead magnets funnel to membership offer (free resource → "Want more like this?")
- Course students automatically offered community upgrade

### Channel 2: Content Marketing
- LinkedIn content strategy builds audience, community offer in bio
- YouTube channel (Year 2) — community as "go deeper" offer
- Blog posts with email capture → nurture → membership pitch

### Channel 3: Partnerships
- Co-host workshops with complementary creators (copywriters, strategists)
- Guest on design/business podcasts with community mention
- Affiliate program for existing members (get $10/mo per referral)

### Channel 4: Product Flywheel
- Template buyers → upsell to community
- Course completers → community for continued support
- AI tool users → community for expert help interpreting results

## Retention Strategy

### First 30 Days (Critical Period)
- Welcome email sequence (5 emails, 30 days)
- Personal DM from Alexander within 24 hours of join
- Assigned to accountability group within 7 days
- First win celebration in public channel

### Months 1–6 (Habit Formation)
- Weekly engagement prompts require < 5 minutes to participate
- Monthly progress check-in thread
- Recognition for contribution (top contributor shoutout)

### Month 6+ (Community Veterans)
- Veterans invited to lead discussions or host workshops
- Pioneer badge and recognition
- Early access to new products
- Reduced pricing lock-in for annual members

### At-Risk Member Recovery
- 30-day inactivity: Automated re-engagement email
- 45-day inactivity: Personal outreach from community manager
- Pre-cancellation: Pause option (1 month free) offered before cancel
