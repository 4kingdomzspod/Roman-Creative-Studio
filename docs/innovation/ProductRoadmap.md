## Year 1 Products (2026): Quick Wins

**Theme:** Productize what already exists. Convert internal assets into sellable digital products with minimal new development time.

### P-01: RCS Proposal Template Pack
**Type:** Document templates (Google Docs + Notion)  
**Price:** $47  
**Audience:** Freelancers, junior agency owners  
**Effort:** 8–12 hours  
**Content:** Discovery questionnaire, project proposal, SOW, client contract, invoice template, project brief  
**Platform:** Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy  
**Revenue estimate:** 20 sales/month = $940/month

### P-02: Agency Operations Starter Kit
**Type:** Notion template pack  
**Price:** $97  
**Audience:** Solo freelancers starting an agency  
**Effort:** 12–16 hours  
**Content:** Client tracker, project tracker, MRR tracker, onboarding checklist, financial dashboard template  
**Platform:** Gumroad or Lemon Squeezy  
**Revenue estimate:** 10 sales/month = $970/month

### P-03: Web Design Prompt Pack v1
**Type:** Digital download (PDF + Notion)  
**Price:** $29  
**Audience:** Web designers, agency owners using AI tools  
**Effort:** 6–8 hours  
**Content:** 50 curated prompts across 8 categories (design, copy, SEO, accessibility, client communication)  
**Platform:** Gumroad  
**Revenue estimate:** 30 sales/month = $870/month

### P-04: Local Business Website Figma Template
**Type:** Figma template  
**Price:** $79  
**Audience:** Web designers serving local businesses  
**Effort:** 20–30 hours (leverages existing project work)  
**Content:** Homepage, About, Services, Contact, Testimonials, FAQ — fully componentized  
**Platform:** Gumroad + Creative Market  
**Revenue estimate:** 15 sales/month = $1,185/month

**Year 1 Product Revenue Target:** $2,000–$4,000/month by Month 12

## Year 2 Products (2027): Momentum

**Theme:** Expand the product line with higher-value offerings and begin audience distribution.

### P-05: Dental Practice Website Kit
**Type:** Figma + HTML/CSS template  
**Price:** $149  
**Audience:** Web designers serving dental practices  
**Effort:** 30–40 hours  
**Content:** Homepage, Services, About, Team, Booking, Patient Resources, FAQ

### P-06: Church Website Kit
**Type:** Figma + HTML/CSS template  
**Price:** $99  
**Audience:** Web designers serving churches and nonprofits  
**Effort:** 25–35 hours

### P-07: RCS Design Token System
**Type:** Figma + CSS variables package  
**Price:** $49  
**Audience:** UI designers and frontend developers  
**Content:** Complete token system (color, typography, spacing, shadows, radius)

### P-08: Accessibility Audit Toolkit
**Type:** Notion + Google Sheets template  
**Price:** $79  
**Audience:** Web designers, developers, agencies  
**Content:** WCAG 2.1 AA checklist, audit template, remediation tracker, client report template

### P-09: SEO Quick-Win Toolkit
**Type:** Notion + PDF  
**Price:** $59  
**Audience:** Small business owners, junior SEO practitioners  
**Content:** On-page SEO checklist, keyword research template, GSC setup guide, local SEO guide

### P-10: RCS Component Library (Figma)
**Type:** Figma component library (shared)  
**Price:** $129/one-time or $19/month  
**Audience:** Web designers and agencies  
**Content:** 100+ components (buttons, forms, cards, navigation, hero sections)

**Year 2 Product Revenue Target:** $5,000–$10,000/month

## Year 3 Products (2028): Platform

**Theme:** Education, community, and AI tools. Move from individual products to a platform model.

### P-11: Web Design Fundamentals Course
**Type:** Video course (self-paced)  
**Price:** $197–$297  
**Platform:** Teachable or Podia  
**Audience:** Career-changers entering web design  
**Content:** 8 modules, 40+ lessons, Figma exercises, portfolio project

### P-12: Agency Foundations Course
**Type:** Video course (self-paced)  
**Price:** $397–$597  
**Audience:** Freelancers building agencies  
**Content:** Pricing, proposals, client management, care plans, scaling

### P-13: RCS Community Membership
**Type:** Private community (Circle or Discord)  
**Price:** $47/month or $397/year  
**Audience:** Designers, developers, and agency owners following RCS  
**Content:** Monthly office hours, resource library, peer feedback, templates, AI prompts

### P-14: Website Audit AI Tool (Beta)
**Type:** Web app  
**Price:** $29/month  
**Audience:** Agency owners, freelancers  
**Content:** Automated website health check (performance, SEO, accessibility, UX)

**Year 3 Product Revenue Target:** $15,000–$25,000/month

## Year 4–5 Products (2029–2030): Scale

**Theme:** SaaS, enterprise licensing, marketplace.

### P-15: Agency OS SaaS Platform
**Type:** Web application  
**Price:** $79–$197/month  
**Audience:** Agency owners (1–10 person teams)  
**Content:** Project management + CRM + care plan delivery + client reporting in one tool

### P-16: Design Token Marketplace
**Type:** Marketplace platform  
**Price:** $0 (free tier) + $29/month premium + creator revenue share  
**Audience:** Designers who want ready-made token systems for different industries

### P-17: White-Label Agency OS
**Type:** Licensable SaaS  
**Price:** $500–$2,000/month (enterprise license)  
**Audience:** Larger agencies wanting to white-label RCS tools for their clients
