## Overview

The Innovation Pipeline is the master view of all active innovation initiatives — from raw ideas to launched products. It is the connective tissue between Research Lab (where ideas are generated), the Experiment Framework (where ideas are tested), and the Product Roadmap (where validated ideas are built).

**Review Cadence:** Monthly pipeline review (first Monday of month, 60 minutes)

---

## Pipeline Stages

```
STAGE 0: Raw Idea
    ↓
STAGE 1: Scored (Innovation Decision Framework)
    ↓
STAGE 2: Experiment Designed
    ↓
STAGE 3: Experiment In Progress
    ↓
STAGE 4: Experiment Complete (Pass / Fail / Pivot)
    ↓
STAGE 5: Build Approved
    ↓
STAGE 6: In Development (MVP)
    ↓
STAGE 7: Launched
    ↓
STAGE 8: Scaling / Optimizing
```

**Exit Conditions:**
- Any stage can exit to KILLED if evidence does not support continuing
- Killed items are archived, not deleted — some resurrect later with new context

## Pipeline Governance

### Monthly Pipeline Review Agenda (60 min)
1. New ideas: Score any Stage 0 items (15 min)
2. Active experiments: Status update and go/no-go check (15 min)
3. Upcoming experiments: Confirm timeline and readiness (10 min)
4. Active builds: Milestone review (10 min)
5. Launched products: Performance review (10 min)

### Decision Authority
| Decision | Authority |
|----------|----------|
| Add idea to pipeline | Anyone — log in Notion |
| Score idea (Stage 1) | CIO |
| Design experiment (Stage 2) | CIO |
| Approve experiment (Stage 2 → 3) | CEO + CIO |
| Kill experiment | CIO (with CEO notification) |
| Approve build (Stage 5) | CEO + CIO + CFO |
| Launch (Stage 7) | CEO |
