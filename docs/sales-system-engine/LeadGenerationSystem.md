# Lead Generation System
## Roman Creative Studio — Phase 5, Document 2

---

### Purpose

A lead generation system creates a predictable flow of qualified prospects into the RCS sales pipeline. The goal is not maximum volume — it is maximum quality. One genuinely qualified lead per week is more valuable than 20 unqualified inquiries.

SEO begins compounding. Instagram content builds authority. Cold email systematic.

**Months 6–12 (Scale):**
SEO becomes primary driver. Referral engine activated. Content authority established.

## Section 2 — SEO Lead Generation

### 2.1 How SEO Generates Leads

The 7 industry pages defined in Phase 4 are the primary long-term lead generation engine:

```
/industries/dental/           → Dental practice owners searching "dental website design"
/industries/church/           → Church administrators searching "church website design"
/industries/healthcare/       → Healthcare providers searching "healthcare website design"
/industries/local-business/   → Local owners searching "local business website design"
/industries/real-estate/      → Agents searching "real estate website design"
/industries/restaurant/       → Restaurant owners searching "restaurant website design"
/industries/startup/          → Founders searching "startup website design"
```

Each page is a qualified lead machine. A visitor arriving on `/industries/dental/` has already self-qualified: they are a dental business looking for website design.

### 2.2 SEO Lead Qualification

Visitors from industry pages are pre-qualified by:
- The specificity of the search query (industry + service)
- The content of the page (not for everyone — for this industry)
- The CTA ("Book a Free Discovery Call" — signals they're ready to talk)

**Lead quality signal from SEO:** When a form submission references their industry specifically, or arrives from an industry page URL (tracked via GA4 + UTM), it is a Tier 1 lead.

## Section 3 — Referral Lead Generation

### 3.1 Referral Characteristics

Referrals are the highest-quality leads available:
- Pre-sold on RCS (a trusted person recommended them)
- Pre-qualified (the referrer understands RCS pricing and standards)
- Higher close rate (70–85% vs. 20–30% for cold leads)
- Faster sales cycle (trust already established)

### 3.2 Referral Activation (covered fully in ReferralSystem.md)

Key trigger: Ask at the peak of client satisfaction — immediately after a successful launch.

## Section 4 — LinkedIn Outreach System

### 4.1 Target Profile

**Search filters on LinkedIn:**
- Title: Owner / Founder / Practice Manager / Pastor / Executive Director
- Industry: Medical / Dental / Religious Institutions / Restaurants / Real Estate
- Company size: 1–50 employees
- Location: Target market (local, regional, or national by service)

### 4.2 Connection Request Message

```
Hi [First Name],

I work with [industry] businesses on their web presence and
noted you're running [Company Name].

I build websites specifically for [industry] — focused on
converting visitors into [patients/members/clients].

Would it be worth a 20-minute conversation?

Either way, happy to connect.

— Alexander, Roman Creative Studio
```

**Rules:**
- Never pitch on first message
- Connection request only — message comes after they accept
- Personalize with their business name and industry
- Keep under 60 words

### 4.3 Follow-Up After Connection

**Day 1 after connection accepted:**
```
Thanks for connecting, [First Name].

I looked at [Company Name]'s website — [one specific, honest observation
about what's working or what could be improved].

Do you have 20 minutes this week to talk about your digital presence?
I can share a few specific things I'd do differently for your industry.

— Alexander
```

**If no response after 5 days:**
```
Hey [First Name] — no pressure at all.

I'll drop a quick thought: [Industry] businesses that invest in
conversion-focused website design typically see a measurable increase
in [inquiries/bookings/calls] within 60–90 days of launch.

If timing isn't right, I'm here when it is.

— Alexander
```

**If no response after second message:** Do not follow up again. Move on.

### 4.4 LinkedIn Content Strategy

Post 3–4 times per week on LinkedIn to stay visible to connections:

| Content Type | Frequency | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Case study result (before/after) | 1x/week | Proof |
| Industry insight (dental, church, etc.) | 1x/week | Authority |
| Website/conversion tip | 1x/week | Value |
| Behind-the-scenes (process, design thinking) | 1x/week | Trust |

**Never post:** Agency self-promotion, vague inspiration quotes, "excited to announce" fluff.

## Section 6 — Cold Email Outreach

### 6.1 Target List Building

Target businesses that:
- Have a website that is clearly outdated (slow, not mobile-friendly, no conversion structure)
- Are in a target vertical (dental, church, healthcare, local service)
- Are actively growing (hiring, expanding, advertising — visible signals)

**Research sources:** Google Maps search, Yelp, industry directories, LinkedIn company pages.

### 6.2 Cold Email Template

**Subject line options (A/B test):**
- "[Business Name]'s website — one quick thought"
- "Noticed something about [Business Name]"
- "Website question for [First Name]"

**Email body:**
```
Hi [First Name],

I came across [Business Name] while researching [industry] businesses in [area].

I noticed [specific, honest observation about their current website —
one thing that's costing them leads, e.g., no online booking, no mobile optimization,
no visible trust signals].

I specialize in building websites for [industry] businesses
that are designed to convert visitors into [patients/clients/members].

I've done this for [relevant example or reference to industry experience].

Would you be open to a 20-minute call to see if it makes sense
for [Business Name]?

No pitch — just a conversation.

Alexander
Roman Creative Studio
romancreativestudio.co
```

**Rules:**
- Personalize with a real observation (not a template observation)
- Never attach files to cold emails
- Never use: "I hope this email finds you well"
- Follow up maximum 2 times after initial email
- Follow-up #1 (3 days later): one-sentence bump — "Wanted to make sure this didn't get buried."
- Follow-up #2 (7 days later): value add — share a relevant article or insight, soft CTA

### 6.3 Cold Email Qualification Signal

A cold email lead who replies is not yet qualified. Qualification happens on the discovery call. But reply signals to look for:

**Positive signals:** Asks about timeline, pricing, or process. Shares their current pain point. Forwards to the decision-maker.

**Negative signals:** Asks only "how much does it cost?" as first response. Seems to be collecting quotes. Says "we'll be looking at this in 6 months."

## Section 7 — Website Audit Lead Magnet

### 7.1 How It Works

Offer a free "Website Conversion Audit" as a low-barrier entry point for leads who aren't ready to book a full discovery call.

**Offer:** "Get a free audit of your website — we'll identify your top 3 conversion leaks in 24 hours."

**Delivery:** A 1-page PDF or Loom video (5–7 minutes) covering:
1. Conversion architecture issues (CTAs, trust signals)
2. SEO structure gaps (title tags, H1, metadata)
3. Mobile experience issues
4. Top 1 recommendation with clear ROI framing

**Outcome:** The audit demonstrates expertise and creates a natural reason to schedule a discovery call.

### 7.2 Audit Lead Follow-Up

After delivering the audit:
```
Subject: Your website audit — [Business Name]

Hi [First Name],

Attached is your website audit.

The most impactful issue I found: [specific issue].

Fixes for this typically result in [measurable outcome — e.g.,
"a 20–40% increase in contact form submissions for [industry] businesses"].

I'd love to walk you through this live — would a 20-minute call
this week or next make sense?

Alexander
```

## Section 8 — Lead Qualification Standards

### 8.1 Minimum Qualification Criteria (ALL must be met)

Before a lead receives a discovery call invite:

1. **Real business** — has a verifiable business, website, or Google listing
2. **Decision-making authority** — is the owner, partner, or has clear authority to sign
3. **Genuine problem** — has articulated a specific problem their website is causing
4. **Budget awareness** — either has indicated a real budget or hasn't objected to premium pricing in early messages
5. **Reasonable timeline** — looking to act within 1–3 months (not "eventually")

### 8.2 Lead Scoring

| Factor | High Score | Low Score |
|---|---|---|
| Source | Referral / SEO industry page | Cold email / social media follower |
| Problem specificity | Describes specific pain with business impact | Vague ("need a better website") |
| Timeline | Within 30 days | 3+ months out |
| Budget signal | Mentioned investment willingness | Asked only about cost |
| Business health | Growing, active, revenue-generating | Pre-revenue, unclear model |
| DM access | DM is on the call | "I'll pass this along" |

**Score 4–6 high:** Tier 1 lead. Prioritize discovery call within 48 hours.
**Score 2–3 high:** Tier 2 lead. Schedule call but qualify harder during call.
**Score 0–1 high:** Tier 3. Nurture via content. Do not invest significant time yet.

### 8.3 What Gets Ignored

The following inquiry types do not receive a personal discovery call response:

- "How much for a basic website?"
- "Can you make something like [competitor's site]?"
- "I need a website for my idea" (pre-revenue, no business model)
- Requests without any business context
- "I need it done in 1 week" (not aligned with RCS quality timeline)

**Response for unqualified inquiries:**
```
Thanks for reaching out.

RCS specializes in conversion-focused website strategy
for established service businesses. Our projects typically
start at $[minimum anchor] and involve a structured discovery
and design process.

If that aligns with where you are, I'd love to connect.
You can book a free discovery call here: [link]

Alexander
```

This response filters without burning the bridge. If they book the call after seeing the anchor price — they've self-qualified.
