## Section 1 — Sales Philosophy

### 1.1 Core Belief

Sales at Roman Creative Studio is not persuasion. It is diagnosis.

The job of every sales interaction is to determine:
1. Does this prospect have a real, solvable business problem?
2. Is a premium digital presence the right lever to solve it?
3. Does this prospect have the budget, authority, and mindset to invest properly?
4. Is RCS the right partner for this specific problem?

If the answer to all four is yes — the sale is a natural conclusion, not a hard close. If any answer is no — the prospect is not an RCS client, and that is a good outcome, not a failure.

### 1.2 Positioning Statement

Roman Creative Studio is a **premium digital strategy and design agency** that builds conversion-optimized web presences for service businesses, healthcare practices, faith organizations, and growth-stage companies.

RCS is not:
- A budget web design shop
- A freelance operation that takes any project
- A template-based production house
- A generalist agency that does everything for everyone

RCS is:
- A strategic partner, not a vendor
- A specialist in conversion, SEO, and brand-quality execution
- A system builder, not a project fulfiller
- An agency that gets involved in business outcomes, not just deliverables

## Section 2 — The Ideal Client Profile

### 2.1 Who RCS Accepts

**Business type:**
- Service-based businesses (dental, healthcare, legal, consulting, fitness)
- Faith organizations (churches, ministries, nonprofits with clear mission)
- Local businesses with a physical or service-area presence
- Startups with validated business models seeking professional digital presence
- Established businesses undergoing growth, rebrand, or market expansion

**Business stage:**
- Generating revenue (not pre-revenue except funded startups)
- Has an existing client base or patient/member base to reference
- Has tried a DIY or low-budget website and outgrown it
- Is ready to invest in professional-grade digital infrastructure

**Decision-making:**
- The person on the discovery call is the decision-maker, or has direct access to sign-off
- Can move to decision within 2–4 weeks (not 6 months)

**Mindset indicators (ideal client says things like):**
- "Our website doesn't represent where we are as a business"
- "We're getting traffic but no one's calling"
- "We want to be taken seriously in our market"
- "We know we need to fix this — we just haven't found the right partner"
- "We want to grow, and we know our digital presence is holding us back"

**Budget alignment:**
- Understands that premium work has premium pricing
- Views the investment as a business decision, not an expense
- Has asked about pricing in terms of ROI, not just cost

### 2.2 Who RCS Rejects (Non-Negotiable)

RCS does not accept clients who exhibit the following:

| Disqualifier | Signal | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Price is the primary decision factor | "Who's cheapest?" / "Can you match X's price?" | Politely decline |
| No clear business model or revenue | Can't explain how their business makes money | Do not proceed |
| Decision-maker not on the call | "I need to show this to my partner/boss" (repeatedly) | Reschedule with DM or decline |
| Scope creep mindset | "Can you also do..." before scope is even agreed | Qualify hard or decline |
| "Just need something simple" | Doesn't understand or value the RCS approach | Redirect or decline |
| No urgency whatsoever | "No rush, whenever" for a business problem | Not an RCS client yet |
| Multiple previous agencies with bad experiences framed as agency's fault | Pattern of blame | High risk — qualify carefully |
| Wants to own the design direction | "I know exactly what I want, just execute" | Not a strategy engagement |

## Section 3 — Market Positioning

### 3.1 The Positioning Ladder

```
DIY / Website Builders    Wix, Squarespace, GoDaddy        $0–$500/year
Freelancers               Fiverr, Upwork, local designers  $500–$3,000
Budget Agencies           Template shops, offshore builds  $1,500–$5,000

── PREMIUM THRESHOLD ──────────────────────────────────────────────────

Roman Creative Studio     Strategy-led, conversion-focused  $5,000–$25,000+
Top-tier agencies         Full-service, large teams         $25,000–$100,000+
```

RCS operates above the premium threshold. Every element of the sales process reinforces this positioning:
- The quality of the website that sells RCS
- The professionalism of the discovery call
- The quality of the proposal document
- The confidence of the pricing conversation
- The structure of the onboarding process

### 3.2 The Competitive Differentiation Statement

When a prospect asks "What makes you different from other agencies?":

> "Most agencies build websites. We build conversion systems. Every decision we make — from how the page is structured, to how CTAs are worded, to how fast it loads — is made against one question: will this bring you more qualified leads? We specialize in industries like dental, healthcare, churches, and service businesses because we've built deep expertise in how those customers think and what makes them trust a business enough to contact them. We don't take every project. We take the ones where we know we can deliver a measurable result."

## Section 4 — Revenue Model

### 4.1 Revenue Streams

| Stream | Type | Frequency | Strategic Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| Website design projects | Project-based | One-time | Primary acquisition revenue |
| Brand identity projects | Project-based | One-time | High-value, high-authority work |
| Website redesign projects | Project-based | One-time | Large ticket, warm leads |
| SEO optimization | Retainer | Monthly | Recurring, compounding value |
| Website Care Plans | Retainer | Monthly | Predictable base revenue |
| Conversion optimization | Project + retainer | Mixed | Ongoing relationship revenue |

### 4.2 Revenue Goal Framing

RCS measures revenue success by:
- **Average project value** (not number of projects)
- **Monthly recurring revenue** (retainer base)
- **Client lifetime value** (project + ongoing care + referrals)
- **Lead-to-close rate** (quality of qualification)

A business doing 2 projects at $15,000 each is stronger than 10 projects at $3,000 each. Fewer clients, higher value, better results, more referrals.
