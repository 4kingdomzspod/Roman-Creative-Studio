## Purpose

Standardize the RCS discovery call process to maximize conversion, qualify leads efficiently, and capture the strategic intelligence needed to write a winning proposal. A great discovery call is not a sales pitch — it is a premium consultation experience.

## Business Value

A structured discovery system increases close rates, reduces time wasted on unqualified prospects, produces better proposals, and creates a record that informs the entire project lifecycle.

---

## Discovery Call Philosophy

> "We don't sell websites. We diagnose digital problems and prescribe growth solutions."

The founder's role on a discovery call is:
1. **Listen 70%, speak 30%**
2. Understand the business, not just the website
3. Uncover the real problem (not just the stated problem)
4. Establish authority by asking questions nobody else asks
5. Position the proposal as a prescription, not a quote

## Pre-Call Workflow

### Step 1: Lead Qualifies (CRM stage: Qualified Lead)
Lead meets BANT criteria. Calendly link sent.

### Step 2: Booking Confirmation (Automated)
Calendly sends:
- Confirmation email with call details
- Calendar invite (Zoom/Google Meet link)
- Pre-call questionnaire (see below)

### Step 3: Pre-Call Questionnaire (Sent 48 hours before)
Email subject: *"A few quick questions before our call, [First Name]"*

Purpose: Arrive prepared. Save 10 minutes of baseline fact-gathering on the call itself.

## Pre-Call Questionnaire

```
ROMANCREATIVESTUDIO.CO — PRE-CALL QUESTIONNAIRE

Thank you for scheduling a call with us. 
Taking 5 minutes to answer these questions helps us make our 30 minutes together as valuable as possible.

1. What is your business name and website URL (if you have one)?

2. In one sentence, what does your business do?

3. Who is your ideal customer? (Be specific — e.g., "first-time homebuyers in Atlanta" not "homeowners")

4. What is the #1 problem you need your website to solve?

5. What does success look like 6 months after launching your new website?

6. What have you already tried, and why didn't it work?

7. Do you have a budget range in mind? (This helps us recommend the right solution.)
   [ ] Under $3,000
   [ ] $3,000 – $6,000
   [ ] $6,000 – $12,000
   [ ] $12,000+
   [ ] Not sure yet

8. Is there a deadline or launch event we should know about?

9. How did you find us?
```

## Call Structure (30 Minutes)

### Opening (2 min)
- Welcome and set the agenda
- "Today I want to understand your business deeply so I can tell you honestly whether we're the right fit — and if we are, what I'd recommend."
- Confirm: Do you have 30 full minutes?

### Business Discovery (10 min)
QUESTIONS:
1. Tell me about your business — what do you do, who do you serve, and what makes you different?
2. How long have you been operating? How many clients/customers per month?
3. How do most of your new clients find you today?
4. What percentage of your revenue would you say comes through your website vs. referrals vs. other channels?
5. If your website were suddenly performing at its best, what would change in your business?

### Problem Diagnosis (8 min)
QUESTIONS:
6. Walk me through your current website — what do you like and what frustrates you about it?
7. When someone visits your website, what do you want them to do first?
8. Do you know what your current conversion rate is? (Most don't — that's OK.)
9. Have you done any SEO, ads, or other marketing? What results have you seen?
10. Who are your top 2-3 competitors? How does their online presence compare to yours?

### Goals & Timeline (5 min)
QUESTIONS:
11. If we work together, what are the top 3 outcomes you need from this project?
12. Is there a hard deadline (event, season, funding announcement)?
13. Who else is involved in the decision to move forward?
14. What would make you hesitant to move forward, even if you loved the proposal?

### Positioning & Next Steps (5 min)
- "Based on what you've shared, here's what I'm thinking..." (high-level tier recommendation)
- "I'll put together a tailored proposal and have it to you within 48 hours."
- Confirm best email and preferred communication method
- Confirm they have decision-making authority or will share with decision-maker
- "Do you have any questions for me before we wrap up?"

## Post-Call Workflow

### Immediately After Call (< 15 min)
- Record raw notes using the Post-Call Notes Template (below)
- Update CRM: stage → Proposal, add notes, set follow-up date
- Send "Proposal incoming" email (see template below)

### Within 24 Hours
- Run discovery notes through AI Discovery Summary Generator (see AIAutomationFramework.md)
- Review and refine AI summary
- Draft proposal using Proposal Generator
- Peer-review proposal for accuracy and premium positioning

### Within 48 Hours
- Deliver proposal via e-signature platform with a personal cover note
- Log delivery in CRM
- Set Day 3 follow-up reminder

## No-Show Protocol

1. **During the meeting window (5 min grace):** Send a quick: "Hi [First Name] — I'm on the call, just wanted to make sure you have the link: [link]"
2. **15 min after scheduled start:** Send a brief "No-show" email: "Looks like something came up — no worries. Happy to reschedule when you're ready."
3. **Calendly re-booking link included in the no-show email**
4. **CRM:** Mark as No-Show. Set 48-hour follow-up. After 2 no-shows, move to Cold Nurture.

---

## Disqualification Criteria

Mark as Disqualified (with reason logged) if:
- Budget is confirmed below $3,000 and they refuse to discuss value
- They want a completed website in under 2 weeks
- They have worked with 3+ agencies and fired all of them for design reasons
- They cannot provide any content or assets and expect RCS to fabricate everything
- They are clearly price-shopping and want the cheapest option
- Red flags: demeaning tone, unrealistic guarantees demanded, refusal to sign a contract

## Technical Notes

- Discovery calls are conducted via Zoom or Google Meet (client preference)
- All calls should be recorded (with client consent) for AI summarization
- Recordings stored in the client folder, not shared unless requested
- Pre-call questionnaire built in Typeform or as a Google Form embedded in the confirmation email
