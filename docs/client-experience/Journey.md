# Client Journey

## Stage 1: Lead

**Goal:** Qualify the lead before investing significant time.

### Checklist
- [ ] Lead submits contact form or books a call
- [ ] Review their current website and social presence before responding
- [ ] Respond within 1 business day
- [ ] Send intake questionnaire if they haven't already filled one out
- [ ] Confirm discovery call time

**Success signal:** Qualified lead confirmed for discovery call.

---

## Stage 2: Discovery

**Goal:** Understand their business, goals, and situation deeply enough to propose the right solution.

### Checklist
- [ ] Prepare: review intake form, audit their current site, research competitors
- [ ] Conduct discovery call (see DiscoveryCall.md)
- [ ] Take notes on: goals, timeline, budget, stakeholders, existing assets
- [ ] Send follow-up email within 2 hours of call
- [ ] Decide: proceed to proposal, or decline gracefully?

**Success signal:** Both parties agree to move forward; proposal timeline set.

---

## Stage 3: Proposal

**Goal:** Present a solution that is tailored, clear, and compelling.

### Checklist
- [ ] Draft proposal within agreed timeframe (3–5 business days)
- [ ] Include: project overview, scope, deliverables, timeline, investment, payment terms
- [ ] Review before sending: does this solve their specific problem?
- [ ] Send via email with a brief personal note
- [ ] Follow up in 2 business days if no response
- [ ] Be available to answer questions promptly

**Success signal:** Proposal accepted.

---

## Stage 4: Contract

**Goal:** Formalize the engagement and protect both parties.

### Checklist
- [ ] Send contract document (scope, timeline, payment, IP transfer, revision policy)
- [ ] Ensure client reads and signs — never skip this step
- [ ] Confirm payment details
- [ ] Set kickoff date

**Success signal:** Signed contract received.

---

## Stage 5: Payment (Deposit)

**Goal:** Receive the 50% deposit before any work begins.

### Checklist
- [ ] Send invoice for 50% deposit
- [ ] Confirm receipt of payment
- [ ] Only begin work after deposit is received — no exceptions

**Success signal:** Deposit received, project officially begins.

---

## Stage 6: Kickoff

**Goal:** Align on the project and set the tone for the entire engagement.

### Checklist
- [ ] Schedule kickoff call (30–45 minutes)
- [ ] Review: project goals, timeline, milestones, communication preferences
- [ ] Collect: all existing brand assets (logo files, photos, copy)
- [ ] Establish: who the primary contact is, how to reach them, turnaround expectation for feedback
- [ ] Share: project timeline document
- [ ] Send kickoff summary email after the call

**Success signal:** Client has clear expectations; all assets received.

---

## Stage 7: Research + Strategy

**Goal:** Understand the client's market, competitors, and customers before designing.

### Checklist
- [ ] Competitor analysis (3–5 competitors): what do they do well, what's missing?
- [ ] Review client's existing content and messaging
- [ ] Identify target customer persona for the website
- [ ] Define sitemap / page structure
- [ ] Define primary CTA for each page
- [ ] Confirm SEO keyword targets for each page

**Success signal:** Strategy document approved by client (or at minimum, reviewed).

---

## Stage 8: Design

**Goal:** Create a design that solves the problem, reflects the brand, and is ready to build.

### Checklist
- [ ] Create wireframes or mockup for key pages
- [ ] Apply brand colors, typography, and style
- [ ] Review against conversion best practices
- [ ] Present design to client with rationale (not just "here it is")
- [ ] Collect feedback (max 2 revision rounds per page)
- [ ] Get design sign-off before moving to development

**Success signal:** Design approved in writing (email confirmation is sufficient).

---

## Stage 9: Development

**Goal:** Build the approved design into a functioning, fast, accessible website.

### Checklist
- [ ] Build from approved design — no scope additions without contract change
- [ ] Mobile-first: build and test at mobile viewport first
- [ ] Test in: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge (minimum)
- [ ] Test on real mobile devices (iOS and Android if possible)
- [ ] Validate HTML for semantic correctness
- [ ] Optimize all images (WebP, proper sizing, lazy loading)
- [ ] Set up analytics (GA4 + Search Console)
- [ ] Implement meta tags, OG tags, and structured data

**Success signal:** Staging site ready for review.

---

## Stage 10: QA

**Goal:** Catch every problem before the client sees the staging site.

### QA Checklist
- [ ] All links working (no broken links, no 404s)
- [ ] All forms tested (submission, confirmation, email delivery)
- [ ] Mobile: test at 375px, 768px, 1024px
- [ ] Speed: Lighthouse performance score 85+ on mobile
- [ ] Accessibility: Lighthouse accessibility score 90+
- [ ] SEO: all pages have title, meta description, H1
- [ ] Images: all have alt text; all optimized
- [ ] Contact information: correct email, phone, address
- [ ] Consistent with approved design

**Success signal:** QA passed; staging link sent to client.

---

## Stage 11: Launch

**Goal:** Deploy to production with zero downtime and celebrate with the client.

### Checklist
- [ ] Final payment invoice sent (and received before launch OR agreed upon billing date)
- [ ] DNS transfer / domain pointed to new host
- [ ] SSL certificate active (HTTPS)
- [ ] Analytics confirmed tracking post-launch
- [ ] Google Search Console: submit sitemap
- [ ] Post-launch check: test all critical pages on live domain
- [ ] Announce launch (client's social, with permission)
- [ ] Send launch congratulations message to client

**Success signal:** Site live on production domain, client notified.

---

## Stage 12: Training

**Goal:** Ensure the client can manage their own content without depending on us for every small update.

### Checklist
- [ ] Schedule training session (30–45 min for most sites)
- [ ] Cover: how to update text, swap images, add blog posts (if applicable)
- [ ] Record session or provide written guide
- [ ] Share hosting/login credentials securely
- [ ] Confirm client has everything they need

**Success signal:** Client confident in managing basic content.

---

## Stage 13: Maintenance

**Goal:** Keep the site performing at launch quality indefinitely.

### Checklist (Monthly, if on Care Plan)
- [ ] Site uptime confirmed
- [ ] Security scan completed
- [ ] Backups current
- [ ] Speed test run (Lighthouse or PageSpeed Insights)
- [ ] Any content updates requested by client completed
- [ ] Monthly report sent (for Growth/Partner Care plans)

**Success signal:** Client on active Care Plan; site healthy and current.

---

## Stage 14: Referral

**Goal:** Turn a satisfied client into an active referral source.

### Checklist
- [ ] 30-day check-in: how is the site performing? Any questions?
- [ ] Ask for testimonial or Google review (if results are positive)
- [ ] Mention referral program
- [ ] 90-day check-in: offer review of analytics / first results
- [ ] Annual check-in: is the site still meeting their goals? Scope any updates?

**Success signal:** Client refers at least one new lead, or leaves a public review.
