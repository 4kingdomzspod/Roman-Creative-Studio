# Client Experience Overview

## The Standard

Every Roman Creative Studio client should finish their engagement thinking:

> *"That was the best money I've spent on my business. I knew what to expect, the work exceeded what I hoped for, and I felt like a partner, not just a customer."*

This doesn't happen by accident. It happens through a documented, intentional client experience at every stage.

---

## The Client Journey

```
Lead → Discovery → Proposal → Contract → Payment
  ↓
Kickoff → Research → Design → Development → QA
  ↓
Launch → Training → Maintenance → Referral
```

## Core Principles

### Proactive Communication
Clients should never have to wonder what's happening. We communicate before they ask.

### Clear Expectations
Every stage starts with "here's what happens next." No surprises.

### Speed on Client Requests
Respond to client messages within 1 business day. For urgent issues, same day.

### Celebrate Wins
Launch day should feel like a celebration, not just a deliverable.

### Graceful Endings
Every project ends with a handoff, training, and a clear path to continued support.
